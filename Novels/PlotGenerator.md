# Plot Generator — Structured Novel Concept System

---

## 1. LOGLINE FORMULA

Every novel begins with a one-sentence logline. Fill this formula:

```
When [PROTAGONIST] with [FLAW] is forced into [CONFLICT],
they must [GOAL] before [STAKES], or [CONSEQUENCE].
Example:
"A bitter middle-class girl who trusts no one is forced to work alongside a privileged rich boy —
she must win the CSP exam before her family falls apart, or stay trapped in poverty forever."
(Zindagi Gulzar Hai)
```

### Logline Templates by Genre

**Spiritual Romance:**
```
When [HEROINE] questions [FAITH/SOCIETY], she meets [HERO/MENTOR] who challenges her —
she must choose between [WORLDLY DESIRE] and [SPIRITUAL TRUTH], or lose her soul entirely.
```

**Crime-Thriller:**
```
When [PROTAGONIST] is accused of [CRIME] they didn't commit, they must find [TRUTH]
before [ANTAGONIST] silences everyone, or spend life in prison for another's sin.
```

**Murder Mystery:**
```
When [VICTIM] dies, [INVESTIGATOR] uncovers a web of [DECEPTION] —
they must find the killer before [DANGER] claims the next victim, or [CONSEQUENCE].
```

**Historical Fiction:**
```
In [YEAR/PLACE], [PROTAGONIST] struggles to [SURVIVE/PRESERVE] as [HISTORICAL EVENT] threatens everything —
they must [CHOOSE] between [PERSONAL DESIRE] and [SURVIVAL], while history is being written.
```

**Class-Divide Romance:**
```
When [POOR HEROINE] clashes with [RICH HERO], their hatred masks [ATTRACTION] —
they must overcome [SOCIAL BARRIERS] before [EXTERNAL FORCE] tears them apart.
```

## 3. THREE-ACT STRUCTURE TEMPLATE

### Act I — Setup (25% of novel)

| Chapter Block | Purpose | Must Include |
|--------------|---------|--------------|
| Ch 1-2 | Introduce protagonist in their normal world | Their flaw causes visible problem |
| Ch 3-4 | Inciting incident — conflict enters | A choice they can't avoid |
| Ch 5-7 | React — protagonist resists change | First encounter with opposition |

**Act I ends when:** Protagonist commits to the journey (point of no return).

### Act II — Rising Action (50% of novel)

| Chapter Block | Purpose | Must Include |
|--------------|---------|--------------|
| Ch 8-12 | New world — challenges, allies, enemies | Subplot introduced |
| Ch 13-18 | Midpoint — stakes raised, everything changes | Major revelation or reversal |
| Ch 19-24 | "All is lost" — lowest point | Protagonist loses hope |

**Act II ends when:** Protagonist finds inner strength to continue.

### Act III — Resolution (25% of novel)

| Chapter Block | Purpose | Must Include |
|--------------|---------|--------------|
| Ch 25-28 | Final preparation — pieces come together | Allies, evidence, clarity |
| Ch 29-31 | Climax — final confrontation | Protagonist uses what they learned |
| Ch 32-33 | Resolution — new normal | Character growth shown, not stated |

## 6. GENRE TONE & CONTENT INDICATORS

| Genre | Tone | Content Notes |
|-------|------|---------------|
| Spiritual Romance | Reflective, hopeful | Prayer scenes, Quranic integration, spiritual dialogue |
| Crime-Thriller | Dark, urgent | Murder, legal process, grey morality, violence (implied/off-stage) |
| Murder Mystery | Suspenseful, cool | Death, investigation, red herrings, rational explanation |
| Historical Fiction | Weighted, atmospheric | Period detail, political context, slower pace |
| Class-Divide Romance | Emotional, satirical | Poverty details, privilege critique, family conflict |
| Feminist Historical | Restrained, sharp | Domestic space, women's interiority, political backdrop |
| Fantasy/Time-travel | Dreamy, imaginative | Supernatural elements, descriptive, longer format |
