## 7. CHARACTER CHECKLIST — BEFORE WRITING

- [ ] Can I describe their personality in 3 words?
- [ ] Do I know what they want in every scene?
- [ ] Do I know what they'd NEVER do?
- [ ] Would their mother describe them differently than their enemy does?
- [ ] Can I hear their voice in my head?
- [ ] Do they have a consistent speech pattern?
- [ ] Is their flaw causing them visible problems in life?
- [ ] Will the reader care about them by page 3?
- [ ] Are they active (making choices) or passive (things happening to them)?
- [ ] Could someone write a paragraph defending their worst action?
