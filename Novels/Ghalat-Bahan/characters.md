# غلط بہن — Character Bible

---

## HERO: ہارون (Haroon)

**Age:** 28
**Occupation:** Architect / freelance designer
**Education:** NCA Lahore, dropped out of Master's abroad
**Family:** Father (retired civil servant), Mother (housewife), one married sister in Karachi
**Returning from:** Kuala Lumpur — where he went for a Master's degree and a relationship that both failed

**Appearance:** Tall, tired eyes, wears simple kurtas and jeans. Doesn't notice what he wears. His hands are steady except when he's emotional.

**Flaw:** He feels too deeply and trusts too easily. After being betrayed, he built walls. Now he mistakes emotional numbness for strength.

**Backstory (hidden):** 
- Engaged to a girl in KL for 2 years. She left him for his best friend.
- His father had a minor stroke during that time — he couldn't come back because he was holding onto a relationship that was already dead.
- His mother still says " اللہ نے بچا لیا " (Allah saved you) about the broken engagement. He's not sure he believes her.
- He carries guilt: he wasn't there when his father was sick. He was busy trying to save something that wasn't worth saving.

**Deep need:** To be chosen. To be the first priority for someone, not the backup plan.

**Voice:** Quiet. Speaks only when he has something to say. Uses irony as defense. When emotional, his voice drops lower and he pauses between words.

**Arc:** Numbness → Feeling again → Trusting the wrong person → Truth → Forgiveness

## YOUNGER SISTER: ماہم (Maham)

**Age:** 22
**Occupation:** Final-year university student (Literature)
**Family:** Lives with mother and older sister (Saba). Father died 6 years ago.

**Appearance:** Small, delicate features. Wears light colors — pastel kurtas, always a dupatta. Hair in a loose braid. Eyes that look down when someone holds her gaze too long.

**First impression:** Soft. Polite. The kind of girl elders call "شریف" and "پاکیزہ". She speaks in a low voice, serves tea without being asked, laughs behind her hand.

**Flaw:** She has learned that survival means being what people expect. She has become so good at being "the good sister" that she doesn't know who she is underneath.

**Hidden pain:** She was in love with the boy who vanished. He loved her too. She was supposed to run away with him — and at the last moment, she couldn't. Her fear destroyed him. She has never forgiven herself.

**What she shows:** Innocence, vulnerability, need for protection
**What she hides:** Guilt, self-loathing, the truth about that night

**Voice:** Soft, hesitant. Uses "شاید" and "مجھے نہیں پتہ" often. When she lies, she touches her hair.

**Arc:** The mask of innocence → Crack appears → Truth emerges → Either breaks or becomes real

---

## OLDER SISTER: صبا (Saba)

**Age:** 26
**Occupation:** School teacher (Islamic Studies / Urdu)
**Family:** Has been the "man of the house" since father died. Never married. No one asks why.

**Appearance:** Sharp features. Wears dark, solid colors. No makeup. Hair tied tight. Eyes that miss nothing. She doesn't smile at strangers.

**First impression:** Cold. Proud. Judgmental. She looks at you like she already knows your worst secret.

**Flaw:** She carries too much. She took a blame that wasn't hers. She pushed everyone away so no one would ask questions. She has been alone for so long that she doesn't know how to let anyone in.

**Hidden truth:** That night — when Maham was supposed to run away — Saba found out. She stopped it. Not out of cruelty, but out of fear. She threatened the boy. She said things she can't take back. He left. And then he died — a road accident, or suicide, depending on who tells the story. Saba has never said which version is true.

**What she shows:** Coldness, control, judgment
**What she hides:** Guilt, love for her sister, the weight of a decision that destroyed three lives

**Voice:** Sharp, precise. Cuts through silence. Uses sarcasm to keep people at a distance. When vulnerable, she quotes Quran or poetry instead of speaking directly.

**Arc:** The villain who isn't one → Walls crack → Confession → Either redemption or sacrifice

## THE BOY WHO VANISHED: سلیم (Saleem)

**Age at death:** 24
**Occupation:** Photographer / journalism student
**Personality:** Intense, passionate, angry at injustice. Loved Maham recklessly.
**The night he vanished:** He was supposed to meet Maham at the bus stop. Midnight. They were going to Lahore together. Saba found out. She came instead of Maham. She told him he was destroying her sister's life. He argued. Saba said cruel things — "تم اس کے لائق نہیں ہو،" "تم اس کی زندگی برباد کرو گے." He left. He drove his motorcycle into the night. Next morning, his bike was found wrapped around a tree on the motorway. His body was found in a ditch three days later. Accident — or deliberate? No one knows but Saba.

## THEMATIC CORE

**Surah reference (Umera Ahmed style):** Surah Al-Hujurat (The Rooms) — "اے ایمان والو! بہت سے گمانوں سے بچو، کیونکہ بعض گمان گناہ ہوتے ہیں" (O believers, avoid many assumptions, for some assumptions are sins — 49:12)

**Core theme:** We judge by what we see. But the person who seems cold may be burning with sacrifice. The person who seems pure may be drowning in guilt. And sometimes, the heart chooses wrong — not because it's foolish, but because it only sees half the story.

**Emotional arc (Umera Ahmed style):** Numbness → Feeling → Attachment → Doubt → Crisis → Truth → Either peace or loss

**Logline:**
When a wounded man returns to his city seeking peace, he falls for the innocent younger sister — only to discover the cold, hostile older sister holds the key to a past that none of them can escape.
