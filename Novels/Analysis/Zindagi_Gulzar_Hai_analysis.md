# Novel Analysis: Zindagi Gulzar Hai (زندگی گلزار ہے) by Umera Ahmed

**Author:** Umera Ahmed  
**Pages:** ~300  
**Genre:** Epistolary Social Romance / Class-Divide Enemies-to-Lovers  
**Pivotal Technique:** Pure Epistolary Format (Dual Diary Perspectives without external narrator).

---

## 1. Plot Overview & Structural Breakdown
* **Protagonists:** Kashaf Murtaza (poor, bitter, fiercely independent student raised by single mother) vs Zaroon Junaid (wealthy, privileged, confident student at GCU).
* **Format:** Unfiltered alternating diary entries revealing their private thoughts, prejudices, insecurities, and gradual emotional transformation.
* **Themes:** Socio-economic disparity, female resilience against patriarchy, gratitude (*Shukr*), and overcoming pride (*Kibriya*).
