# Novel Analysis: Paras (پارس) by Nimra Ahmed

**Author:** Nimra Ahmed  
**Pages:** ~96  
**Genre:** Short Murder Mystery / Wealth vs Worth / Non-Linear Suspense  
**Pivotal Technique:** Flashback suspense structure in short-form fiction.

---

## 1. Plot Overview & Structural Breakdown
* **Plot:** A compact murder mystery centered around wealth, greed, and moral worth (*Paras* - the philosopher's stone that turns metal into gold).
* **Technique:** Uses strategic non-linear flashbacks to answer one clue while raising another.
