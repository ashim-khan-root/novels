# Novel Analysis: Hasil (حاصل) by Umera Ahmed

**Author:** Umera Ahmed  
**Pages:** ~100  
**Genre:** Condensed Spiritual Romance / Interfaith Conflict / Faith Crisis  
**Pivotal Technique:** Fast-paced condensed spiritual arc (~100 pages showing how the Umera Ahmed faith-crisis arc works in short-form).

---

## 1. Plot Overview & Structural Breakdown
* **Characters:** Hadeed (wealthy, spiritually empty introvert who loses his inheritance) and Saania (middle-class girl of strong faith, alienated after wanting to marry David, a Christian man).
* **Conflict:** Interfaith boundaries, church/cathedral scenes as emotional battlegrounds, and fast-paced financial downfall driving spiritual redemption.
