# Novel Analysis: Aangan (آنگن) by Khadija Mastur

**Author:** Khadija Mastur  
**Pages:** ~250  
**Genre:** Feminist Historical Fiction / Political Microcosm / Domestic Realism  
**Pivotal Technique:** Domestic Space Narrative (All historical/political events filtered through household dialogue).

---

## 1. Plot Overview & Structural Breakdown
* **Setting:** A traditional North Indian Muslim household during the 1930s-1940s Partition movement.
* **POV:** Aliya (young woman observing family disintegration, political factionalism, and female oppression).
* **Technique:** Political movements (Muslim League, Congress, Khilafat) are never shown on battlefields; they break into the home through family arguments, male imprisonment, and female sacrifice.
* **Prose Style:** Spare, restrained, rich in vernacular dialogue, zero excess description.
