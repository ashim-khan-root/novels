# Novel Analysis: Udas Naslain (اداس نسلیں) by Abdullah Hussain

**Author:** Abdullah Hussain  
**Pages:** ~500+  
**Genre:** Epic Historical Realism / Generation Saga / War & Partition Epic  
**Pivotal Technique:** Episodic Vignettes spanning 1913–1947 (WWI, Jallianwala Bagh, Peasant Movement, Partition).

---

## 1. Plot Overview & Structural Breakdown
* **Protagonist:** Naim (a rural youth who fights in World War I in Europe, loses his arm, experiences alienation, attempts political activism, and suffers Partition trauma).
* **Themes:** Historical dislocation, war trauma, class disparity between rural peasants and anglicized elites (Azra), and loss of human innocence.
* **Prose Style:** Sweeping, unsentimental, imagist, capturing historical inevitability without melodrama.
