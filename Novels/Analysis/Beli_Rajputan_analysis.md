# Novel Analysis: Beli Rajputan Ki Malika (بیلی راجپوتانہ کی ملکہ) by Nimra Ahmed

**Author:** Nimra Ahmed  
**Pages:** ~379  
**Genre:** Historical Mystery-Romance / Rajput Period Drama / Outlaw Hero  
**Pivotal Technique:** Historical period research blended with an outlaw hero (*Dacoit with a code*) archetype and twist ending.

---

## 1. Plot Overview & Structural Breakdown
* **Setting:** Historic Rajputana region; feudal estates, hidden secrets, and romantic rivalries.
* **Archetype:** Badar (outlaw hero/dacoit with a moral code protecting the vulnerable).
* **Technique:** Love triangle with suspenseful historical atmosphere and a shocking climax twist.
