# Author Profile: Sadia Rajpoot

**Novels analyzed:** Ishq-Aatish
**Genre specialty:** Romantic / Social / Philosophical

## Writing Style
- Philosophical, reflective prose
- Strong authorial voice with direct reader address (preface)
- Sentences blending narrative with moral commentary
- Emotional and dramatic storytelling

## Emotional Palette
- Love, sacrifice, pain, regret, forgiveness
- Focus on psychological depth and mental health
- Father-daughter relationships as emotional core

## Key Themes
- Philosophy of love (haqiqi vs majazi — divine vs worldly)
- Sacrifice and its consequences
- Regret and repentance
- Social attitudes toward abandoned children
- Mental health (schizophrenia) awareness
- Past trauma and healing

## Narrative Techniques
- Comparative time structure (past vs present)
- Philosophical reflections woven into narrative
- Strong moral framework
- Emotional shock in every chapter

## About Ishq-Aatish (Love Fire)
- **Published:** 2016 (first novel)
- **Pages:** 397
- **Story:** Maleeha's sacrifice for her father vs love for Wijdan; Noorul Huda and Wijdan's journey as parents
- **Impact:** Called "future Peer-e-Kamil" by some readers for its depth
- **Unique:** Explores schizophrenia in Urdu fiction
- **Reader praise:** Emotional depth, philosophical richness, memorable characters
