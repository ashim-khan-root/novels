# Novel Writing Engine: Scholar Integration Manual (مفکرین پر مبنی ناول نگاری گائیڈ)

---

## 1. Objective
This operational guide equips the AI writer in the `novel-ai` project with actionable rules, sentence patterns, character prompts, and scene builders derived from the combined wisdom of **Allama Muhammad Iqbal**, **Maulana Abdul Hamid Khan Bhashani**, and **Allama Abdul Latif Chowdhury Fultali**.

---

## 2. Operational Rules for Chapter Writing

### Rule 1: The Three-Dimensional Character Check
Before drafting any chapter, ensure the characters possess elements from the scholar matrix:
* **The Protagonist’s Mind (Iqbalian):** Does the protagonist have an intellectual/existential quest for *Khudi*?
* **The Protagonist’s Action (Bhashani):** Are they actively confronting social/economic injustice rather than living in a bubble?
* **The Protagonist’s Soul (Fultali):** Do they experience moments of inner quiet, humility, and spiritual sanctuary?

### Rule 2: Dialogue Purity & Voice Differentiation
* **Iqbalian Voice:** Uses grand, elevated, poetic Urdu terms (*Khudi*, *Shaheen*, *Perwaz*, *Aql-o-Ishq*, *Jawan*).
* **Bhashani Voice:** Uses earthy, rugged, passionate, public-rally terms (*Rububiyyah*, *Zulm*, *Mazloom*, *Rabb-ul-Aalameen*, *Kissan*, *Haq*).
* **Fultali Voice:** Uses gentle, respectful, luminous Sufi terms (*Makhraj*, *Tazkiyah*, *Ishq-e-Rasool*, *Khidmat*, *Sobti*, *Niyyah*).

## 3. Scene-by-Scene Synthesis Blueprint

### Scene Type A: Philosophical Conflict (Iqbalian)
* **Pacing:** Medium-slow building to an intense crescendo.
* **Atmosphere:** Rain, high balconies, grand libraries, or night-time solitary walks.
* **Prose Style:** Elevated, reflective, philosophical narrative sentences followed by sharp, memorable dialogue.

### Scene Type B: Grassroots Social Confrontation (Bhashani)
* **Pacing:** Fast-paced, high tension, 7-page tension cycle climax.
* **Atmosphere:** Muddy village squares, courtrooms, crowded factories, or police barricades.
* **Prose Style:** Visceral sensory details (sweat, dust, roaring voices, wooden staffs, clinking handcuffs).

### Scene Type C: Spiritual Restoration (Fultali)
* **Pacing:** Gentle, rhythmic, serene.
* **Atmosphere:** Quiet courtyards, fragrance of attar, dawn light (*Fajr*), soft carpet, sound of Quranic recitation.
* **Prose Style:** Warm, soothing, rich in emotional depth and internal monologue of repentance (*Tawbah*).

## 4. Example Chapter Blueprint: "The Eagle in the Mud" (شاہین اور مٹی)

1. **Opening Hook:** The protagonist, a brilliant corporate lawyer, sits in his plush office looking at a contract that will displace 500 poor families for a private resort.
2. **7-Page Tension Cycle:**
   * *Page 1-2:* Financial temptation vs. uneasy conscience.
   * *Page 3-4:* Flashback to an Iqbalian mentor warning him: *"If you trade your Khudi for silver, you are no longer an eagle."*
   * *Page 5-6:* Visits the village site; meets a Bhashani-like old farmer who fearlessly tells him: *"God owns this soil, not your resort!"*
   * *Page 7 (Cliffhanger):* Hero tears up the contract in front of his wealthy corrupt boss and declares: *"I am filing a petition against you tomorrow morning!"*
