## 1. Primary Bibliographical Corpus

### A. Major Philosophical Works
1. **Asrar-e-Khudi (اسرارِ خودی - 1915):** The Secrets of the Self. Primary Persian philosophical poem laying down the metaphysics of *Khudi*, self-preservation, and self-strengthening.
2. **Rumuz-e-Bekhudi (رموزِ بے خودی - 1918):** The Mysteries of Selflessness. The integration of individual *Khudi* into the collective moral Ummah.
3. **Payam-e-Mashriq (پیامِ مشرق - 1923):** Message from the East. Philosophical response to Goethe’s *West-östlicher Divan*.
4. **Bal-e-Jibril (بالِ جبریل - 1935):** Gabriel's Wing. Urdu masterpiece featuring poems like *Masjid-e-Qartaba*, *Saaqi Nama*, and *Zauq-o-Shauq*.
5. **Zarb-e-Kaleem (ضربِ کلیم - 1936):** The Blow of Moses. Cultural and political critique against modern Western secular materialism and mental slavery.
6. **Javid Nama (جاوید نامہ - 1932):** Epic spiritual journey through the spheres, guided by Rumi, confronting historical and modern souls.
7. **The Reconstruction of Religious Thought in Islam (1930):** Seven lectures on Islamic metaphysics, time, free will, and dynamic *Ijtihad*.
