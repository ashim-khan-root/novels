## 1. Primary Bibliographical & Institutional Corpus

### A. Major Written Works & Scholarly Texts
1. **Al-Tanwir Ala Al-Tafsir (التنوير على التفسير):** Quranic commentary written during his imprisonment (1971–1973). Focuses on divine mercy, social solidarity, and spiritual steadfastness under trial.
2. **Al-Qaolus Sadid fi Al-Tajweed (القول سديد في التجويد):** Classic textbook on the rules of Quranic recitation, vocal articulation (*Makharij*), and Tajweed precision.
3. **Muntakhab us-Siyar (منتخب السير):** Biography of the Holy Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, emphasizing Sunnah adherence, gentle character (*Akhlaq*), and mercy to creation.

### B. Global Institutions & Foundations
* **Darul Qiraat Majidiah Fultali Trust (Est. 1950):** Global network for standardizing Tajweed education and Quranic memorization.
* **Anjumane Al Islah:** Socio-religious organization for humanitarian relief, youth welfare, and community education.
* **Core Sufi Axiom:** *"Sufism is nothing but service to creation"* (تصوف مخلوق کی بے غرض خدمت کا نام ہے).
