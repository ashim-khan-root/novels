# Beta Reader Checklist — Structured Evaluation Form

---

## Instructions for Beta Reader

Read the novel at your natural pace. After finishing, answer the following honestly.
There are no wrong answers. Your honest reaction is more valuable than praise.

---

## SECTION A: FIRST IMPRESSION

Rate 1-5 (1=Not at all, 5=Very much)

```
A1. Did the first page make you want to continue?                    1 2 3 4 5
A2. Did the first chapter make you want to read Chapter 2?           1 2 3 4 5
A3. How long did it take you to feel invested? (pages: ___)
A4. At what point did you almost stop reading? (page: ___, or N/A)
```

## For the Author: How to Use Beta Feedback

1. Read all feedback before changing ANYTHING. Let it sit.
2. Look for PATTERNS — if 2+ readers say the same thing, it's a real issue.
3. One reader's opinion is just that. Two is a signal. Three is a pattern.
4. You don't have to accept every suggestion. But consider every one.
5. Fix plot/character issues FIRST. Prose last.
6. Thank your beta readers. They gave you their time and honesty.
