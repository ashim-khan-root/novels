is ne apane baal khol diay thay — ab woh bandhe nahin rehte thay. woh hansti thi — aur jab woh hansti thi toh is ka pora chehra badal jata tha.

yawasaf ki warakshap ab bari ho gai thi — wahi purani amarat, magar ab neeche ki manazl bhi is ki thi. is ne mhajar bachon ke liay ek chhota sa mausiqi skol bana diya tha — mafat. unhein ni b-jaana skhata — aur in bachon ke chaharon par woh sukoon aata jo woh khod kabhi qonia mein dhonda kurta tha.

zara wahan aksar aati — dophar ko, jab skol khatam hota. woh aa kar kaunay mein bith jati aur bachon ko b-jate dekhti. kabhi woh ise utha kar le jata — basaforas ke kanare chai pine — aur woh khamosh baithe rehte.

koi jaldi nahin thi. aath saal in ke dramayan thay — magar woh ab unhein abor kar chake thay. ab woh sirf ek doosray ke saath thay — b-ghair kisi sharat ke.

---
