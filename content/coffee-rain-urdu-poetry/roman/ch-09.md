ab woh har roz milte thay — aur har roz kuch naya hota tha.

aaj — aasaíam ne kuch aur kiya.

woh kife mein aaya toh is ke haath mein ek chhota sa pikt tha — bhore kaghaz mein lapta, soti dori se bandha hawa.

mhar ne ise dekha toh is ki aankhon mein tajasas tha:

"yeh kiya hai?"

aasaíam ne pikt is ki traf brhaya — aur is ke haath kanap rahe thay:

"kholo —"

mhar ne dori kholi aur kaghaz hataya — andar ek kitab thi. purana aidishan, jalad phati hui — *dayawanِ ghalab*.

"yeh —"

"mein janta hoon aap fiz ko psnd karti ho — maapr mein ne socha ke shayad ghalab bhi aap ko psnd aaye. yeh miri psndida kitab hai — mein ne ise brason pehle kharida tha, aur yeh miri sab se qimati chizon mein se ek hai."

mhar ne kitab ko haath mein liya — jaise woh koi nazk chiz ho — aur ise khola. pehle safahe par ek purani likhai thi — shayad kisi aur ki, shayad brason pehle ki. aur is ke neeche, aasaíam ne likha tha:

*"mhar ke liay — jis ne mujhe skhaya ke ghalab aur fiz ek hi sake ke do rakh hain. aur shayad — mohabbat bhi."*

mhar ne yeh parha — aur is ki aankhein nam ho gaئin.

"aap ne — yeh sab se khubsurat thafa hai jo kisi ne mujhe diya hai."

---

woh shaam ko kife se nikalay toh bahar hawa thandi thi.

woh saath saath chal rahe thay — gali ki purani roshnion mein, jahan har limp ki apani khani thi. in ke dramayan fasla tha — shayad ek fat, shayad is se bhi kam — magar woh fasla ab khala nahin tha, woh ek kshash thi.

achank mhar ka paaon patthar se takraya — woh zara si dagamagaئi — aur aasaíam ne is ka baazu pakar liya.

is ka haath is ke baazu par tha — gram, naram, magar pakar mazabot thi.

"theek hain?"

mhar ne is ki traf dekha — in ke chehray bahut qareeb thay. atana qareeb ke woh is ki saans mhasos kar sakti thi:

"hoon — shukriya."

magar woh hata nahin — aur is ne bhi apana haath nahin hataya. woh ek lamha, do lamhe — asi tarah khare rahe — is ka haath is ke baazu par, is ki aankhein is ki aankhon mein.

aur phir — aasaíam ne apana haath hata liya.

is liay nahin ke woh nahin chahta tha — balke is liay ke woh bahut kuch chahta tha, aur woh drata tha ke agar is ne ek lamha aur haath na hataya toh woh sab kuch kha de ga jisay khane ka waqt abhi nahin aaya tha.

---

woh chalte rahe — magar khamoshi ab pehle jisi nahin thi.

woh khamoshi bojahal nahin thi — woh alfaz se bhari hui thi jo kahe nahin gaye thay.

mhar ne rak kar kaha:

"woh kitab — mein ise snabhal kar rakhon gi."

aasaíam ne ise dekha: "ise parhna — aap ko ghalab psnd aaye ga."

"mein jaanti hoon — kyunke woh aap ki psndida kitab hai. aur jo chizin aap ko psnd hain — woh shayad mujhe bhi psnd aaein gi."

aasaíam ne is ki traf dekha — aur ise laga jaise is ne woh kha diya jo woh kehna chahta tha magar nahin kha ska tha.
