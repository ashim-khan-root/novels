asi raat — jab Aasim apane ghar mein ba-ba krim se baat kar raha tha — mhar ek aur sheher mein thi.

woh ek chhotay se hospital ke kamray mein baithi thi — is ke samne ek bistar tha, aur is bistar par is ki maan leti thi. manitr ki bip, adawayat ki bo, aur kahein daur se aanay wali koi aawaz — yeh sab ise haqiqat yaad dala raha tha.

magar is ki god mein — *klayatِ fiz* thi.

---

mhar ki aankhein band thin — magar woh so nahin rahi thi. woh is kitab ko apane sine se lagaئe baithi thi, jaise yeh is ki zindagi ka aakhari shara ho.

ise yaad tha — woh raat jab ise phone aaya. is ki ami ko dil ka dora pra tha — aur woh sheher se bahar thin. mhar ne is raat kife se sidha hospital jaana tha — magar is se pehle, woh kife gai thi. kyun?

ise khod nahin pata tha. shayad is liay ke woh jaanti thi ke agar woh kife na gai toh woh *klayatِ fiz* ke b-ghair atane din nahin gazar sake gi. shayad is liay ke woh kitab — is ke liay Aasim se malane ka wahad rasta thi.

is ne kitab uthai thi aur sidhi hospital chali gai thi — aur is ke paas yeh batane ka waqt nahin tha ke woh kahan jaa rahi hai.

---

teen hafate ho chake thay — aur ami ab bhi hospital mein thin.

mhar ne apani nokri se chahati le li thi — woh din mein hospital mein rehti, raat ko ek chhotay se kamray mein soti jo is ne hospital ke qareeb kraئe par liya tha. is ke paas kisi ko batane ka waqt nahin tha — aur shayad woh drati thi ke agar is ne Aasim ko bataya toh woh prishan ho jaئe ga.

magar sach yeh tha — woh khod prishan thi. aur ise sab se zyada dar is baat ka tha ke woh Aasim ko kho de gi.

---

har raat, jab ami so jati thin — mhar kitab kholati thi.

woh is ke alfaz parhti — Aasim ke lakhe huye woh sher, woh sawal, woh khat. woh unhein bar bar parhti, jaise woh ise yaad kar rahi ho ke bahar ek duniya hai jahan koi is ka antazar kar raha hai.

pehle hafate — is ne socha ke shayad woh ek do din mein waps chali jaئe gi. is ne apane phone mein message taئap kiya — magar delete kar diya. is ne kaha — kal lakhon gi.

doosray hafate — ami ki halat bagr gai. woh raaton ko jaagti rehti, doctoron ke paas bhagati, aur is ke paas kuch sochane ka waqt nahin tha. magar is ne kitab parhna nahin chhora — har raat, wahi chand minute, wahi alfaz, wahi yaqeen.

teesray hafate — is ne Aasim ka khat parha — woh khat jis mein is ne likha tha: *"kiya aap theek hain?"* aur is ki aankhon se aansoo behenay lage.

woh roti rahi — is liay nahin ke woh kamzor thi, balke is liay ke ise aakharakar mhasos hawa ke woh ktani tanha thi, aur woh is tanhai mein — alfaz ke swa — kuch nahin thi.

---

aaj — chothe hafate — ami ki halat behtar thi.

doctor ne kaha tha ke woh jalad ghar jaa sakti hain — aur mhar ne pehli bar ek gehri saans li. is ne khirki se bahar dekha — bahar baarish ho rahi thi.

is ne kitab kholi — aur is ne Aasim ka aakhari khat parha. jab woh yahan aai thi toh is ke paas sirf atana waqt tha ke woh kitab utha le — woh is ka jawab nahin de saki thi.

magar ab — woh jawab dena chahti thi.

is ne apane big se qalam n-kala — wahi qalam jis ne pehla jawab likha tha — aur is ne kitab ke khali hashaye par likhna sharoa kiya:

*"aasim —*

*min jaanti hoon ke aap bahut prishan hoon ge — aur mein har din sochati rahi ke aap ko bataؤan. magar mire paas alfaz nahin thay — aur waqt nahin tha, aur shayad mein drati thi ke agar mein ne likha toh ro pron gi.*

*miri ami bimar ho gai thin — aur mein in ke saath hoon. ab woh behtar hain — aur mein jalad waps aa rahi hoon.*

*magr jaan lijaye — mein ne aap ke khutoot ko har raat parha. woh mire liay roshni ki manand thay — is andhire mein jahan mein thi.*

*min kabhi nahin bhagi — mein sirf waps aanay ka rasta dhond rahi thi.*

*aap kay*
*mhar"*
