jamaa ka din aaya — aur Aasim pehle se kife mein tha.

is ne jaan bojah kar jaldi ki thi — woh is se pehle phanachana chahta tha ke woh aaye. woh jaanna chahta tha ke kiya woh waqai aaye gi — aur agar aaye gi toh woh kiya kare ga.

woh apani jagah par baitha kife ka darwaza dekh raha tha — har bar jab ghanti bajati, is ka dil ek bar zor se dharakta. magar woh nahin aai. ek ghanta guzar gaya, do ghante — aur woh nahin aai.

shayad woh badh ko aai thi aur ab nahin aaye gi. shayad woh bhi drati thi — jaise woh drata tha.

woh almari ke paas gaya — *klayatِ fiz* wohein thi. is ne ise khola toh is ka likha hawa sher tha — aur is ke neeche, ek aur jawab:

*"mein ne aap ko dekha — magar mein nahin jaanti ke aap thay ya nahin. shayad mein ne sirf woh chiz dekhi jisay mein dekhna chahti thi."*

Aasim ne yeh parha aur maskraya — woh bhi asi alajahan mein thi jaise woh tha.

is ne qalam n-kala aur likha:

*"mein wohein tha jahan aap ne mujhe dekha tha — aur mein aap ko pehchan nahin ska. magar ab jab mein aap ko janta hoon toh mujhe lagta hai ke mein aap ko hamesha se janta tha."*

woh aai.

is ne is ki traf dekha — aur is ki aankhon mein woh sawal tha jo alfaz mein nahin poocha jaa sakta.

"maaf kijaye —"

is ne kaha — magar woh jumla makamal nahin kar paa raha tha.

is ne ise dekha — aur is ne bahut narami se kaha:

"ji?"

Aasim ki saans rak gai — is ki aawaz wahi thi. ise yaqeen tha — yeh wahi thi.

"ji — bahut psnd hai. khas tore par woh sher jo kisi kitab mein likha ho."

is ka dil zor se dharaka — woh jaan gai thi. woh jaan gaya tha ke woh jaan gai hai. aur donon khamosh khare thay — kitabon ki almari ke samne, jaise yeh woh jagah thi jahan unhein milna tha.

"mein mhar hoon —"

"aap wahi hain na — jo —"

"haan — mein wahi hoon jo jamaa ko aata hoon, kitab mein sher likhta hoon, aur aap ke jawab ka antazar kurta hoon."

woh khamosh ho gai — magar is ki aankhon mein nami thi. woh khushi ke aansoo thay, ya shakar ke — ke yeh slasla jo kitab ke safahat mein sharoa hawa tha, ab haqiqat ban raha tha.
