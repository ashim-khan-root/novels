jamaa ka din tha — aur baarish ho rahi thi.

Aasim aur mhar kife ke darwazay par khare thay — in ke haath aaps mein mile huye thay, aur in ke samne wahi purani gali thi jahan se sab kuch sharoa hawa tha.

mhar ne peeche mar kar kife ko dekha — wahi purani pital ki ghanti, wahi lakri ki alamarayan, wahi gram roshni:

"yeh jagah — hamesha hamari rahe gi."

Aasim ne bhi peeche mar kar dekha: "yeh sirf ek kife nahin — yeh woh jagah hai jahan hum mile, jahan hum ne mohabbat sikhi, aur jahan hum ne apani khani likhi."

woh donon khamosh khare rahe — phir woh andar gaye.

---

aakhari bar — unhon ne *klayatِ fiz* ko almari se n-kala.

woh purani kitab, jis ne unhein malaya tha, jis mein in ke pehle alfaz thay, in ki pehli hansi, in ka pehla dar, in ki pehli mohabbat — woh kitab ab in ki khani ka hasa thi.

Aasim ne kitab kholi — aur is ne ek safed kaghaz n-kala. is ne qalam se kuch likha — aur phir mhar ki traf brhaya:

"yeh — aakhari bar."

mhar ne kaghaz liya — aur is ne is ke neeche likha. phir donon ne mal kar ise kitab ke asi safahe par rakha jahan se sab kuch sharoa hawa tha — pehle sher ke balakl neeche.

Aasim ne kitab band ki — aur ise waps almari mein rakh diya.

asi jagah — jahan woh hamesha rahi thi.

---

woh kife se nikalay — aur bahar baarish ab bhi ho rahi thi.

magar is bar — in ke paas chahatri nahin thi. woh baarish mein khare rahe — bhigate huye, hanaste huye, ek doosray ko dekhte huye.

Aasim ne mhar ka haath thama: "chalo —"

mhar ne ise dekha: "kahan?"

Aasim maskraya: "kahein bhi — bas saath saath."

woh chal pre — baarish mein, purani galion mein, haathon mein haath daalay — aur in ke peeche, kife ki roshni dhandala rahi thi.

kuch khanayan — jahan kitabein khatam hoti hain, wahan sharoa hoti hain.
