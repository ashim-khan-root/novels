ek saal guzar gaya tha — aur aaj phir wahi jamaa tha.

baarish ho rahi thi — balakl asi tarah jaise is din hui thi jab Aasim ne pehli bar kife mein qadam rakha tha. bahar sheher bhig raha tha, aur kife ki khirki se wahi manazr dakhaئi de raha tha — purani glayan, limp ki roshni, aur bondon se dhaki sarak.

Aasim aur mhar asi kaunay mein baithe thay — jahan woh pehli bar mile thay, jahan in ki khani sharoa hui thi.

Aasim ne kaha: "aaj ek saal ho gaya —"

mhar ne kaafi ka cup athaya: "mujhe pata hai — mein ne socha tha ke aap bhol gaye hoon ge."

"bholana — yeh din kaise bhol sakta hoon? yeh woh din tha jab mein ne pehla sher likha tha — aur pehli bar kisi ne mujhe samjha tha."

---

woh khamosh baithe kife ko dekh rahe thay — wahi purani alamarayan, wahi gram roshni, wahi kitabein. sab kuch wisa hi tha — magar woh badal chake thay.

Aasim ne apani jib se kuch n-kala — ek chhoti si prachi, balakl wisi jisi is ne ek saal pehle kitab mein chhori thi.

mhar ne ise dekha toh woh hans pri: "phir se prachi?"

Aasim maskraya: "baz aadatin nahin badalatin —"

is ne prachi is ki traf brha di.

mhar ne ise khola — aur is par likha tha:

*"mein ab aap ko likhna nahin chahta.*

*min aap se har roz baat karna chahta hoon."*

---

mhar ne prachi ko parha — aur is ki aankhon mein nami thi:

"yeh —"

Aasim ne is ka haath thama — aur is ki aankhon mein woh yaqeen tha jo is ne kabhi kisi ke liay mhasos nahin kiya tha:

"mein janta hoon — hamari khani alfaz se sharoa hui thi. magar ab — mein chahta hoon ke woh alfaz haqiqat ban jaein."

mhar ne ise dekha: "aap kiya kha rahe hain?"

Aasim ne gehri saans li — aur is ne woh kaha jo is ke dil mein brason se tha:

"mein kha raha hoon — ke mein aap se mohabbat kurta hoon. aur mein chahta hoon ke yeh mohabbat sirf kitab ke safahat tak mhadod na rahe — yeh hamari zindagi ban jaئe."

mhar ke aansoo behenay lage — magar woh maskra rahi thi:

"mein bhi — mein bhi yahi chahti hoon."

---

ba-ba krim kaafi le kar aaya — aur is ne meez par do cup rakhe:

"yeh loo — aap donon ke liay. khas — aaj ke din ke liay."

Aasim ne cup liya: "aap ko pata hai, ba-ba ji?"

ba-ba krim maskraئe — in ki aankhon mein woh chamak thi jo chalees saalon mein kabhi kam nahin hui thi:

"mujhe sab pata hai — mein ne sab dekha hai. aur mein sirf yeh keon ga — tum donon ek doosray ke liay banaئe gaye ho."

woh chala gaya — aur woh donon khamosh baithe rahe, ek doosray ko dekhte huye, yeh jante huye ke yeh sirf sharoaat thi.

Aasim ne kaha: "kal —"

mhar ne ise dekha: "kal?"

"kal bhi jamaa hai — aur mein yahan hoon ga. aur is ke baad har jamaa — aur har doosra din."

mhar ne is ka haath thama — aur is ne kaha:

"aur mein — hamesha yahan hoon gi."
