---
title: "Chapter 19 — dar"
weight: 19
type: roman
---


agli subah Aasim utha toh is ka pehla khayal mhar tha — aur is ke saath hi ek dar bhi aaya.

is ne kal raat kaha tha — "mein aap se mohabbat kurta hoon." aur woh sach tha. magar sach khane ke baad — ek khamoshi aati hai, ek lamha jab aap sochate hain: ab kiya?

kiya woh ise aaj phone kare? kiya woh kife aaye gi? kiya kal raat ke alfaz aaj subah bhi wahi rahein ge?

is ne phone athaya — magar rak gaya. ise nahin pata tha ke kiya kehna hai.

---

mhar bhi asi alajahan mein thi.

woh apane kamray mein baithi kaafi pi rahi thi — magar kaafi ka zaiqa karwa tha. is ne kal raat kaha tha — aur woh apane alfaz par yaqeen rakhti thi. magar is ke saath hi ek dar bhi tha — ke kahein is ne sab kuch jaldi toh nahin kar diya.

is ne apani diary kholi — aur is ne likha:

*"mein ne kha diya — aur ab mein dar rahi hoon. is liay nahin ke mein jhoot bol rahi hoon, balke is liay ke sach bolane ke baad — wapsi nahin hoti."*

---

dophar ko is ne Aasim ko message kiya:

*"kiya aap theek ho?"*

jawab aaya — das minute baad:

*"hoon — bas soch raha hoon."*

*"kiya soch rahe ho?"*

*"wahi — jo aap soch rahi ho."*

mhar maskraئi — magar is muskurahat mein be chini bhi thi:

*"kiya hum kife aaein aaj?"*

*"theek hai — shaam ko."*

---

shaam ko woh kife aaye — magar pehle das minute tak kuch nahin bolay.

woh ek doosray ko dekh rahe thay — jaise pehli bar, jaise ajanabi hoon. magar woh ajanabi nahin thay — woh wahi do thay jo kal raat baarish mein khare thay, magar ab din ki roshni mein, alfaz bhaari lag rahe thay.

phir mhar ne khamoshi tori:

"kiya humein — is ke bare mein baat krani chahiye?"

Aasim ne ise dekha: "haan — magar mein nahin janta kaise."

mhar ne kaafi ka cup apane haathon mein liya — jaise is mein jawab chahapa ho:

"mein drati hoon — is liay nahin ke mein ne jhoot kaha, balke is liay ke yeh sach hai. aur sach — sach hamesha drata hai."

Aasim ne is ki traf dekha — aur is ne is ka haath thama:

"mein bhi drata hoon — magar mein ne kal raat jo kaha, woh sach tha. aur aaj subah jab mein utha — toh woh sach ab bhi tha."

mhar ne ise dekha — is ki aankhon mein woh dar tha jo aahasta aahasta kam ho raha tha:

"toh phir — hum kyun dar rahe hain?"

"kyunke — mohabbat karna aasaan hai, magar yaqeen karna mushkil ke yeh khatam nahin ho gi."

---

woh der tak baithe rahe — aur aahasta aahasta, woh dar kam hone laga.

unhon ne baatein kin — chhoti chhoti baatein, jaise kal ka khana, aaj ki baarish, kal ka mosm. aur in chhoti baaton ke dramayan — in ka dar ghalane laga, jaise baraf paani mein.

jab woh uthe toh mhar ne kaha:

"aap jante hain — mein ne aaj sara din socha ke shayad mein ne ghalat kiya. magar ab — jab mein aap ke samne hoon — mujhe lagta hai ke mein ne wahi kiya jo shih tha."

Aasim maskraya: "hum ne shih kiya — aur yeh dar bhi guzar jaئe ga. jaise baarish guzar jati hai — aur is ke baad sirf tazagi ra jati hai."

woh kife se nikalay — aur is bar, in ke dramayan woh dar nahin tha jo pehle tha. sirf ek praskon yaqeen tha — ke unhon ne shih kaha, shih waqt par, shih alfaz mein.
