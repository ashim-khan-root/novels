woh kife se nikalay toh raat gehri thi — aur bahar baarish ho rahi thi.

halki phawar — jaise aasman bhi is shaam ki khobasorati mein shamal hona chahta ho. sarak par limp ki roshni paani mein manaks ho rahi thi, aur har traf woh khamoshi thi jo sirf baarish mein aati hai.

Aasim ne apani chahatri n-kali — aur ise mhar ke oopar kar diya. woh donon chahatri ke neeche khare thay — ek doosray ke qareeb, atana qareeb ke woh ek doosray ki saans mhasos kar sakte thay.

mhar ne kaha: "baarish — hamesha hamari khani ka hasa rahi hai."

Aasim maskraya: "aur hamesha rahe gi — kyunke baarish woh waqt hai jab duniya khamosh ho jati hai, aur dil bolane lagte hain."

woh saath saath chalane lage — chahatri ke neeche, purani galion mein, jahan har mor par ek naya manazr tha. baarish ki bondin chahatri par gr rahi thin — aur woh aawaz mausiqi ki tarah thi.

mhar ne rak kar kaha: "aap jante hain — jab mein hospital mein thi, mein har raat khirki se bahar dekhti thi aur sochati thi ke shayad baarish mire liay koi paigham laئe."

"haan — baarish ne kaha ke mein waps jaon. ke wahan koi hai jo mira antazar kar raha hai."

Aasim khamosh raha — magar is ki khamoshi mein ek pori khani thi.

---

woh ek purani seerhi par bith gaye — wahi seerhi jahan woh kabhi baithe thay, jab in ki khani sharoa ho rahi thi. ab wahi seerhi, wahi baarish, magar sab kuch badal chaka tha.

Aasim ne chahatri ek traf rakh di — aur woh donon baarish mein bith gaye. bhigate huye, hanaste huye, ek doosray ko dekhte huye.

mhar ne kaha: "paagal ho gaye hain? — hum bhig rahe hain."

Aasim hans pra: "paagal toh pehle se hain — magar yeh bhigana is ke qabl hai."

mhar ne ise dekha — aur woh bhi hans pri. woh donon baarish mein baithe hans rahe thay — jaise duniya mein koi gham nahin, koi fikr nahin, sirf yeh lamha tha.
