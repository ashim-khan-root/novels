ek din mhar ne poocha: "woh kaun sa kaam hai jo aap ne hamesha karna chaaha — magar kabhi nahin kiya?"

woh kife ki chhat par baithe thay — jahan ba-ba krim ne kuch purani krasayan rakhi thin aur ek chhota sa bagh banaya tha. yeh jagah in ki mulaqaaton ka naya maqam ban gai thi — khuli hawa, neela aasman, aur daur tak phila sheher.

aasaíam ne kuch der socha: "mein ne hamesha ek pal banane ka khwab dekha — magar woh pal nahin jo sheher ko sheher se malaئe. ek aisa pal jo logon ko malaئe — ek doosray se, in ke khwabon se, in ki yadon se."

mhar ne ise dekha: "yeh khubsurat hai — toh phir aap ne ise kyun nahin banaya?"

"kyunke — mein drata tha ke shayad yeh mumkin nahin. shayad pal sirf astil aur kankrit se bante hain — khwabon se nahin."

mhar khamosh ho gai — aur phir is ne kaha:

"mein ne hamesha shayari lakhane ka khwab dekha — apani shayari, apane alfaz mein. magar mein ne kabhi kisi ko nahin dakhaئi."

"kyun nahin?"

"kyunke — shayari woh chiz hai jo aap ke dil ke qareeb hoti hai. aur agar koi ise psnd na kare toh lagta hai jaise is ne aap ko psnd nahin kiya."

---

aasaíam ne is ki traf dekha — aur is ne woh kaha jo ise lagta tha:

"mujhe dakhaؤ — aap ki shayari."

mhar ne ise dekha — is ki aankhon mein hachakachahat thi, magar is ke saath ek chhoti si umeed bhi:

"aap sach mein dekhna chahte hain?"

"haan — mein sach mein dekhna chahta hoon."

mhar ne apana big khola aur is mein se ek purani note bak n-kali — jalad phati hui, kuch safahat pile. is ne ise aasaíam ki traf brhaya — aur is ke haath kanap rahe thay:

"yeh — mein ne brason mein kisi ko nahin dakhaئi."

aasaíam ne note bak li aur ise khola — pehle safahe par ek sher tha, is ki apani tehreer mein:

*"mein ne socha tha ke shayari sirf kitabon mein hoti he*

*magr phir mein ne dekha ke shayari baarish mein bhi hoti he*

*kafi ki khushbu mein bhay*

*war kisi ajanabi ki aankhon mein bhay*

*jo aap ko pehchan le —*

*aap ka nam jaane b-ghair"*

aasaíam ne yeh parha — aur woh khamosh raha. is ne note bak band ki aur mhar ki traf dekha:

"yeh — yeh sab se khubsurat chiz hai jo mein ne parhi hai."

mhar ne ise dekha — is ki aankhon mein yaqeen nahin tha:

"sach?"

"sach — aap shaayar ho, mhar. aap ne kabhi kisi ko bataya nahin — magar aap hain."

---

woh shaam ko kife se nikalay toh aasaíam ke haath mein mhar ki note bak thi — is ne ise prhane ke liay liya tha, aur mhar ne ise de diya tha, b-ghair kisi hachakachahat ke.

"aap ko pata hai — yeh pehli bar hai jab mein ne apani shayari kisi ko di hai."

aasaíam ne ise dekha: "mein janta hoon — aur mein ise snabhal kar rakhon ga."

"ise parhna — magar sirf is liay ke aap ise samjho ge, is liay nahin ke aap ise psnd karo ge."

aasaíam maskraya: "aap ki shayari ko samajhna — aur ise psnd karna — ek hi chiz hai. kyunke yeh aap hain — aur mein aap ko pehle hi psnd kurta hoon."

mhar ne ise dekha — aur is ki aankhon mein woh chamak thi jo sirf is shakhas ko aati hai jisay pehli bar samjha gaya ho:

"shukriya —"

"ks baat ka?"

"is baat ka — ke aap ne mujhe woh krane diya jo mein ne socha tha ke mein kabhi nahin kar skon gi — apani shayari kisi ko dakhana."

woh khamosh ho gaye — magar in ki khamoshi mein ek wada tha: ke woh ek doosray ke khwabon ki hafazt karein ge, chahe woh ktane hi chhotay kyun na hoon.
