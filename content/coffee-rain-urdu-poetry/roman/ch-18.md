title: "Chapter 18 — lafz"
weight: 18
type: roman
---


mhar ne is din apani diary mein ek masara likha — aur phir ise kat diya.

phir dobara likha — aur dobara kat diya.

woh teen bar lakh chaki thi — magar har bar alfaz na-kaafi lage. woh kehna chahti thi — magar woh drati thi ke agar is ne kha diya toh sab kuch badal jaئe ga.

woh shaam ko kife aai toh aasaíam pehle se mojod tha — is ne ise dekhte hi poocha:

"aap theek hain? — aap thaki hui lag rahi hain."

mhar ne sar halaya aur bith gai: "raat ko neend nahin aai — bas."

aasaíam ne ise dekha — is ki aankhon mein kuch tha jo woh chahapa nahin sakti thi:

"kuch kehna chahti ho?"

mhar ne ise dekha — aur ise laga jaise woh is ke zhan mein sab kuch prh sakta hai. is ne gehri saans li — aur is ne woh kha diya jo woh kehna chahti thi:

"mire khayal mein — mein mohabbat kar rahi hoon."

---

aasaíam khamosh raha — is ka dil zor se dharak raha tha, magar woh kuch nahin kha ska.

mhar ne ise dekha — is ki aankhon mein dar tha:

"aap kuch kyun nahin kehte?"

aasaíam ne is ki traf dekha — aur is ki aawaz mein woh narami thi jo is ne pehle kabhi nahin sni thi:

"kyunke — mein sirf yeh snana chahta hoon ke aap yeh kahein. mein yeh yaqeen karna chahta hoon ke yeh sach hai."

mhar ne is ka haath thama — is ki aankhon mein aansoo thay, magar woh maskra rahi thi:

"mein mohabbat kar rahi hoon — aap se."

pehli bar is ne atane qareeb se 'aap' kaha tha — aur woh ek lafz hi kaafi tha ke sab kuch badal jaئe.

---

aasaíam ne is ka haath thama — aur is ne is ki aankhon mein dekhte huye kaha:

"mein bhi — mein bhi aap se mohabbat kurta hoon. shayad is se bhi pehle ke mein janta ke mohabbat kiya hoti hai."

mhar ke aansoo behenay lage — magar woh khushi ke aansoo thay:

"toh phir — ab kiya ho ga?"

aasaíam maskraya: "ab — wahi ho ga jo hona chahiye. hum saath rahein ge, baatein karein ge, kitabein parhein ge, kaafi paئin ge, baarish mein chalein ge — aur ek doosray se mohabbat karte rahein ge."

mhar hans pri: "atana aasaan?"

"haan — kyunke mohabbat mushkil nahin hoti. mohabbat toh sirf ek faisla hai — aur hum ne woh faisla kar liya hai."

---

woh is raat kife se der se nikalay — magar unhein prawaa nahin thi.

bahar baarish ho rahi thi — magar is bar, woh is se bachane ki koshash nahin kar rahe thay. woh baarish mein khare rahe — is ka haath is ke haath mein, aur is ka sar is ke kandhay par.

mhar ne kaha: "mein ne socha tha ke yeh din kabhi nahin aaye ga."

aasaíam ne ise apane qareeb kiya: "mein ne bhi — magar jab aap aaein toh pata chala ke kuch chizin sirf waqt leti hain — woh namumkin nahin hotin."

woh khamosh khare rahe — baarish mein, raat mein, ek doosray ki b-unhon mein.

aur is raat — alfaz kaafi nahin thay. sirf khamoshi kaafi thi.
