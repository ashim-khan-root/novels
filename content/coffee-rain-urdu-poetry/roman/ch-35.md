Aasim uth khara hawa — aur is ke ghatane kanap rahe thay.

woh kife mein mojod logon ko dekh raha tha — sare chehray, sari aankhein, sari toqaat. ise lag raha tha jaise woh kisi bahut awanachi jagah par khara ho aur neeche ghera paani ho.

magar phir is ne mhar ki traf dekha — woh maskra rahi thi, aur is ki aankhon mein woh yaqeen tha jo is ne hamesha ise diya tha — b-ghair kuch kahe, b-ghair kisi wade ke.

is ne gehri saans li — aur is ne astij ki traf brhana sharoa kar diya.

kife mein khamoshi chaha gai — magar woh khamoshi snane wali thi.

Aasim ne jari rakha — aur is ki aawaz ab pehle se zyada mazabot thi:

"aur jab mein ne is ka jawab parha — toh mujhe laga jaise pehli bar koi mujhe samjha hai. b-ghair mire chehray ko dekhe, b-ghair mira nam jaane — is ne mujhe pehchan liya tha."

is ki nazar mhar par thi — jo ab apani aankhein nahin chahapa rahi thi:

"aur aaj — mein yahan hoon, is ke samne, aur mein sirf atana kehna chahta hoon —"

woh rak gaya — is ki aankhon mein nami thi. is ne gehri saans li:

"ke woh jawab — wahi miri zindagi ka sab se khubsurat lamha tha. aur mein — mein is ke liay shakar gazar hoon. hamesha."

woh is ke paas aaya — aur woh donon ek doosray ke samne khare thay, kife ki gram roshni mein, mom batayawan ke dramayan, kitabon ke qareeb.

mhar ne bahut aahasta se kaha — is ki aawaz kanap rahi thi:

"woh sher — woh mira bhi hai — kyunke mein ne ise apana bana liya hai. jaise mein ne aap ko apana bana liya hai."

Aasim ne is ka haath thama — aur is bar, is ne ise chhora nahin:

"aur mein ne aap ko — is pehle din se — jab mein ne kitab mein woh jawab dekha tha."

woh khamosh khare rahe — kife mein mojod sab log unhein dekh rahe thay, magar unhein kisi ki prawaa nahin thi.

woh sirf ek doosray ko dekh rahe thay — aur yahi kaafi tha.
