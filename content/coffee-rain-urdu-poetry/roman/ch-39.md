ek din kife ki chhat par baithe huye — mhar ne woh sawal poocha jo is ne kabhi nahin poocha tha:

"sna — kisi thin?"

aasaíam khamosh ho gaya — is ka nam kisi aur ne is se pehle nahin liya tha. is ne kaafi ka cup apane haathon mein liya — aur bahut der tak khamosh raha. phir is ne kehna sharoa kiya:

"sna — woh bahut khubsurat thin. andar se bhi, bahar se bhi. woh mire skol ki tichar thi — hum teen saal saath thay."

is ne gehri saans li:

"magar mein — mein in se mohabbat kurta tha, magar woh mohabbat zahar nahin kar sakta tha. mein khamosh tha, band tha — aur woh chahti thin ke mein khul kar baat karon. mein nahin kar ska — aur woh chali gaئin."

mhar ne is ka haath thama: "kiya aap ko lagta hai ke woh ghalat thin?"

aasaíam ne ise dekha: "nahin — woh shih thin. har shakhas ko woh mohabbat malani chahiye jo woh chahta hai. mein unhein woh nahin de sakta tha — aur unhon ne woh dhond liya jo woh chahti thin."

"magar aap ab bhi —"

"ab bhi unhein yaad kurta hoon — magar woh yaad ab dard nahin deti. woh sirf ek yaad hai — aur mein aage brh chaka hoon."

mhar ne bhi apani khani snaئi — aur is bar, is ne woh sab bataya jo is ne pehle kabhi kisi ko nahin bataya tha:

"woh aadmi — is ne mujhe bataya ke mein is ki zindagi mein sab se aham chiz hoon. magar woh yeh bhi chahta tha ke mein apani shayari chhor don, apani nokri chhor don, apane khwab chhor don."

is ki aankhon mein purana dard tha — magar woh maskra rahi thi:

"aur mein ne do saal is mein gazare — khod ko chhota karte huye. jab mein ne ise chhora toh mujhe laga jaise mein ne apani jaan bacha li — magar saath hi mujhe laga ke shayad mohabbat mire liay nahin hai."

aasaíam ne ise dekha: "aur ab — kiya lagta hai?"

mhar ne is ki aankhon mein dekha: "ab mujhe lagta hai ke mohabbat mire liay thi — magar shih shakhas ke saath. aur woh shakhas — aap hain."

---

woh der tak khamosh baithe rahe — chhat par, staron ke neeche, ek doosray ke qareeb.

in ke dramayan ab koi raz nahin tha — unhon ne apane zakham ek doosray ko dakha diay thay, aur woh zakham ab kam dard kar rahe thay.

kyunke kabhi kabhi — zakhamon ko bantana hi in ka sab se bhatrin alaj hota hai.

aasaíam ne kaha: "mein ne aaj pehli bar sna ke bare mein kisi se baat ki — aur mujhe laga jaise mein ne ek bojah utaar diya."

aasaíam ne ise apane qareeb kiya: "hum ek doosray ke zakhamon ko mandamal krane mein madad kar sakte hain — sirf ek doosray ke saath ra kar."

mhar ne is ke kandhay par sar rakha — aur is ne aankhein band kar lein:

"yahi toh hai — yahi sab kuch hai."

woh khamosh baithe rahe — aur in ke purane zakham aahasta aahasta bharane lage.
