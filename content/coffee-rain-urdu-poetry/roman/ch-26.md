chotha jamaa — aur aasaíam ko ab yaqeen ho chala tha ke woh nahin aaye gi.

woh kife mein aaya — magar is bar ise jaldi nahin thi. woh aahasta chalta hawa dakhal hawa, jaise woh kisi janaze mein sharik ho. is ne kaafi ka aaradr diya — aur pehle kaafi pi, phir almari ke paas gaya.

woh khat — jahan is ne apana dil khol kar rakh diya tha — wisa hi tha, jaise is ne chhora tha. kisi ne ise chahawa bhi nahin tha.

---

woh kitab le kar apani jagah par aaya aur bith gaya. is ne khat ko n-kala aur khod hi ise parha — jaise woh bhol gaya ho ke is ne kiya likha tha. har lafz ise yaad tha — magar ise prh kar ise laga jaise koi aur shakhas yeh khat lakh raha tha, koi aisa shakhas jo bahut kamzor tha, bahut tanha, bahut mohabbat mein mbatala.

is ne khat ko jora — aur ise waps kitab mein rakh diya. phir is ne kitab band ki aur ise almari mein waps rakh diya — magar is bar, is ne ise aise rakha jaise woh ise dobara kabhi nahin chahawaئe ga.

"aaon ga, ba-ba ji — kife toh aaon ga."

"yeh rakho —"

aasaíam ne kitab ki traf ashara kiya — magar is ki traf dekhe b-ghair:

"yeh kitab — ab ise wohein rhane do. shayad koi aur aaye jo ise prhe."

woh mara aur kife se nikal gaya — aur ba-ba krim khamosh khare rahe, kitab ko dekhte huye, jaise woh jante thay ke yeh khatama nahin tha.

aasaíam ghar aaya toh is ne apana coat utaara aur sofe par gr gaya.

ise bahut thakawat mhasos ho rahi thi — magar woh thakawat jasmani nahin thi. woh is ke dil mein thi, is ke zhan mein, is ki roh mein.

is ne socha — shayad yeh mohabbat nahin thi. shayad yeh sirf ek waham tha jo is ne apane zhan mein bana liya tha kyunke woh tanha tha. shayad woh sirf is liay is se mohabbat kar baitha tha ke ise kisi ki zarorat thi — aur woh is ki zarorat ke waqt aa gai thi.

magar phir ise woh sab yaad aa gaya — is ki hansi, is ki khamoshi, is ki aankhon mein woh chamak jab woh sher parhti thi. aur woh jaan gaya ke yeh waham nahin tha — yeh sach tha.

aur shayad asi liay — yeh atana takleef da tha.
