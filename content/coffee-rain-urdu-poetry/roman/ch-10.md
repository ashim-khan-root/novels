do hafate guzar gaye — aur woh har roz milte rahe.

kifa in ki duniya ban chaka tha — lakri ki alamarayan, gram roshni, kaafi ki khushbu, aur woh purana jaz jo ba-ba krim har shaam b-jate thay. woh donon is duniya mein rehte thay — bahar ki duniya se kte huye, sirf ek doosray ke liay mojod.

magar aaj — kuch makhatalaf tha.

Aasim kife mein aaya toh mhar pehle se mojod thi — magar is bar is ke samne *klayatِ fiz* rakhi thi, aur is ke haath mein qalam tha.

"kiya kar rahi hain?"

mhar ne qalam rakha aur kitab is ki traf brha di — woh safha jahan is ne pehla sher likha tha, ab pora bhar chaka tha. ek se zyada sher, ek se zyada jawab — aur ab, sab se neeche, mhar ne apani tehreer mein likha tha:

*"hum ek doosray ka nam nahin jante thay — magar hum ek doosray ko pehchan gaye. shayad is liay ke nam sirf pakarane ke liay hote hain — aur pehchan toh dil se hoti hai."*

Aasim ne ise parha — aur is ne mhar ki traf dekha:

"aap ne kyun likha?"

"kyunke mein chahti hoon ke yeh kitab hamesha yaad rakhe — ke hum kahan se sharoa huye thay. chahe aage kuch bhi ho — yeh safha hamesha rahe ga."

---

woh is din kife se nikalay toh shaam ho chaki thi — magar woh alag nahin huye.

woh saath saath chalte rahe — purani galion mein, jahan har mor par ek naya manazr tha. mhar ne bataya ke ise yeh sheher kaise lagta hai — ajib, magar khubsurat, jaise koi purani film jisay bar bar dikhane ka dil kurta hai.

Aasim ne bataya ke woh is sheher mein pla brha hai — magar is ne kabhi ise is tarah nahin dekha jaise ab dekh raha hai.

"kiya badal gaya?" mhar ne poocha.

Aasim ne ise dekha — aur is ne sach kaha:

"aap."

---

woh ek chhoti si seerhi par bith gaye — jo ek purani dakan ke bahar thi, jahan se pori gali dakhaئi deti thi.

Aasim ne poocha: "kiya aap ne kabhi socha hai — ke agar hum is kitab mein na milte toh kiya hota?"

mhar ne kuch der socha — aur phir kaha:

"shayad hum kabhi na milte — ya shayad kisi aur tarah milte. magar mujhe lagta hai ke jis tarah se hum mile — wahi shih tha. kyunke alfaz ke b-ghair — shayad hum ek doosray ko pehchan na pate."

Aasim ne sar halaya: "aap jante hain — mein ne brason socha ke mohabbat ek chiz hai jo hoti hai — ya nahin hoti. magar aap se mal kar mein ne sikha ke mohabbat koi chiz nahin — yeh ek rasta hai. aur hum is raste par saath saath chal rahe hain — b-ghair yeh jaane ke yeh kahan khatam hota hai."

mhar ne is ka haath thama — pehli bar, b-ghair kisi hachakachahat ke:

"shayad — yeh kahein khatam nahin hota. shayad yeh rasta sirf sharoa hawa hai."

---

raat kaafi gehri thi jab woh alag huye ـ magar is bar, in ke dramayan woh be yaqini nahin thi jo pehle thi.

woh jante thay — yeh sirf ek dosti nahin thi. yeh sirf ek kitab ka tabadala nahin tha. yeh woh chiz thi jis ka nam woh nahin de sakte thay — magar jisay woh donon mhasos kar rahe thay.

Aasim ne ise is ke darwazay tak chhora — aur jate jate, mhar ne mar kar kaha:

"kal — jamaa hai."

"mein janta hoon."

"mein aaon gi."

"mein antazar karon ga — jaise hamesha kurta hoon."

woh maskraئi aur andar chali gai — aur Aasim wohein khara raha, is ke jaane ke baad bhi, is darwazay ko dekhta raha jo is ke peeche band ho gaya tha.

---

woh raat is ne apani diary mein likha:

*"aaj mein ne mhasos kiya ke mohabbat ka matalab kisi ko pane se nahin — kisi ko phachanane se hai. aur mein ne mhar ko pehchan liya hai — is ke alfaz mein, is ki khamoshi mein, is ki muskurahat mein, aur is ki aankhon mein jo jhoot nahin bolatin.*

*ham ne abhi tak kuch nahin kaha — aur shayad yahi sab se khubsurat chiz hai. kyunke jab alfaz aate hain toh woh apane saath toqaat late hain — aur hum ne toqaat ke b-ghair mohabbat karna sikh liya hai.*

*ia sirf sharoaat hai. mein janta hoon — kyunke kitab abhi khatam nahin hui.*"
