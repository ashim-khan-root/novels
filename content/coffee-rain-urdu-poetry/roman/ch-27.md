---
title: "Chapter 27 — aakhari khat"
weight: 27
type: roman
---


panachawan jamaa tha — aur aasaíam ne ghar se nklane se pehle bahut der socha.

woh jaane ka arada kar ke athata — aur phir bith jata. teen bar yeh hawa. chothi bar — woh utha aur ghar se nkla.

kife ka darwaza is ke liay pehle se zyada bhaari lag raha tha. is ne ise dhakila — aur purani pital ki ghanti ne apani aawaz di, wahi aawaz jo ise kabhi ghar jisi lagti thi. ab woh aawaz ise ajanabi lag rahi thi.

woh andar aaya — aur is ne dekha ke is ka woh kona khali tha. woh kursi jahan woh baitha kurta tha — woh khali thi.

magar woh is bar is kaunay mein nahin baitha.

---

woh sidha almari ke paas gaya — aur is ne *klayatِ fiz* n-kali. woh purani kitab, jis ne unhein malaya tha, jis mein in ke pehle alfaz thay, in ki pehli hansi, in ka pehla dar — woh kitab ab khamosh thi.

is ne kitab kholi — aur is ne ek safed kaghaz n-kala jo is ne ghar se laya tha. is ne qalam n-kala — wahi qalam jis ne pehla sher likha tha.

aur is ne likhna sharoa kiya:

---

*"mhar,*

*aaj panachawan jamaa hai — aur mein ne faisla kiya hai ke yeh aakhari khat ho ga.*

*min nahin janta ke aap kyun nahin aaein — aur shayad mein kabhi nahin jaanon ga. magar mein ne apane aap se wada kiya hai ke mein aaj ke baad mazid nahin lakhon ga.*

*aap ne mujhe bahut kuch diya — shayad aap khod nahin jantin ke ktana. aap ne mujhe dakhaya ke ek purani kitab mein bhi nayi khanayan likhi jaa sakti hain. aap ne mujhe skhaya ke khamoshi mein bhi baatein ki jaa sakti hain. aur aap ne mujhe — ek ajanabi ko — yeh yaqeen diya ke dil sardion ke baad bhi khul sakte hain.*

*min aap ka shakar gazar hoon — is har jamaa ke liay, har sher ke liay, har khamoshi ke liay.*

*agr yeh alwida hai — toh yeh jaan kar jaon ga ke mein ne aap se mohabbat ki, chahe woh mohabbat adhori hi kyun na rahi.*

*khada hafaz*
*aasim"*

---

aasaíam ne khat ko do bar parha — aur is ki aankhein nam thin. is ne khat ko ahatayat se jora — jaise woh kisi qimati chiz ko chhu raha ho — aur ise kitab ke asi safahe par rakh diya jahan se sab kuch sharoa hawa tha.

phir is ne kitab band ki — aur ise almari mein waps rakh diya.

magar is bar — is ne kitab ko aise rakha jaise woh ise hamesha ke liay alwida kha raha ho.

---

woh mara — aur kife se nklane laga.

ba-ba krim ne ise dekha — aur unhon ne kuch nahin poocha. woh sirf kaؤantr ke peeche khare rahe, khamosh, jaise woh jante thay ke yeh lamha aaye ga.

aasaíam darwazay tak phanacha — magar phir rak gaya. is ne peeche mar kar dekha — kife ki gram roshni, purani alamarayan, woh kona jahan woh baitha kurta tha, woh kursi jahan woh baitha karti thi.

is ne gehri saans li — aur is ne darwaza khola.

purani pital ki ghanti ne phir aawaz di — magar is bar woh aawaz kisi aur chiz ki tarah thi. woh kisi darwazay ke band hone ki aawaz thi.

---

woh bahar nkla toh shaam thi — aur hawa thandi thi.

woh sarak par khara raha — aur is ne aasman ki traf dekha. baadal thay — shayad baarish ho gi, shayad nahin. magar ise prawaa nahin thi.

woh ghar gaya — aur is raat, is ne *dayawanِ ghalab* n-kali. wahi kitab jo is ne mhar ko di thi — aur jo mhar ne ise waps kar di thi. is ne ghalab ka ek sher parha:

*"hum ko maloom hai jannat ki haqiqat likan*

*dal ke khatr rakhte hain tsor ghalab"*

is ne kitab band ki — aur woh smajah gaya ke is sher ka matalab kiya tha. woh janta tha ke jannat haqiqi nahin thi — magar is ne ise apane tsor mein basaئe rakha tha. jaise woh mhar ko apane dil mein basaئe rakhe ga — chahe woh kabhi na aaye.

---

is raat is ne apani diary mein nahin likha — kyunke lakhane ke liay kuch nahin bacha tha.
