woh ek hafta antazar kar sakta tha — magar is ne teen din baad hi kife ka rakh kar liya.

badh ki dophar thi, kife mein sirf do log thay — ek boorha aadmi akhbar prh raha tha aur ek larki lip tap par kaam kar rahi thi. ba-ba krim ne ise dekha toh in ki abro uth gai:

"aaj jamaa nahin hai, beta."

Aasim ne jawab nahin diya — woh sidha almari ke paas gaya. *klayatِ fiz* wohein thi. is ne kitab n-kali aur is safahe ko khola jahan is ne sher likha tha — aur woh sher ab bhi wohein tha.

magar neeche ka jawab — woh bhi wohein tha.

is ne ise dobara parha — jaise ise yaqeen nahin aa raha tha ke yeh waqai hawa hai. woh kife mein bith gaya, kitab apane samne rakhi, aur is likhai ko dekhta raha — khubsurat, dhalawan, jaise har haraf mein jaan thi.

is ne apani jib se qalam n-kala — wahi qalam jis ne pehla sher likha tha — aur is ne doosray sher ke neeche likhna sharoa kiya:

*"ab ke hum bachahare toh shayad kabhi khwabon mein malin*

*jas tarah sokhe huye phool kitabon mein mile"*

is ne qalam band kiya, kitab band ki, aur ise apani jagah par rakh diya — almari mein, asi jagah jahan se is ne li thi.

ba-ba krim ise dekh rahe thay — unhon ne kuch nahin kaha, magar in ki aankhon mein woh chamak thi jo kuch kha rahi thi.

"kiya baat hai, ba-ba ji?"

ba-ba krim ne sar halaya: "kuch nahin, beta — sirf yeh soch raha tha ke chalees saalon mein mein ne is kitab ko kabhi khali nahin dekha. ab is mein do sher aur aa gaye — shayad yeh kitab kabhi bhar jaئe."

---

woh jamaa ka antazar nahin kar ska — woh agle din phir aaya.

yeh jamarat thi, aur is bar kife mein zyada log thay. is ne kitab n-kali aur ise khola — aur is ka dil phir se rak gaya.

jawab tha.

*"yeh baat phil gai ak sheher mein ke hum na male*

*to phir koi aur shi — magar aap na mile"*

Aasim ne ise parha — aur woh maskraya. ise nahin pata tha ke yeh kaun hai, magar woh janta tha ke yeh koi aisa shakhas hai jo fiz ko samajhta hai — aur shayad ise bhi samajhta hai.

is ne qalam n-kala aur jawab likha:

*"aap aaye hain toh utar aaye ho gham ke phar*

*warana tanhai bhi ak bojah tha kandhon ke liay"*

---

woh jamaa ko phir aaya — aur yeh slasla jari ra gaya.

har bar jab woh aata, kitab mein kuch naya hota — ek naya sher, ek naya jawab. kabhi woh fiz ke sher astamal kurta, kabhi ghalab ke, kabhi apane alfaz mein kuch lakh deta. aur woh — jis ka nam woh nahin janta tha — woh bhi asi tarah jawab deti thi.

ise pata chal gaya tha ke woh aurat hai — likhai se, alfaz ke intikhab se, aur is narami se jo har sher mein chahapi hoti thi. magar is ne kabhi poocha nahin.

kyunke kabhi kabhi — nam ke b-ghair, chehray ke b-ghair — baatein zyada sachi hoti hain.

---

do hafate guzar gaye — aur Aasim ne mhasos kiya ke woh jamaa ka antazar krane laga hai.

woh daftar mein baitha palon ke dizaئan bana raha tha magar is ka zhan in sheron ke grad ghom raha tha. woh soch raha tha — yeh kaun hai? yeh fiz ko kyun parhti hai? yeh atani khubsurat lakhati kyun hai?

magar is ne kabhi nahin poocha.

ek din is ne kitab mein likha:

*"mein aap ka nam nahin janta — magar mujhe lagta hai ke mein aap ko janta hoon. kiya yeh mumkin hai?"*

agle din jawab aaya:

*"namon ne toh humein kabhi samjha hi nahin — shayad alfaz behtar jante hain ke hum kaun hain."*

Aasim ne yeh parha aur woh der tak khamosh baitha raha — kife ki gram roshni mein, is kitab ke saath jo ab sirf ek kitab nahin rahi thi.

yeh ab ek darwaza thi — aur woh is mein se guzar raha tha, b-ghair yeh jaane ke doosri traf kiya hai.
