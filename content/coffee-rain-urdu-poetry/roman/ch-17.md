ba-ba krim ne chalees saalon mein bahut kuch dekha tha.

unhon ne is kife mein logon ko aate jate dekha tha — kuch kaafi pine aate, kuch kitabein prhane, kuch tanhai se bhagane, aur kuch — kuch aise bhi thay jo mohabbat dhondane aate thay, chahe woh khod na jante hoon.

magar aasaíam aur mhar — woh makhatalaf thay.

ba-ba krim apane kaؤantr ke peeche khare unhein dekh rahe thay — woh chhat par baithe thay, is ki kitab is ke haath mein thi, aur woh ise kuch prh kar sna rahi thi. is ki aankhein chamak rahi thin — woh aankhein jo is ne pehli bar is waqt dekhi thin jab woh kife mein aai thi, ajanabi, khofazada, apane saath sirf ek kitab aur ek tota dil le kar.

aur ab — woh hans rahi thi. sach mein hans rahi thi, jaise ise bhol gai ho ke ise drana hai.

ba-ba krim ne kaafi banai — aur woh khod in ke paas le gaya.

"yeh loo — aap ke liay khas."

mhar ne cup liya: "shukriya, ba-ba ji — aap humein kharab kar rahe hain."

"beta — mein ne aap ko is kife mein aate dekha hai jab aap toti hui thin. aur mein ne aap ko dobara jarate dekha hai — is ki waja se nahin, balke is liay ke aap ne khod ko jorane ka faisla kiya."

mhar ne ise dekha: "aap sab jante hain, hai na?"

"mein chalees saalon se logon ko dekh raha hoon — aur mein ne sikha hai ke kabhi kabhi sab se bari madad khamosh rehna hoti hai. aap donon ko ek doosray ko dhondane ki zarorat thi — aur mein ne sirf woh jagah banai jahan aap dhond sako."

---

is raat kife band hone ke baad — ba-ba krim akela baitha raha.

is ne *klayatِ fiz* ko almari se n-kala — woh purani kitab jisay is ne kabhi kisi ko bichane nahin diya tha. is ne woh safha khola jahan se sab kuch sharoa hawa tha — aasaíam ka pehla sher, aur mhar ka pehla jawab.

woh khamoshi se maskraya.

ise apani biwi yaad aa gai — woh bhi asi tarah mili thi. kitab ke zariae, khutoot ke zariae, alfaz ke zariae. is ne socha — shayad mohabbat ka koi aur tareeqa nahin hota. shayad mohabbat sirf alfaz se hoti hai, aur phir woh alfaz shakl badalate hain — magar kabhi khatam nahin hote.

"dekho — mein ne aap se wada kiya tha ke yeh kife mohabbat ki jagah rahe ga. aur aaj — mein ne phir se dekh liya ke woh jado jo kitabon mein hota hai — woh haqiqat mein bhi ho sakta hai."
