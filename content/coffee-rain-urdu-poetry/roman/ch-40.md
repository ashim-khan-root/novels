ek din Aasim kife mein aaya toh is ke haath mein ek chhota sa pikt tha — bhore kaghaz mein lapta, soti dori se bandha hawa.

mhar ne ise dekha toh maskraئi: "yeh kiya hai — ek aur thafa?"

Aasim ne pikt is ki traf brhaya: "yeh — aakhari thafa nahin, magar ek khas thafa hai."

mhar ne dori kholi aur kaghaz hataya — andar ek kitab thi. purana aidishan — *dayawanِ ghalab*, jalad phati hui, kuch safahat pile. magar yeh koi aur kapi nahin thi — yeh wahi thi jo Aasim ne ise pehle di thi, aur jo is ne waps kar di thi.

"yeh toh —"

"mein janta hoon — aap ne yeh waps kar di thi. magar mein chahta hoon ke aap ise rakhein — is bar hamesha ke liay."

Aasim ne kitab kholi — aur pehle safahe par is ne naya likha tha:

*"mhar ke liay —*
*jas ne mujhe skhaya ke mohabbat sirf alfaz mein nahin,*
*blaka khamoshi mein bhi hoti hai.*
*aap ke saath, mein ne sikha ke dil sardion ke baad bhi khul sakte hain —*
*war aap woh b-haar hain jo mire dil mein hamesha rahe gi.*
*— aasim"*

mhar ne yeh parha — aur is ki aankhein nam ho gaئin:

"aap ne — yeh likha —"

Aasim ne sar halaya: "yeh wahi hai jo mein ne hamesha kehna chaaha — magar alfaz nahin mile."

---

mhar ne bhi apane big se kuch n-kala — ek chhoti si diary, jalad siyah, sada magar khubsurat.

"yeh — aap ke liay."

Aasim ne diary li aur ise khola — pehle safahe par mhar ne likha tha:

*"aasim ke liay —*
*apani shayari lakhane ke liay.*
*kayawanka aap sirf ghalab aur fiz ko prhane ke liay nahin banaئe gaye —*
*aap khod ek shaayar hain, aur ab waqt hai ke aap apane alfaz talash karein.*
*— mhar"*

Aasim ne diary ko dekha — aur is ki aankhein chamak uthein:

"yeh — yeh sab se khubsurat thafa hai."

mhar maskraئi: "ab aap ke paas koi bhana nahin — ab aap ko likhna ho ga."

Aasim ne diary ko apane sine se lagaya — jaise woh koi qimati chiz ho:

"mein lakhon ga — aur pehli nazm aap ke liay ho gi."

---

woh shaam kife ki chhat par baithe baatein karte rahe — kitabon ke bare mein, khwabon ke bare mein, in chizon ke bare mein jo woh ek saath karna chahte thay.

mhar ne kaha: "ek din — mein apani shayari ki kitab shaئa karon gi."

Aasim ne kaha: "ek din — mein woh pal banaؤan ga jis ka mein ne khwab dekha tha."

mhar ne ise dekha: "aur hum —"

Aasim maskraya: "aur hum — saath rahein ge. jaise ab hain, jaise hamesha rahe."

woh khamosh baithe rahe — aur in ke samne sheher ki roshanayan phili hui thin, jaise in ke amakanat ki tarah lamhadod.
