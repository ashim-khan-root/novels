ek din kife mein baithe huye — in ke dramayan ek bhas chahar gai.

mhar ne kaha: "fiz sab se baray shaayar hain — koi in ki tarah mohabbat aur anaqalab ko ek saath nahin lakh sakta."

Aasim ne sar halaya: "fiz azim hain — magar ghalab."

mhar ne ise dekha: "ghalab? woh toh shakait krane wale shaayar hain — har sher mein dard, har masara mein gla."

Aasim hans pra: "aap ghalab ko nahin samjhein — ghalab sirf dard ke shaayar nahin hain. woh zindagi ke shaayar hain — is ki aapam pichidagayawan ke saath."

"tabhi toh mein fiz ko psnd karti hoon — woh sada hain, sidhe, dil ki baat kehte hain b-ghair kisi alajahan ke."

"aur ghalab alajahan hai? — ghalab toh woh aaina hai jis mein aap apani pori zindagi dekh sakte ho. ek sher mein woh aap ko hanasata bhi hai aur ralata bhi."

"yeh woh sawal hai jis ka koi jawab nahin — jaise yeh poocho ke kaafi behtar hai ya chai. donon ki apani jagah hai, donon ka apana zaiqa hai."

"magar —" Aasim ne kehna chaaha.

ba-ba krim ne ise roka: "sno, beta — fiz aur ghalab mein fraq nahin hai. woh ek hi chiz ke do rakh hain — ek traf mohabbat hai, doosri traf zindagi. aur aap donon — ek traf fiz ho, doosri traf ghalab. aur shayad asi liay aap ek doosray ke liay bane ho — kyunke aap ek doosray ko makamal karte ho."

---

woh donon khamosh ho gaye — ba-ba krim ki baat ne unhein sochane par majabor kar diya tha.

mhar ne pehle khamoshi tori: "shayad ba-ba ji shih kehte hain — mein fiz hoon aur aap ghalab."

Aasim maskraya: "aur shayad — asi liay hum ek doosray ki shayari ko samajhte hain. kyunke hum makhatalaf hain, magar ek hi zaban bolate hain."

mhar ne kaafi ka cup athaya — aur is ne kaha:

"aap jante hain — aaj mein ne fiz ko thoda sa kam aur ghalab ko thoda sa zyada samajhna sharoa kar diya hai."

"kyun?"

"kyunke — aap ghalab hain. aur mein aap ko samajhna chahti hoon."

Aasim ne ise dekha — aur is ne bahut narami se kaha:

"aur mein — aaj se fiz ko thoda aur qareeb se prhon ga. kyunke aap fiz hain — aur mein aap ko apani zindagi ka hasa b-nana chahta hoon."
