"aap jante hain — yeh pehla moqa hai jab mein kisi se is tarah baat kar rahi hoon. b-ghair kisi dar ke, b-ghair kisi deewar ke."

"ba-ba ji ko sab pata hai — woh is kife ke malak se zyada is ki roh hain."

ba-ba krim ne sar halaya: "chalees saal — mein ne yahan ktane logon ko aate jate dekha hai. magar aap donon — aap makhatalaf ho."

is ne jaane se pehle ek bar aur dekha — aur is ki aankhon mein woh chamak thi jo sirf is shakhas ki hoti hai jo koi khubsurat waqaa hote dekh raha ho.

aasaíam ne is ki aankhon mein dekha — aur is ne kaha:

mhar ne kuch nahin kaha — magar is ka khamosh rehna bhi ek jawab tha.

---

jab woh alag huye toh bahar baarish ho rahi thi.

"kiya aap —" donon ne ek saath kaha.

woh hans pre.

"pehle aap," mhar ne kaha.

aasaíam ne gehri saans li: "kiya aap kal bhi ao gi — jamaa nahin hai, magar shayad —"

"aaon gi —"
