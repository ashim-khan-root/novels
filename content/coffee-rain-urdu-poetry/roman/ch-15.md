---
title: "Chapter 15 — qismat ya intikhab"
weight: 15
type: roman
---


woh kife ki chhat par baithe thay — raat thi, aur oopar stare thay.

bahut der ki khamoshi ke baad — mhar ne woh sawal poocha jis ke bare mein woh donon soch rahe thay magar kha nahin paa rahe thay:

"kiya mohabbat — qismat hai ya intikhab?"

Aasim ne ise dekha — yeh sawal aasaan nahin tha:

"mein nahin janta — shayad yeh donon hai."

"kaise?"

"kisi se milna qismat hai — magar is ke saath rehna intikhab hai. hum atafaq se milte hain — magar hum rhane ka faisla khod karte hain."

mhar ne is ke jawab par ghor kiya — aur phir is ne kaha:

"mein pehle sochati thi ke mohabbat sirf qismat hai — ke aap kisi ke liay banaئe gaye ho aur ek din aap ise paa loo ge. magar ab —"

"ab?"

"ab mein sochati hoon ke mohabbat intikhab bhi hai — kyunke agar sirf qismat hoti toh hum kabhi kisi ke liay nahin larate. aur mein — mein aap ke liay larana chahon gi."

---

Aasim ne is ki traf dekha — aur is ke dil mein hazaar baatein thin magar woh sirf atana kha ska:

"aap jante hain — sna ke baad, mein ne socha ke mohabbat ek atafaq hai jo hota hai — aur phir khatam ho jata hai. magar aap se mal kar mein ne sikha ke mohabbat atafaq nahin — yeh ek rasta hai jisay aap chante hain, har roz, har fisle mein."

mhar ne ise dekha: "toh kiya hum —"

"hum ne ek doosray ko chana — shayad is se pehle ke hum jante ke hum chan rahe hain. mein ne aap ko is waqt chana jab mein ne pehla sher likha — aur aap ne mujhe is waqt chana jab aap ne jawab likha."

mhar khamosh rahi — magar is ki khamoshi mein ek pori khani thi.

---

phir is ne kaha:

"mein drati hoon —"

"ks baat se?"

"is baat se ke agar yeh intikhab hai — toh ghalat bhi ho sakta hai."

Aasim ne is ka haath thama — aur is ki aankhon mein woh yaqeen tha jo sirf is shakhas ko hota hai jis ne apane dar ka samana kiya ho:

"har intikhab ghalat ho sakta hai — magar kuch intikhab atane khubsurat hote hain ke agar woh ghalat bhi hoon toh woh safr yaad rehta hai. aur mein — mein aap ke saath is safr par jaane ke liay tayar hoon, chahe yeh kahein bhi khatam ho."

mhar ne is ki aankhon mein dekha — aur is ne woh kaha jo is ne kabhi kisi se nahin kaha tha:

"mein bhi —"

---

woh raat jab woh alag huye toh stare ab bhi chamak rahe thay.

Aasim ne ghar jaa kar apani diary mein likha:

*"aaj hum ne is sawal ka jawab dhonda jis ka koi jawab nahin — ke mohabbat qismat hai ya intikhab. aur shayad jawab yeh hai ke mohabbat woh chiz hai jo qismat se sharoa hoti hai aur intikhab se prawan charhati hai.*

*min ne ise chana — aur is ne mujhe chana. aur ab, chahe kuch bhi ho, hum ek doosray ke intikhab mein khare hain.*

*ihi kaafi hai."*
