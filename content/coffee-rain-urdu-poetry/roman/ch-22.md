ba-ba krim ke kife ko chalees saal ho gaye thay — aur is moqa par kife mein ek chhoti si taqarib thi.

purani roshanayan, mom batayan, aur har traf phool — kife pehle se bhi zyada khubsurat lag raha tha. wahi purana jaz baj raha tha, wahi kaafi ki khushbu thi — magar aaj kuch khas tha.

Aasim aur mhar ek kaunay mein baithe thay — ek doosray ke qareeb, kife ko dekhte huye.

Aasim ne kaha: "yeh kife — is ne humein ek doosray se malawaya. agar yeh na hota — toh shayad hum kabhi na milte."

mhar ne ise dekha: "milte — magar shayad is tarah nahin. mein manti hoon ke yeh jagah jadawaئi hai."

"kiya chiz ise jadawaئi banati hai?"

mhar ne chaaron traf dekha — kitabein, roshanayan, log — aur phir is ne kaha:

"woh log jo yahan aate hain — aur woh khanayan jo woh apane saath late hain. aur shayad — woh khanayan jo yahan sharoa hoti hain."

---

ba-ba krim ne sab ko makhatab kiya — woh kaؤantr ke paas khare thay, in ke haath mein ek purani kitab thi — *klayatِ fiz*.

"chalees saal pehle — mein ne yeh kife khola tha. is waqt mire paas kuch nahin tha — sirf kuch kitabein, ek purani kaafi mashin, aur ek khwab ke yeh jagah mohabbat ki jagah ho gi."

is ki aawaz mein jazbaat thay:

"aaj — chalees saal baad — mein dekh sakta hoon ke woh khwab sach hawa hai. mein ne is kife mein mohabbat ko kaئi bar aate dekha hai — kabhi dosti mein, kabhi kitabon mein, kabhi kaafi ke cup mein. magar aaj —"

is ne Aasim aur mhar ki traf dekha:

"aaj mein yeh kha sakta hoon ke is kife ne apani sab se khubsurat khani dekhi hai — do log jo kitab ke zariae mile, alfaz ke zariae jare, aur ab — woh ek doosray ke b-ghair adhore hain."

---

taqarib khatam hui toh woh donon chhat par aa gaye — oopar stare thay, aur neeche sheher ki roshanayan.

mhar ne kaha: "kiya aap ne kabhi socha tha — ke yeh din aaye ga?"

Aasim ne ise dekha: "nahin — kabhi nahin. mein ne mohabbat ko apane liay mumkin nahin samjha tha. magar aap ne mujhe dakhaya ke kuch chizin — jab hoti hain toh lagta hai ke woh hamesha se honi chahiye thin."

mhar is ke qareeb aa gai — aur is ne is ka haath thama:

"mein nahin jaanti ke aage kiya ho ga — magar mein jaanti hoon ke ab se — har jamaa, har baarish, har kaafi ka cup — aap ke b-ghair adhora ho ga."

Aasim ne ise apane qareeb kiya — aur is ne is ke kaan mein kaha:

"phir — hum kabhi adhore nahin hoon ge. kyunke mein aap ke saath hoon — har jamaa, har baarish, har kaafi ke cup mein."
