phir se baarish sharoa ho gai — aur is bar woh teen din tak raki nahin.

sheher paani mein doba hawa tha — glayan khali thin, dakanin jaldi band ho gai thin, aur kife mein sirf wahi do log thay — aur ba-ba krim, jo apane rikarad playaئar par purani dhanein b-jaa rahe thay.

mhar ne bahar dekhte huye kaha: "aap ko maloom hai — mein ne hamesha socha ke baarish kyun ghar jisi lagti hai?"

aasaíam ne kaafi ka cup athaya: "shayad is liay ke baarish woh waqt hota hai jab duniya rak jati hai — aur aap ko apane saath rhane ka moqa milta hai."

mhar ne ise dekha: "aap hamesha aise kyun bolate ho — jaise aap ne kisi kitab se jawab yaad kar liya ho?"

aasaíam hans pra: "shayad is liay ke mein ne brason kitabon se baatein ki hain — aur ab jab mein aap se baat kurta hoon toh woh kitabein majah se baatein karti hain."

---

is ne mhar ko apane bachapan ki ek yaad snaئi:

"jab mein chhota tha — baarish ke dinon mein, miri ami kachan mein chai banati thin aur mein khirki ke paas bith kar baarish dekhta tha. woh waqt — woh mire liay duniya ka sab se mhafoz waqt tha."

mhar ne kaha: "mire ghar mein bhi — baarish ke din ami humein khanayan snati thin. purani khanayan — shahazadon aur shahazadayawan ki, jinon aur dayawaؤan ki."

"aur ab — kiya aap khod ko khanayan snati ho?"

mhar ne ise dekha — aur is ki aankhon mein woh chamak thi jo ise khas banati thi:

"nahin — magar ab mujhe kisi aise shakhas ne mila hai jo khanayan nahin snata balke in mein rehta hai."

aasaíam ne kuch nahin kaha — is ki khamoshi hi is ka jawab thi.

---

baarish tez ho gai — aur phir bijli chamaki.

mhar zara si hal gai — aur aasaíam ne yeh mhasos kiya:

"dar lagta hai?"

"thoda — bachapan se bijli se dar lagta hai."

aasaíam ne apani kursi is ke qareeb kar li — aur is ne woh kaha jo is ne pehle kabhi kisi se nahin kaha tha:

"jab mein chhota tha — mein akile bijli ka samana krane se drata tha. magar mire abbu kehte thay ke drane ki zarorat nahin — kyunke bijli sirf roshni hai, aur roshni se drana koi baat nahin."

"magar ab — ab bhi dar lagta hai?"

"kabhi kabhi — magar ab jab aap mire saath ho toh dar kam ho jata hai."

mhar ne is ki traf dekha — aur is ne is ka haath tham liya. bahut halake se, jaise woh kha rahi ho: "mein bhi aap ke saath hoon."

---

woh shaam der tak kife mein rahe — baarish raki nahin, aur woh bhi nahin rake.

jab woh uthe toh bahar andhera tha — magar baarish rak chaki thi. hawa mein woh tazagi thi jo sirf baarish ke baad aati hai — aur sarak par paani ke talab chamak rahe thay.

mhar ne darwazay par rak kar kaha:

"aaj mein ne sikha ke baarish kyun ghar jisi lagti hai."

"kyun?"

"kyunke baarish woh waqt hota hai jab aap kisi ke saath khamosh bith sakte ho — aur phir bhi mhasos kar sakte ho ke aap akile nahin ho."

woh maskraئi aur chali gai — aur aasaíam wohein khara raha, is ke jaane ke baad bhi, baarish ki khushbu mein khawaya hawa.
