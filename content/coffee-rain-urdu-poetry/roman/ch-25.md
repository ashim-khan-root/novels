teesra jamaa — aur Aasim ab umeed nahin rakhta tha.

woh kife mein aaya toh is ke chehray par woh chamak nahin thi jo pehle hoti thi. woh ab wahan aanay ki waja nahin janta tha — magar phir bhi aata tha, jaise yeh is ki majabori ban chaki thi.

is ne darwaza khola — aur is bar is ne is kaunay ki traf nahin dekha jahan mhar bithati thi. woh sidha almari ke paas gaya — jaise woh ab sirf ek rasm ada kar raha ho.

is ne *klayatِ fiz* n-kali — aur ise khola.

asi safahe par is ka aakhari sawal tha — aur woh sawal ab bhi be jawab tha.

---

Aasim ne kitab ko dekha — aur ise laga jaise yeh kitab ab in ki khani ka hasa nahin rahi. yeh ab sirf ek purani kitab thi, jis mein kuch sherein thin aur kuch yaadein — magar woh yaadein ab khatam ho rahi thin.

woh apani jagah par bith gaya — is bar is ne kaafi nahin mangawaئi. woh sirf baitha raha, khali meez ko dekhta raha, aur sochata raha.

is ne socha — shayad woh kabhi nahin aaye gi. shayad woh dar gai, shayad is ne faisla kiya ke yeh sab ghalat tha, shayad woh kisi aur sheher chali gai.

aur shayad — shayad ise kuch ho gaya hai.

yeh aakhari khayal is ke dil ko tham line wala tha. is ne forً ise jahataka — magar woh khayal waps aata raha, bar bar, jaise koi lhar jo sahal se takrati hai aur phir lot aati hai.

---

is ne qalam n-kala — aur is bar is ne woh likha jo is ne kabhi kisi ko nahin bataya tha:

*"mhar —*

*min nahin janta ke aap yeh kab parhein gi — ya aap parhein gi bhi ya nahin. magar mujhe likhna hai. kyunke agar mein nahin lakhon ga toh mein khod ko kho don ga.*

*sna ke jaane ke baad — mein ne socha tha ke mohabbat mire liay nahin hai. mein ne apane aap ko band kar liya tha, apane jazbaat ko deewaron mein qaid kar diya tha. magar aap ne — aap ne b-ghair kisi koshash ke woh deewarein gra dein.*

*aap ne mujhe skhaya ke khamoshi bhi ek zaban hoti hai — aur mein ne aap ke saath khamosh rehna sikha. aap ne mujhe dakhaya ke baarish mein bhigana bhi ek abadat ho sakti hai — aur mein ne aap ke saath baarish mein khare rehna sikha.*

*magr ab — aap nahin hain. aur mein phir se is khamoshi mein kho gaya hoon jisay mein brason pehle chhor aaya tha.*

*min aap se sirf ek baat poochhon ga — aur is ke baad, agar aap jawab nahin dena chahin gi toh mein smajah jaon ga.*

*kaya aap theek hain?*

*saraf yeh batayiay — aur phir mein aap ko woh karon gi jo aap chahti hain. agar aap waps aana chahti hain toh mein yehein hoon. agar aap jaana chahti hain toh — mein aap ko jaane don ga.*

*magr pehle — batayiay ke aap theek hain.*

*aap k*
*aasim"*

---

is ne qalam rakha — aur is ke haath kanap rahe thay. is ne atana lamba khat kabhi nahin likha tha — na sna ko, na apani maan ko, na kisi ko.

is ne kaghaz ko kitab mein rakha — prachi ki tarah nahin, balke ek khat ki tarah, jor kar, jaise woh kisi qimati chiz ko snabhal raha ho.

phir is ne kitab band ki — aur ise almari mein rakh diya.

magar is bar — is ne ise hamesha ke liay rakh diya.

---

woh utha aur kife se nklane laga — magar ba-ba krim ne ise rok liya:

"jaa rahe ho, beta?"

"haan — aaj jaldi nikalon ga."

ba-ba krim ne ise dekha — aur in ki aankhon mein woh smajah thi jo sirf waladin ki hoti hai:

"kal phir ao ge?"

Aasim ne kandhay achakaئe — aur woh pehli bar jhoot bola:

"pata nahin — shayad."

magar woh janta tha — woh aaye ga. kyunke woh ab kife ke b-ghair nahin ra sakta tha, jaise woh mhar ke b-ghair nahin ra sakta tha.

---

woh ghar gaya toh raat thi — aur is ne apani diary mein nahin likha. woh sirf sofe par baitha raha, andhire mein, aur chhat ko dekhta raha.

is ne socha — agar woh kabhi waps nahin aai toh woh kiya kare ga?

aur is sawal ka jawab — is ke paas nahin tha.
