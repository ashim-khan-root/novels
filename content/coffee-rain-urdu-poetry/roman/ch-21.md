title: "Chapter 21 — qayda"

ab woh roz milte thay — magar kisi ne is mulaqaat ko nam nahin diya tha.

kiya woh dost thay? kiya woh kuch aur thay? kiya in ke dramayan jo kuch tha — ise ek nam dena zaroori tha?

ek din kife ki chhat par baithe huye — mhar ne woh sawal poocha jis ke bare mein woh donon soch rahe thay magar kha nahin paa rahe thay:

"hum kiya hain?"

aasaíam ne ise dekha — yeh sawal aasaan nahin tha:

"aap kiya chahti hain ke hum hoon?"

mhar ne kuch der socha — aur phir kaha: "mein nahin jaanti — mein sirf atana jaanti hoon ke jab aap nahin hote toh mujhe aap ki yaad aati hai. jab aap hinte hain toh mein mhafoz mhasos karti hoon. aur jab aap baat karte hain toh mein snana chahti hoon."

aasaíam maskraya: "yahi toh hai — yahi sab kuch hai. hum woh hain jo hum hain — aur ise ek nam dine ki zarorat nahin."

mhar ne ise dekha: "magar — kiya hum —"

"kiya?"

"kiya hum — ek doosray ke hain?"

---

aasaíam ne is ka haath thama — aur is ne is ki aankhon mein dekhte huye kaha:

"hum ek doosray ke hain — magar is tarah nahin jaise log samajhte hain. hum ek doosray ke is liay nahin hain ke hum ne koi maahada kiya hai — hum ek doosray ke is liay hain ke hum ne ek doosray ko chana hai."

mhar ne ise dekha: "toh phir — kiya yeh kaafi hai?"

"yeh sab se zyada hai — yeh is se bhi zyada hai jo zyada tr logon ko milta hai. kyunke hum ne alfaz se sharoaat ki — aur ab hamare paas woh sab kuch hai jo alfaz ke baad aata hai."

mhar khamosh ho gai — magar is ki khamoshi mein ataminan tha.

---

woh shaam ko kife se nikalay toh ba-ba krim ne unhein roka:

"sno — mein aap donon ko kal raat ek khas daot par blana chahta hoon."

"daot?" mhar ne hirani se poocha.

ba-ba krim maskraئe: "haan — is kife ki chaliswin salagra hai. aur mein chahta hoon ke aap donon wahan ho — kyunke aap is kife ki sab se khubsurat khani ho jo abhi tak likhi gai hai."

aasaíam aur mhar ne ek doosray ko dekha — aur woh maskraئe.

"hum aaein ge, ba-ba ji — zaroor aaein ge."

woh kife se nikalay — aur is bar, in ke dramayan woh be yaqini nahin thi jo pehle thi. unhon ne apane rashate ko nam nahin diya tha — magar shayad, kuch rashate namon se baray hote hain.
