---
title: "Chapter 12 — tanhai"
weight: 12
type: roman
---


ek hafta guzar gaya — aur mhar ki note bak Aasim ke paas rahi.

woh har raat ise parhta — ek sher, do sher, kabhi ek pori nazm. har lafz ise mhar ke qareeb le jata — is ki sochon mein, is ke dar mein, is ki tanhai mein.

ek shaam woh kife ki chhat par baithe thay — suraj dob raha tha, aur aasman narangi aur glabi rangon mein ranga hawa tha.

Aasim ne note bak waps ki — aur is ke chehray par woh snajidagi thi jo is ne pehle kabhi nahin dekhi thi:

"mein ne aap ki shayari parhi — sari."

mhar ne kitab li — is ki aankhon mein be chini thi:

"aur —"

"aur mein ne aap ko pehchan liya — jaise mein ne pehle kabhi nahin phachana tha."

mhar khamosh rahi — woh jawab ka antazar kar rahi thi.

Aasim ne kaha: "aap ki shayari mein tanhai hai — woh tanhai jisay mein brason se janta hoon. woh tanhai jo raat ko aati hai, jab ghar khamosh hota hai aur aap apane swa kisi ke bare mein nahin soch sakte."

---

mhar ne ise dekha — aur is ki aankhon mein nami thi:

"aap kaise jante ho?"

"kyunke — mein bhi wohein rehta hoon."

Aasim ne gehri saans li — aur is ne kehna sharoa kiya:

"jab sna gai toh mein ne socha ke yeh sirf ek brik ap hai — aap jante hain, log totate hain aur phir jorate hain. magar yeh woh nahin tha. woh jaane ke baad — mein ne mhasos kiya ke mein pehle bhi tanha tha. woh sirf woh shakhas thi jis ke samne mein tanha baithta tha."

mhar ne is ka haath thama:

"mein bhi — is rashate mein, mein ne socha ke mein kisi ke saath hoon — magar mein pehle se zyada tanha thi. kyunke jab aap kisi aise shakhas ke saath ho jo aap ko samajhta nahin — toh tanhai aur bhi gehri ho jati hai."

---

woh der tak khamosh baithe rahe — suraj dob chaka tha, aur ab andhera chaha raha tha. magar woh andhera khofanak nahin tha — kyunke woh is mein ek saath thay.

phir mhar ne kaha:

"aap jante hain sab se ajib baat kiya hai? — mein brason se tanha thi, magar mein ne kabhi kisi ko bataya nahin. kyunke mujhe lagta tha ke agar mein ne bataya toh log samjhein ge ke mein kamzor hoon."

"aur ab?"

"ab — mujhe lagta hai ke tanhai ko bantana kamazori nahin hai. shayad — yeh sab se bari taqat hai jo do log ek doosray ko de sakte hain."

Aasim ne is ki traf dekha — aur is ne kaha:

"aap jab se aai ho — mein ne mhasos kiya hai ke miri tanhai kam ho gai hai. yeh khatam nahin hui — magar yeh ab bojah nahin hai. yeh ek aisi chiz hai jisay mein aap ke saath baant sakta hoon."

mhar is ke kandhay par sar rakh diya — aur woh donon khamosh baithe rahe, andhire mein, staron ke neeche.

kabhi kabhi — tanhai ko bantana hi is ka sab se khubsurat hal hota hai.
