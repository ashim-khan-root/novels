jamaa ki subah thi — aur aasaíam pehle se kife mein tha.

is ne jaan bojah kar jaldi ki thi — woh mhar se pehle phanachana chahta tha, take ise aate dekh sake. yeh ek chhoti si khwahish thi, be zarar, magar is ke dil ke qareeb.

woh khirki ke paas baitha kaafi pi raha tha — jab darwaza khula aur woh aai.

mhar aaj makhatalaf lag rahi thi — is ne safed kurta phana tha, baal khulay thay, aur is ke haath mein *klayatِ fiz* thi — wahi kitab, wahi purani kitab jo in ki khani ki gawah thi.

is ne ise dekha toh maskraئi — aur woh muskurahat is ki pori subah badal gai.

---

woh is ke paas aa kar baithi — aur is ne kitab is ki traf brha di:

"dekho — mein ne kal raat is mein kuch likha."

aasaíam ne kitab kholi — aur ek naye safahe par, mhar ki tehreer mein, ek naya sher tha:

*"kuch log milte hain toh lagta he*

*jise woh hamesha se the*

*saraf hum unhein dhond nahin paئe the*

*kayawanka woh is waqt milte hin*

*jab hum khod ko dhond lete hain"*

aasaíam ne ise parha — aur is ne mhar ki traf dekha:

"yeh — aap ne likha?"

"haan — kal raat, jab mein so nahin saki toh mein ne yeh likha."

"ks bare mein?"

mhar ne ise dekha — aur is ke chehray par woh naram muskurahat thi jo sirf sach bolati hai:

"hamare bare mein."

---

woh is din kife mein nahin baithe — woh bahar nikal gaye.

sheher ki purani galion mein saath saath chalna ab in ki aadat ban chaki thi — har jamaa ko kife ke baad, woh kahein na kahein chale jate. kabhi kitabon ki purani dakan par, kabhi parak mein, kabhi b-ghair kisi manazl ke.

aaj woh ek purani masjid ke shan mein bith gaye — jahan ek bara sa bragad ka darakht tha aur neeche patthar ki banachin.

mhar ne kaha: "mein ne kabhi nahin socha tha ke mein kisi ke saath is tarah chalon gi — b-ghair kisi waja ke, sirf is liay ke mein is ke saath hoon."

aasaíam maskraya: "yahi toh sab se khubsurat waja hai — kisi ke saath hona, b-ghair kisi aur waja ke."

---

woh shaam ko kife waps aaye toh ba-ba krim ne in ke liay ek chhoti si meez par mom bati rakhi thi.

"yeh kiya hai, ba-ba ji?"

ba-ba krim maskraئe: "aaj jamaa hai — aur mein ne socha ke aap donon ko kuch khas chahiye."

mhar ne ba-ba krim ka haath thama: "aap ne hamare liay yeh sab kiya — humein kaise pata tha ke humein ek doosray ki zarorat hai?"

ba-ba krim ne donon ki traf dekha — aur is ki aankhon mein woh chamak thi jo chalees saal ki hakamat se aati hai:

"kyunke mein ne is kife mein mohabbat ko kaئi bar aate dekha hai — aur mein janta hoon ke jab do log kitabon ke dramayan milte hain, toh woh mulaqaat kabhi atafaqi nahin hoti."

woh chala gaya — aur woh donon mom bati ki roshni mein baithe rahe, kife ki gram roshni mein, ek doosray ke samne.

aur is raat, kisi ne kuch nahin kaha — magar sab kuch kha diya gaya.
