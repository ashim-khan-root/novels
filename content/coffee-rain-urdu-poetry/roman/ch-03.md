ab woh har roz kife nahin aata tha — kyunke ise dar tha ke agar woh bahut zyada aaya toh yeh khatam ho jaئe ga.

yeh ajib tha — woh kisi aise shakhas ke bare mein soch raha tha jisay is ne kabhi dekha nahin tha. magar woh is ke khayalon mein thi — jab woh jaagta, jab woh kaam kurta, jab woh sonay se pehle chhat ko dekhta.

is ne apane aap se kaha: yeh paagal pan hai.

magar phir ise woh sher yaad aa gaya — is ka jawab, is ki likhai, woh khubsurat haath jis ne alfaz ko aise sajaya tha jaise woh kisi thafe ko lapit rahi ho.

aur woh jaan gaya ke — shayad paagal pan hi sachi chizon ka aaghaz hota hai.

---

badh ko woh kife ki traf nikal khara hawa — is bar is ka koi bhana nahin tha. woh sirf yeh dekhna chahta tha ke wahan kaun hai.

is ne darwaza khola toh ghanti baji — purani pital ki ghanti jis ki aawaz ise ab pehchan lagti thi. kife mein teen log thay: ek jora ek kaunay mein baitha baatein kar raha tha, aur ek aurat kaؤantr par khari kaafi ka antazar kar rahi thi.

is ki amar pachis ke qareeb thi — siyah baal, aankhon par aink, aur is ke haath mein ek purani kitab thi. is ne sar athaya aur is ki traf dekha — ek lamha, do lamhe — aur phir woh maskraئi. ek chhoti, shaئasta muskurahat.

Aasim ne bhi maskra kar jawab diya — magar woh yaqeen nahin kar ska.

kiya woh thi?

is ka dil zor se dharaka. woh kaؤantr ke paas gaya — is aurat ke balakl paas — aur is ne ba-ba krim se kaafi ka aaradr diya. is aurat ne apani kaafi li aur ek traf bith gai — is ne ek note bak n-kali aur likhna sharoa kar diya.

Aasim apani jagah par bith gaya — woh ise dekhe b-ghair nahin dekh sakta tha. woh lakh rahi thi — kuch aisi toja se ke ise apane aas paas ka hosh nahin tha.

---

aadha ghanta guzar gaya. woh aurat uthi, apani kaafi khatam ki, aur darwazay ki traf brhi. jate jate is ne ek bar peeche mar kar dekha — aur is ki nazar Aasim se jaa mili.

is bar woh maskraئi nahin — sirf dekha, ek lamha, aur phir chali gai.

Aasim khali kursi ko dekhta ra gaya.

woh utha aur almari ke paas gaya — is ne *klayatِ fiz* n-kali aur ise khola. is safahe par jahan is ne aakhari sher likha tha, ab koi nayi baat nahin thi.

magar ek prachi thi — kitab ke dramayan mein, b-ghair kisi nishaan ke. is ne ise n-kala aur parha:

*"mein ne socha tha ke aap jamaa ko ao ge — magar aap badh ko aa gaye. kiya aap bhi antazar nahin kar sakte?"*

---

Aasim ne prachi ko dobara parha — aur is ke haath kanap rahe thay.

woh jaan gaya tha — woh aurat wahi thi. wahi aurat jo kaؤantr par khari thi, wahi jis ne ise dekh kar maskraya tha, wahi jo is kitab mein sher lakhati thi.

magar is ne ise pehchan nahin ska tha — aur is ne ise bataya bhi nahin tha.

is ne prachi ko jib mein rakha aur apani jagah par waps aa kar bith gaya. is ne qalam n-kala aur kitab mein likha:

*"mein antazar nahin kar ska — aur lagta hai aap bhi nahin kar skin. shayad hum donon ek hi chiz ke mantazr hain."*

is ne kitab band ki aur ise almari mein rakh diya. magar is bar — woh kife se nahin nkla. woh baitha raha, kaafi pita raha, aur kitab ko dekhta raha.

woh jaanna chahta tha ke woh kab aaye gi — magar woh nahin aaya.

shayad is liay ke woh drata tha — agar woh ise dekh le ga toh yeh sab khatam ho jaئe ga. shayad yeh behtar tha, yeh parda, yeh fasla, yeh khamoshi jo alfaz se bhari hui thi.

woh shaam ko ghar aaya toh is ne apani diary mein likha:

*"aaj mein ne ise dekha — aur mein ne ise phachana nahin. magar jab mein ne ise dekha toh mujhe laga jaise mein ise brason se janta hoon. shayad mohabbat pehli nazar mein nahin hoti — shayad mohabbat pehle lafz se hoti hai, aur nazar toh sirf is baat ki tsadiq hoti hai ke dil pehle hi faisla kar chaka tha."*
