jamaa ko baarish ho rahi thi — is bar pehle se kahein zyada tez.

aasaíam kife mein phanacha toh is ke kapre bhig chake thay. is ne apana coat utaara aur ise kursi par lataka diya — paani tapak raha tha, farsh par ek chhota talab bana hawa tha.

mhar pehle se mojod thi — woh ise dekh kar hansi:

"aap pori tarah bhig gaye — kiya aap ke paas chahatri nahin?"

"hai — magar baarish atani tez thi ke chahatri bikar thi."

is ne apani jagah par baithte huye kaha — aur mhar ne ise apana romal diya. is ne ise liya aur apane chehray se paani ponachaha — romal mein is ki khushbu thi, halki phoolon wali, bahut naram.

"shukriya —"

"khush aamadid —"

"mein bhi —" is ne kehna sharoa kiya — magar phir rak gaya.

"mein bhi kisi ko kho chaka hoon — magar is tarah nahin jaise aap ne khawaya. miri mangitr thi — sna."

"ab — mein sikh raha hoon. aap ke saath — mein sikh raha hoon ke khamoshi zaroori hai, magar alfaz bhi zaroori hain."

baarish tez ho gai — aur phir aahasta aahasta rakane lagi.

woh donon khirki ke paas khare thay — bahar sarak par paani bha raha tha, aur har traf woh tazagi thi jo sirf baarish ke baad aati hai.

mhar ne kaha: "baarish ke baad sab kuch saaf kaise ho jata hai — jaise duniya nayi ho gai ho."

aasaíam ne is ki traf dekha: "shayad is liay ke baarish woh kar dakhati hai jo hum nahin kar sakte — woh purani chizon ko dho deti hai aur nayi chizon ki jagah banati hai."

mhar ne ise dekha — aur is ki aankhon mein kuch tha jo is ne pehle nahin dekha tha:

"shayad — humein bhi baarish ki tarah hona chahiye."

woh donon khamosh khare rahe — bahar baarish rak chaki thi, magar in ke dramayan kuch sharoa ho raha tha.
