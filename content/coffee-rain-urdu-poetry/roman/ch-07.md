title: "Chapter 7 — jawab mein sawal"
weight: 7

---


woh hafta ko bhi aai — aur atawar ko bhi.

kife mein bithane ki aadat ab ek rasm ban chaki thi — woh b-ghair kisi wade ke aate, aur ek doosray ko wahan pate. ba-ba krim ne in ke liay wahi kona makhasos kar diya tha — gawaya woh jante thay ke yeh jagah ab in ki hai.

magar satawin din — mhar nahin aai.

Aasim ne is ka antazar kiya. ek ghanta, do ghante — magar woh nahin aai. is ne phone n-kala — is ke paas is ka nambr nahin tha. ise ehsaas hawa ke woh ab tak ek doosray ko sirf kife mein jante hain — bahar ki duniya mein in ka koi wajod nahin.

woh ghar aaya toh is ke zhan mein hazaar sawal thay — kiya woh theek hai? kiya is ne aanay ka arada badal liya? kiya woh dar gai?

is ne apani diary mein likha — magar alfaz adhore ra gaye.

---

agle din — woh kife mein thi.

Aasim ne darwazay se dakhal hote hi ise dekha — woh wohein thi, asi kaunay mein, magar is ke chehray par woh chamak nahin thi jo pehle thi. woh thaki hui lag rahi thi — jaise woh raat bhar jaagti rahi ho.

woh is ke paas bith gaya — aur is ne pehle kuch nahin poocha. woh sirf baitha raha, is ke saath, khamoshi se.

das minute baad mhar ne kaha — bahut aahasta se:

"mein kal nahin aa saki — kyunke mein dar gai thi."

Aasim ne ise dekha — is ne kuch nahin kaha. ise pata tha ke ab ise bolane dena hai.

"mein ne socha — ke shayad yeh bahut jaldi hai. shayad hum bahut tizi se aage brh rahe hain. aur mein — mein is se pehle bhi yahan aa chaki hoon. mein ne socha ke koi mujhe samajhta hai — aur phir woh chala gaya."

is ki aawaz kanap rahi thi — magar woh raki nahin:

"mein drati hoon, Aasim — is liay nahin ke aap kuch ghalat karo ge, balke is liay ke aap shih ho. aur mein jaanti hoon ke jab koi shih hota hai — toh ise khona aur bhi takleef da hota hai."

---

Aasim ne is ki baatein snin — aur is ne apane andar wahi dar paya jo is ke andar tha.

"mein bhi drata hoon, mhar — shayad is se bhi zyada jatana aap soch sakti hain. magar jab mein aap ko dekhta hoon — jab mein aap ke saath baithta hoon — toh woh dar kam ho jata hai."

is ne apana haath meez par rakha — is ke qareeb, magar ise chhuay b-ghair:

"mein aap se koi wada nahin kar sakta — kyunke wade tot jate hain. magar mein atana kha sakta hoon — jab tak aap yahan baithna chaaho gi, mein aap ke samne bithon ga. jab tak aap baat karna chaaho gi, mein snon ga. aur jab tak aap khamosh rehna chaaho gi — mein aap ke saath khamosh rhon ga."

mhar ne ise dekha — aur is ki aankhon mein aansoo thay, magar woh muskurahat bhi thi.

"aap jaan bojah kar aise kyun ho?"

"kaise?"

"atane — naram."

Aasim ne kandhay achakaئe: "shayad is liay ke mein janta hoon ke naram hone mein bhi taqat hoti hai — aur shayad is liay ke aap majah se woh nkal leti ho jo mein ne socha tha ke khatam ho chaka hai."

---

woh shaam der tak kife mein rahe — aur jab woh uthe toh bahar andhera tha.

ba-ba krim ne unhein jate dekha — aur unhon ne kuch nahin kaha, magar in ki khamoshi mein ek dua thi.

darwazay par mhar ne rak kar kaha:

"kal jamaa hai — aur mein aaon gi. kisi dar ke b-ghair."

Aasim maskraya: "mein aap ka antazar karon ga — jaise hamesha kurta hoon."

woh alag huye — aur is bar, in ke dramayan woh khala nahin tha jo pehle tha. woh khala ab alfaz se bhar chaka tha — aur shayad kuch aur bhi.
