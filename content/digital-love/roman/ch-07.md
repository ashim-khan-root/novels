---

raat do bajay thay jab zara ka paigham aaya.

*"kiya tum kabhi sochate ho ke hum kyun aise hain jaise hain?"*

aayan ne yeh sawal parha aur jawab dine se pehle kaafi der socha.

*"har roz."*

*"toh phir batao — tum kyun ho jaise ho?"*

woh khamosh raha. is ne kabhi kisi ko yeh jawab nahin diya tha. lekin aaj raat kuch makhatalaf tha. shayad is liay ke woh ise nahin jaanti thi — aur shayad asi liay woh ise bata sakta tha.

*"kyunke jab mein chaudah saal ka tha, mire walad ka antaqal ho gaya. is ke baad jo log 'hamesha' saath rhane ka wada karte thay, woh chand mhinon mein ghaئab ho gaye. is din mujhe pata chala ke log tum se nahin, tamhari hisit se mohabbat karte hain."*

is ne paigham bhija aur forً pachahata gaya. bahut zyada kha diya tha. lekin paigham waps nahin aata.

phir is ka phone roshan hawa.

*"mire walad ne mujhe is waqt chhora jab mein aath saal ki thi. ek din subah uthi toh woh nahin thay. darwazay par in ke jootay nahin thay. mein ghanton darwazay ke paas baithi antazar karti rahi — woh kabhi waps nahin aaye. tab se mein ne kisi ka antazar nahin kiya."*

aayan ne yeh parha aur is ka dil bith gaya.

woh donon ek jaise thay. donon ko chhor diya gaya tha. donon ne apane aap ko bachane ke liay deewarein bana li thin.

lekin aaj raat woh deewarein gr rahi thin.

---

woh subah tak baat karte rahe.

unhon ne apane khwabon ke bare mein bataya — woh khwab jo unhon ne kabhi kisi ko nahin bataye thay. zara ne bataya ke woh bachapan mein ek lakhari banana chahti thi, magar walad ke jaane ke baad is ne woh khwab dafan kar diya. aayan ne bataya ke woh apane walad ki tarah ustad banana chahta tha, magar taqadir ne ise lakhari bana diya.

fajar ki azan hui toh unhein ehsaas hawa ke pori raat guzar gai thi.

zara ne likha:

*"mein ne yeh sab kabhi kisi ko nahin bataya."*

*"mein ne bhi nahin."*

*"tum jante ho, mein tamhara nam nahin jaanti. tamhara chehra nahin jaanti. lekin tum woh shakhas ho jis se mein ne apani zindagi ki sab se gehri baat shayaئar ki hai."*

aayan ne yeh parha aur is ki aankhon mein nami thi.

*"shayad asi liay hum ek doosray ko bata sake — kyunke hum ek doosray ko nahin jante."*

traf se jawab nahin aaya. lekin khamoshi bol rahi thi.

woh donon jante thay ke ab kuch badal gaya hai. ab yeh sirf ek bhas nahin rahi thi. yeh kuch aur tha — kuch ghera, kuch khofanak, kuch khubsurat.
