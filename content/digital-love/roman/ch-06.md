---

daftar mein subah ki meeting sharoa ho chaki thi.

zara ne apani prizantishan ke kaghazaat snabhale aur khari ho gai. woh praatamad thi — is ne raat bhar jaag kar yeh plan tayar kiya tha. aaj woh sabat kar de gi ke woh is nokri ke laئaq hai.

"toh jisa ke mein ne kaha — raz ki nayi kitab ki marakitang ke liay hum ek prasarar mham chalaئin ge. masnaf ki shanakhat ko hi hamari sab se bari marakitang astritaji banaya jaئe ga."

kamray mein asabat mein sar hale.

phir ek aawaz aai — praskon, be nayaz.

"yeh kaam nahin kare ga."

sab ne aayan ki traf dekha. woh apani sit par peeche jahaka baitha tha, aankhein zara par thin.

zara ne ise dekha. "kyun?"

"kyunke tum jis chiz ko bich rahi ho, woh raz ki shanakhat nahin, is ki tehreer hai. agar tum is ki shanakhat ko marakitang ka hathayar banaؤ gi, toh kitab khod kho jaئe gi."

zara ki aankhon mein changarayan thin. "aur tumhein kiya lagta hai ke log raz ko kyun prhate hain? is ki tehreer ki waja se? ya is liay ke woh ek asarar hai?"

"donon."

"toh phir asarar ko torane se kiya fraq prata hai?"

aayan khamosh raha. is ke jabre mein tanaؤ tha. aakhar bolay b-ghair na ra ska:

"kyunke asarar totane ke baad sirf ek insaan ra jata hai. aur insaan ghalatayan karte hain. kiya tum is ke liay tayar ho?"

kamray mein khamoshi chaha gai.

zara ne ise dekha. is ke lhaje mein kuch tha — koi zati dard, koi ghera tajaraba. woh sirf ek aiditoril kanaslatant ki tarah baat nahin kar raha tha. woh kisi aise shakhas ki tarah baat kar raha tha jis ne yeh sab pehle dekha ho.

"tum raz ko jante ho, hai na?"

aayan ne jawab nahin diya. woh utha aur kamray se bahar chala gaya.

zara ne ise jate dekha aur is ke dil mein shak ki ek lhar dor gai.

---

asi raat woh apane gamanam dost se baat kar rahi thi.

*"aaj daftar mein bahut bura din tha."*

*"kyun?"*

*"ek aiditr hai — bahut magharor. har meeting mein miri baat katata hai. jaise ise sab kuch pata ho."*

*"lagta hai bara jar hai."*

zara hans di.

*"haan. aur sab se ajib baat yeh hai ke woh raz ke bare mein kuch janta hai — lekin bataata nahin."*

*"shayad woh raz hai?"*

zara ne yeh mazaq parha aur aankhein ghama dein.

*"bahut mazhaka khiz. raz koi adhir amar ka aadmi hai jo ghar se bahar nklata bhi hoga ya nahin. aur yeh larka shayad pachis saal ka hai."*

*"tum ne is ki amar kaise andaza lagaya?"*

*"... pata nahin. bas andaza."*

lekin woh jaanti thi ke yeh sirf andaza nahin tha. is ne aayan ko dekha tha — is ki aankhein, is ke haath, is ke lakhane ka andaz. aur ise laga tha ke woh ise kahein dekha hai.

lekin woh yeh tslim nahin karna chahti thi.

---

aayan is raat so nahin ska.

woh apane kamray mein thal raha tha, haath mein phone tha. zara se is ki baat hui thi — is ne is ke daftar wale aiditr ke bare mein shakait ki thi. is ne likha tha *"lagta hai bara jar hai"*. aur woh khod hi woh jar tha.

woh hans pra. ktana ajib tha yeh sab.

din mein woh is ka dushman tha. raat mein woh is ka dost tha. aur woh donon ko ek saath nabha raha tha.

lekin kab tak?

is ne apana phone neeche rakha aur khirki se bahar dekha. sheher ki roshanayan daur tamatama rahi thin. woh soch raha tha — agar woh ise sach bata de toh kiya hoga?

"mein wahi hoon jis se tum nafrat karti ho. mein raz hoon."

lekin woh janta tha ke yeh sach is ke chehray par tamanacha hoga. aur woh ise kho de ga — din ka dushman bhi, raat ka dost bhi.

is liay woh khamosh raha.

lekin khamoshi bhi ek jhoot thi. aur jhoot ki deewar kabhi na kabhi grati hai.
