title: "b-ab 15 — khamoshi"
weight: 15
type: roman

---

teen mhine guzar gaye.

aayan ne apane bare mein koi wazahat nahin di. is ne koi antrawayo nahin liya. koi post nahin ki. koi bayan jari nahin kiya. woh khamosh tha. makamal tore par khamosh.

log bholane lage thay. naya askindal aaya, nayi khabar aai, aur raz ka askindal purani ho gai. lekin zara nahin bholi thi.

woh apane kamray mein is ki purani kitabein parhti thi — ab ise pata tha ke yeh aayan ne likhi thin. har jumla, har lafz, har khayal — woh sab aayan tha. aur woh in kitabon mein ise dhondati thi, jaise woh kahein alfaz ke peeche chahapa ho.

ise ghussa aata tha — is par ghussa ke is ne jhoot bola. aur khod par ghussa ke woh ise bhol nahin paa rahi thi.

ek raat is ne apana purana phone n-kala — woh jisay is ne tore diya tha. is ne ise aan kiya aur is ki purani baatein parhein. woh raatein jab woh subah tak baat karte thay. woh hansi. woh khamoshayan.

is ne ek paigham parha jo is ne ise bhija tha:

*"tum jante ho, mein tamhara nam nahin jaanti. tamhara chehra nahin jaanti. lekin tum woh shakhas ho jis se mein ne apani zindagi ki sab se gehri baat shayaئar ki hai."*

aur is ka jawab:

*"shayad asi liay hum ek doosray ko bata sake — kyunke hum ek doosray ko nahin jante."*

zara ne phone band kar diya aur aankhein band kar lein.

woh ab ise jaanti thi. aur yeh pehle se zyada takleef da tha.

---

aayan ne teen mhinon mein kuch nahin likha tha.

is ka lip tap band pra tha. is ki note bak khali thi. alfaz is ka saath chhor gaye thay.

hasn aata tha, ise khane par majabor kurta tha, kabhi kabhi ise bahar le jata tha. lekin aayan khamosh rehta tha.

"tumhein kuch kehna chahiye," hasn ne ek din kaha.

"kiya keon?"

"kuch bhi. wazahat. maafi. sach."

"woh nahin snana chahti."

"tumhein kaise pata?"

aayan ne jawab nahin diya. lekin woh janta tha — is ne ise har jagah blak kar rakha tha. is ke paas is tak phanachane ka koi rasta nahin tha.

phir ek raat ise khayal aaya.

is ne apana lip tap khola aur likhna sharoa kiya.

yeh koi antrawayo nahin tha. koi wazahat nahin thi. yeh ek khani thi — in ki khani. jaise is ne pehle kabhi nahin likhi thi. sachi. be naqab. b-ghair kisi dhaal ke.

is ne likhna sharoa kiya.

**bab panazadham khatam**
