woh paigham bhijate hi aayan ko laga jaise is ne ghalati kar di.

is ne apana phone neeche rakha aur donon haathon se apana chehra dhanap liya. "yeh mein ne kiya kar diya?" woh soch raha tha. ek gamanam masnaf, jo khod chahap kar likhta hai, ab ek gamanam naqad ko chilanaj kar raha hai?

lekin paigham jaa chaka tha. aur waps aanay wala nahin tha.

is ne antazar kiya. ek minute. do minute. das minute.

phir screen roshan hui.

*"sabat karein."*

aayan ne yeh lafz parha aur is ke chehray par muskurahat aa gai. woh jaane kyun khush tha. shayad is liay ke ise ek chilanaj mila tha — ek aisa shakhas jo is ki kitab ko sirf prh kar nahin, balke ise smajah kar tanqeed kar raha tha.

*"sabat krane ke liay humein pehle baat krani hogi. aap ka nam?"*

jawab tizi se aaya:

*"naqad. aap ka?"*

aayan maskraya.

*"ek qari."*

zara ne woh jawab parha aur is ki aankhon mein roshni aa gai. ise yeh gamanam mbahasa psnd aanay laga tha. koi is se drata nahin tha. koi ise khush krane ki koshash nahin kar raha tha. sirf do log — do na-maloom — aur alfaz ki larai.

*"ek qari jisay lagta hai ke woh raz ko samajhta hai."*

*"kiya aap ko nahin lagta?"*

*"mujhe lagta hai ke raz khod ko bhi nahin samajhta. woh sirf likhta hai — kyunke likhna is ka bhis hai."*

*"aur aap? aap bhis mein kyun hain?"*

traf se koi jawab nahin aaya.

lekin khamoshi bhi ek jawab thi.

---

is din ke baad se woh har raat baat krane lage.

kabhi kisi kitab par bhas hoti. kabhi kisi flasafe par. kabhi sirf khamoshi se ek doosray ki mojodagi mhasos karte. unhon ne kabhi nam nahin poocha. kabhi chehra nahin manga. sirf alfaz thay — aur alfaz kaafi thay.

lekin ek raat, jab in ki bhas apani antha par thi, zara ne woh sawal poochh hi liya jis se woh donon bach rahe thay.

*"tum raz se atana pyar kyun karte ho?"*

aayan ne yeh sawal parha aur lamba saans liya.

baqadr kaafi der sochane ke baad is ne jawab likha — sirf ek jumla jis ne zara ko khamosh kar diya:

*"kyunke woh woh likhta hai jo mein kha nahin sakta."*

zara ne yeh jumla parha aur is ka dil bith gaya.

woh bhi yahi mhasos karti thi. har bar jab woh raz ki kitab parhti thi, ise lagta tha jaise koi is ke andar ki baat kha raha hai. aur yahi waja thi ke woh is se atani nafrat karti thi — kyunke woh ise woh dakha deta tha jo woh khod se chahapa rahi thi.

is ne jawab nahin diya.

lekin is ki khamoshi ne sab kha diya.
