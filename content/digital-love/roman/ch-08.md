---

is raat ke baad woh badal chake thay.

ab in ki baatein pehle jisi nahin thin. pehle woh bhas karte thay, larate thay, ek doosray ko ghalat sabat krane ki koshash karte thay. ab woh khamoshi se snte thay. ab woh sawal poochte thay, aur jawab ka antazar karte thay.

ab woh ek doosray ko jante thay.

aur yeh in donon ke liay khofanak tha.

---

chaar din baad zara ne woh paigham bhija jis ke bare mein woh kaafi der se soch rahi thi:

*"humein ek wada karna chahiye."*

*"kisa wada?"*

*"hum kabhi nahin poochein ge ke doosra kaun hai."*

aayan ne yeh parha aur is ka haath rak gaya.

yeh wahi tha jo woh chahta tha. yeh is ke liay bhatrin sorat hal thi — woh kabhi nahin jaan paئe gi ke woh raz hai, aur woh ise kabhi nahin khawaئe ga.

lekin phir ise ehsaas hawa — yeh wada drasl ek jhoot tha. kyunke woh pehle hi jaan chaka tha ke woh kaun hai. woh ise har roz daftar mein dekhta tha. woh is ki aawaz snta tha, is ki hansi, is ki khamoshi.

aur woh ise bata nahin sakta tha.

is ne jawab likha:

*"tumhein yaqeen hai? kabhi nahin?"*

*"kabhi nahin."*

*"theek hai. wada."*

is ne paigham bhija aur phone neeche rakh diya. ise laga jaise is ne koi bhaari chiz utha rakhi thi aur ab ise neeche rakh diya hai. lekin saath hi ise yeh bhi laga ke yeh rahat jhooti thi — kyunke woh janta tha ke sach kabhi na kabhi bahar aaye ga.

sawal sirf yeh tha — kab?

---

is raat woh der tak jaagta raha. is ne apana phone athaya, zara ke saath in tamam baaton ko parha jo unhon ne pichlay dinon mein ki thin.

phir is ne woh taئap kiya jo woh kaafi din se likhna chahta tha:

*"agar mein tum se jhoot bolon toh kiya tum mujhe maaf karo gi?"*

is ne apani ungli "bhejein" batan par rakhi.

phir ise yaad aaya — wada. unhon ne ek doosray se jhoot na bolane ka wada nahin kiya tha. lekin kuch wade alfaz se nahin hote.

is ne paigham delete kar diya.

lekin sawal is ke zhan mein ra gaya. aur is ka jawab ise bahut jalad milna tha.
