---

pablashar ne ise dhoka diya.

aayan ko pata bhi nahin tha ke woh ks tarah lik hawa. ek subah jab is ne aankhein kholin, is ka phone notifikishanaz se bhara hawa tha. hazaaron misijaz. kalin. ai milaz.

is ne ek lank khola — aur is ka chehra dekha.

*"raz ka asli chehra samne aa gaya — aayan hidr, gamanam masnaf ki haqiqat."*

is ki tswir thi — woh tswir jo is ne kabhi kisi ko nahin di thi. is ka nam. is ki amar. is ki zindagi ki khani.

aur sab se neeche likha tha:

*"khabaron ke matabaq, aayan hidr wahi shakhas hain jo brason se raz ke nam se lakh rahe thay. woh asi pablashang haؤas mein aiditoril kanaslatant ke tore par kaam karte hain jahan in ki apani kitabein shaئa hoti hain."*

aayan ne phone neeche rakha. is ke haath kanap rahe thay.

woh janta tha ke yeh din aaye ga. lekin woh is ke liay tayar nahin tha.

---

woh ghar se nahin nkla. teen din.

phone band kar diya tha. darwazay band thay. parday gِre huye thay. woh andhire mein leta raha, chhat ko ghorata raha, aur sochata raha ke sab kuch kahan ghalat ho gaya.

log bahar is ke bare mein baat kar rahe thay. kuch kha rahe thay ke woh dhoka baz hai. kuch kha rahe thay ke woh bhadr hai. kuch kha rahe thay ke unhein pata hi tha.

lekin woh kuch nahin sn sakta tha. is ne apane kaanon ko band kar liya tha.

sirf ek shakhas tha jis ke bare mein woh soch raha tha — zara.

woh ab jaan chaki hogi.
ise sab pata chal gaya hoga.
aur woh is se kabhi baat nahin kare gi.
