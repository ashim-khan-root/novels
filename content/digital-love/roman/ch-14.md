---

zara ne khabar is waqt dekhi jab woh apane kamray mein thi.

is ka phone achank roshan hawa — ek nayawaz alarat. is ne ise khola aur parha. phir dobara parha. phir tswir ko ghora.

aayan hidr.

raz.

ek hi shakhas.

wahi shakhas jis se woh har roz larati thi. wahi jis ne ise kaafi di thi. wahi jis ke saath woh lafat mein khari hui thi aur is ki aankhon mein kuch dekha tha jo woh smajah nahin paai thi.

wahi shakhas is ka gamanam dost tha.

wahi jis ne ise apane walad ki maut ke bare mein bataya tha. wahi jis ne ise bataya tha ke log sirf hisit se mohabbat karte hain. wahi jis ke saath is ne wada kiya tha ke woh kabhi asli nam nahin poochein ge.

aur woh jaan bojah kar is se jhoot bol raha tha.

har roz. har raat. har paigham.

woh ise dekhta tha, ise janta tha, aur kuch nahin kehta tha.

is ne apana phone athaya aur ise deewar ki traf phink diya.

sheesha tot gaya. screen bakhar gai. lekin is ke andar ka dukh nahin tota.

---

woh ise blak krane bith gai.

pehle is ka asli nambr — aayan hidr. phir is ka gamanam akaؤant — woh akaؤant jis se woh raaton ko baatein karti thi. phir is ka anastagram. is ka wats aip. har woh rasta jo ise is se jorata tha.

woh blak karti gai. aur har blak ke saath woh khod ko bata rahi thi — *tam shih kar rahi ho. is ne tamhare saath dhoka kiya. is ne tum se jhoot bola.*

lekin jab sab kuch blak ho gaya, toh woh khali haath ra gai.

woh kamray mein baithi rahi, is ka tota hawa phone is ke haath mein tha, aur ise laga jaise is ne na sirf ise, balke khod ko bhi kho diya hai.

kyunke woh shakhas jis se woh sab se zyada nafrat karti thi — wahi woh shakhas tha jis se woh sab se zyada pyar karti thi.

aur woh nahin jaanti thi ke is ke saath kiya karna hai.

---

**bab chaharadham khatam**
