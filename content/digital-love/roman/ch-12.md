---

aayan ke liay agle din bahut bhaari thay.

har subah woh daftar jata, zara ko dekhta — aur is ki aankhon mein woh sawal dekhta jis ka jawab woh nahin de sakta tha. woh ab is se baat nahin karti thi. mitangaz mein woh is ki traf dekhti bhi nahin thi. lekin jab bhi woh kamray mein aata, is ka jism akr jata tha.

woh is se daur bhag rahi thi. aur woh ise rok nahin sakta tha.

ek shaam jab sab daftar jaa chake thay, woh apani sit par baitha raha. is ne apana phone n-kala aur zara ke saath apani purani baatein parhein — woh raatein jab woh subah tak baat karte thay, woh hansi, woh khamoshayan.

phir is ne ek paigham taئap kiya.

*"mein tum se kuch chahapa raha hoon. aur yeh chahapana mujhe kha raha hai. tum majah se naraz ho, aur tumhein haq hai. lekin agar tum mujhe ek moqa do toh mein sab bata sakta hoon. sirf ek bar mal loo."*

is ne apani ungli "bhejein" batan par rakhi.

phir is ne socha — agar ise pata chal gaya toh woh ise kho de ga. aur agar ise nahin bataya toh woh pehle hi ise kho chaka hai.

is ne bhej diya.

lekin koi jawab nahin aaya. na is raat, na agle din, na is ke baad.

---

woh teen din tak antazar kurta raha. na koi jawab, na koi nashani. woh daftar mein ise dekhta, lekin woh is ki traf dekhti tak nahin thi. is ne apane gamanam akaؤant se bhi message kiya — lekin wahan bhi khamoshi thi.

woh ise donon jagahon par kho chaka tha.

teesray din is ne mhasos kiya ke woh ab lakh nahin paa raha. alfaz is ka saath chhor gaye thay. is ne apane lip tap par ek naya dakayawamant khola — khali safha. woh ise ghorata raha, lekin ek lafz bhi nahin lakh ska.

is ki khamoshi is ki tehreer ko bhi khamosh kar rahi thi.
