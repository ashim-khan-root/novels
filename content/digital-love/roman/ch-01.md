screen ki neeli roshni mein is ka chehra be nor lag raha tha.

aayan hidr ne aankhein jahapakin aur ek bar phir woh nambr parha — *#1 Best Seller*.

teen maa ki mhant, saat drafat, pandrah bar nazarasani. aur ab yeh. ise lagta tha ke jab yeh din aaye ga toh woh chalaئe ga, chahalang lagaئe ga, kuch aisa kare ga jo aam log karte hain.

lekin woh sirf dekhta ra gaya.

kamra khamosh tha. atane mein pankhe ki aawaz thi, aur bahar kahein daur ek gaari ke haran ki bazagashat. magar koi aawaz nahin thi jo kahe — "wah, kiya baat hai!" koi haath nahin tha jo is ki kamar par thapaki de.

woh apane aparatamant mein akela baitha tha, aur kamayabi ka zaiqa namakin tha aur is ka koi hum zaiqa nahin tha.

is ne apana phone athaya. anastagram khola. hazaaron notifikishanaz. tabasare. tareefein. dil. lekin woh sab ek nam par jaa rahe thay jis ka is ke chehray se koi talaq nahin tha.

**raz.**

woh nam jisay is ne khod chana tha. is waqt socha tha ke yeh ek dhaal hoga — ise b-chai ga. lekin ab woh khod is dhaal ke peeche qaid tha.

is ne apane akaؤant se ek astori daali. sirf do lafz:

*shakria.*

koi tswir nahin. koi chehra nahin. bas do lafz. aur das minute mein das hazaar wayawaz.

log is se mohabbat karte thay. is ki tehreer se, is ke khayalaat se, is ke alfaz se. lekin woh khod aayan hidr nami is shakhas ko nahin jante thay jo is waqt apane ghar mein akela baitha chai ki payali ko ghor raha tha, soch raha tha ke yeh kamayabi atani khali kyun hai.

---

subah teen bajay thay jab is ke phone ki ghanti baji.

is ne b-ghair dekhe athaya. sirf ek shakhas atani raat ko kal kar sakta tha.

"mubarak ho, raqam!"

hasn ki aawaz mein neend thi aur josh bhi. aayan maskraya. kam az kam ek shakhas toh tha jis ne ise is ke asli nam se pakara.

"pata hai ktani bajay hain?"

"pata hai aur mujhe prawaa nahin. baithe baithe socha ke mira dost jo ab nambr wan hai, ise kaun mbarakabad de ga agar mein na don?"

aayan khamosh raha. hasn ise achi tarah janta tha. woh janta tha ke yeh khamoshi kiya kha rahi hai.

"kiya hawa?"

"kuch nahin."

"aayan."

"wahi hai yar. soch raha hoon… jab yeh din aaye ga toh mein khush hoon ga. lekin hoon nahin."

hasn ne lambi saans li. "tum jante ho kyun nahin ho?"

"batao."

"kyunke tum ne koi aisi chiz paa li hai jo banti nahin jaa sakti. tum kamayab ho, lekin tamhari kamayabi ka koi gawah nahin. phir woh kamayabi kaise haqiqi ho sakti hai?"

aayan ne chai ka aakhari ghont liya. woh thandi ho chaki thi.

"tum kab se flasafi ho gaye?"

"is lamhe se jab mein ne apane bist frind ko atana bara lakhari bante dekha ke woh khod se drane laga."

aayan kuch nahin bola.

"tumhein apani shanakhat zahar krani chahiye, aayan."

"hum ne yeh bhas hazaar bar ki hai."

"aur mein hazaar bar wahi keon ga. tum atane baray lakhari ho, lekin tum khod nahin lakh sakte. tum sirf raz hain. kiya tumhein nahin lagta ke tum ne is nam ko apani zindagi se bara bana liya hai?"

aayan ne jawab nahin diya.

---

kal khatam hui toh aayan ne apani aankhein band kin. woh janta tha ke hasn shih kha raha hai. lekin ise yeh bhi pata tha ke haqiqat batane ki qimat kiya ho sakti hai.

is ne apani pehli kitab ka pablashar yaad kiya, jis ne is se kaha tha: *"tamhara chehra nahin, tamhari tehreer bichi jaئe gi."* yeh is waqt ek thafa laga tha. ab yeh ek jail thi.

is ne mobile askrol kiya. log is ki kitab ke bare mein lakh rahe thay. kuch ise apani zindagi ka bhatrin nawal qarar de rahe thay. kuch kha rahe thay ke raz ne apane jazbaat ko be naqab kar diya hai.

ek larki ne likha: *"raz sahab, aap ki tehreer prh kar lagta hai jaise aap ne mira dil prh liya ho."*

aayan ne woh comment dekha aur ise laga jaise woh is larki ko jawab dena chahta hai — *"agar tumhein pata hota ke mein ktana khali hoon, toh tum yeh na kehtin."*

magar is ne nahin likha.

is ne phone neeche rakha aur khirki ki traf dekha. bahar sheher roshan tha. karoron log. karoron khanayan. aur woh in sab mein se ek tha — lekin kisi ko nahin pata tha ke woh kaun hai.

---

agli subah woh apani aankhein khole b-ghair pra raha. phone mein battery khatam ho chaki thi. is ne ise charge par lagaya aur is waqt tak nahin dekha jab tak chai na bana li.

nashate mein is ne khashak tost khaya. kuch khas nahin.

phir is ne phone khola.

notifikishanaz ki baarish thi. lekin is ki nazar ek chiz par thahar gai.

ek rayawayo.

is ne ise khola. likha tha:

*"raz ke nam se mashahor masnaf ki nayi kitab — ek mbalagha aamiz jazabati karago jis mein adab nam ki koi chiz nahin. sirf alfaz ka hajom aur jazbaat ki hira phiri. is kitab ko jo bhi prhe ga, woh ek bar zaroor sochay ga — kiya waqai yahi nambr wan hai?"*

aayan ne ise do bar parha. phir teesri bar.

woh ghussa nahin tha. woh hiran tha.

yeh rayawayo kisi ne aam qalam se nahin likha tha. is mein ek be rhami thi, ek sachaئi thi jisay woh jahatala nahin sakta tha. jaise koi is ke andar ki khali pan ko prh gaya ho aur ise mas kar diya ho.

is ne rayawayo krane wale ka nam dekha.

**naqad (Naqqad)** — ek gamanam pij jis ne kitabon ko be naqab krane ka mashan bana liya tha.

aayan maskraya. ise nahin pata tha ke yeh muskurahat kiya thi — tanaz? tareef? ya koi aur chiz jis ka nam woh nahin janta tha?

is ne woh rayawayo neeche rakha aur apani note bak kholi.

phir is ne likhna sharoa kiya.

kuch aisa jis ka ise khod andaza nahin tha — woh is naqad ko jawab dine wala tha. apani asl shanakhat se nahin, balke ek naye nam se.

ek naya akaؤant. ek nayi shanakhat.

is ne socha — *chalo dekhte hain ke yeh naqad mujhe pehchanay b-ghair majah se ktani daur tak bhas kar sakta hai.*

ise nahin pata tha ke yeh faisla is ki zindagi ka sab se aham mor sabat hoga.

lekin woh yeh bhi nahin janta tha ke woh naqad ek larki thi.

ek larki jis ka nam tha — zara nor.

aur woh is se bhi zyada akeli thi jatana woh tha.
