title: "b-ab 17 — wahi kife"
weight: 17
type: roman
---


---

paigham bahut chhota tha:

*"kaafi?"*

sirf ek lafz. lekin is ek lafz mein ek saal ki khamoshi, ek saal ka dard, aur ek saal ki mohabbat thi.

aayan ne yeh paigham parha aur ise yaqeen nahin aaya. is ne ise teen bar parha, phir jawab diya:

*"kab aur kahan?"*

*"wahi kife. aaj shaam paanch bajay."*

---

woh kife wahi tha jahan woh donon pehle kabhi nahin gaye thay — lekin jahan se woh kaئi bar guzaray thay. woh kifa jis ke bare mein unhon ne ek doosray ko nahin bataya tha, lekin jis ke bare mein unhon ne kitabon mein likha tha.

aayan pehle phanacha. is ne woh sit chani jahan se darwaza dakhaئi deta tha. is ne kaafi mangawaئi lekin phir bhi nahin pi. is ke haath kanap rahe thay.

paanch bajay ka waqt tha. chaar baj kar pachapan minute par darwaza khula.

zara andar aai.

woh pehle se makhatalaf lag rahi thi. pehle se naram, pehle se khamosh. is ne chaaron traf dekha, ise dhonda, aur jab is ki nazar is par pri toh woh ek lamhe ko rak gai.

phir woh is ki traf brhi aur is ke samne bith gai.

donon khamosh thay.

---

kaafi thandi ho rahi thi aur woh abhi tak khamosh thay.

aakhar zara ne baat sharoa ki:

"mein ne tamhari kitab parhi."

aayan ne aankhein jahaka lein. "mujhe pata hai."

"tum ne bahut kuch likha hai is mein."

"mein ne sach likha hai."

zara ne ise dekha. is ki aankhon mein woh ghussa nahin tha jo pehle hawa kurta tha. ab is ki aankhon mein thakawat thi, aur kuch aur — kuch aisa jo woh chahapa nahin sakti thi.

"tum ne mujhe kyun nahin bataya?"

"mein drata tha."

"ks chiz se?"

aayan ne is ki traf dekha. is ki aankhon mein nami thi.

"is se ke tum mujhe sirf is liay chhor do gi kyunke mein woh nahin hoon jo tum ne socha tha."

zara khamosh rahi. phir is ne kaha:

"mein tum se is liay naraz nahin thi ke tum raz ho. mein is liay naraz thi ke tum ne majah par bharosa nahin kiya jab mein ne tum par bharosa kar liya tha."

aayan ne is ki traf dekha. "mein jaan gaya tha ke tum wahi ho. is din jab tum ne woh jumla kaha — is raat ke bare mein jo tum ne apane walad ke bare mein bataya tha. mein is lamhe jaan gaya tha. aur mein batana chahta tha. har roz batana chahta tha."

"toh kyun nahin bataya?"

"kyunke mein tum se pyar kurta hoon. aur mein tumhein khona nahin chahta tha."

khamoshi. bahut lambi khamoshi.

zara ne kaafi ka cup athaya aur ek ghont liya. phir is ne ise neeche rakha aur kaha:

"mein tum se naraz hoon. shayad kaafi der tak rhon. lekin mein tum se pyar karti hoon. aur mein samajhti hoon."

is ne is ki traf dekha aur pehli bar is ki aankhon mein muskurahat aai — ek chhoti si thaki hui muskurahat.

"lekin aayan, hum ek saath nahin ho sakte jab tak tum mujhe sab kuch na bata do — aur jab tak tum khod se jhoot bolana band na kar do."

aayan ne sar halaya. woh smajah gaya tha.

"toh kiya ab hum sharoa kar sakte hain?"

"kiya sharoa kar sakte hain?"

"dobara. safr se. koi jhoot nahin. koi raz nahin. sirf do aam log jo ek doosray par bharosa karna sikh rahe hain."

zara ne ise dekha. is ne apana haath brhaya aur meez par is ke haath ko chahawa.

"sharoa kar sakte hain."
