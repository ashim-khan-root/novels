---

woh bhas rozana ka mamol ban chaki thi.

subah nau bajay aayan apani chai ke saath baithta aur apana jali akaؤant kholata. is ke paighamaat mein se pehla paigham hamesha zara ka hota — kabhi kisi kitab par tabasara, kabhi kisi flasafe par sawal, kabhi sirf ek lafz: *"aaj raat?"*

aur woh hamesha haan kehta.

is ne khod ko is aadat se rokane ki koshash ki. is ne socha — yeh sirf ek aarazi tafrih hai. ek gamanam shakhas ke saath be maqasad baatein. lekin har bar jab woh apana phone athata, is ki ungliyan khod bakhod is akaؤant par chali jatin.

---

ek raat bhas ne naya rakh liya.

zara ne likha:

*"tumhein lagta hai ke tum raz ko samajhte ho, lekin tum ghalat ho. woh apani kitabon mein jazbaat ko astamal kurta hai — apane qaraئin ko jorator krane ke liay. yeh adab nahin, hira phiri hai."*

aayan ne yeh parha aur is ke andar kuch tot sa gaya.

kiya waqai is ki tehreer sirf hira phiri thi? kiya woh sirf qaraئin ke jazbaat se khil raha tha?

is ne jawab likha:

*"agar jazbaat ko chahona hira phiri hai, toh phir koi bhi fan hira phiri hai. tum ise sirf is liay 'jorator' kehte ho kyunke tum khod apane jazbaat se drati ho."*

fori jawab aaya:

*"mein nahin drati."*

*"drati ho. aur yahi waja hai ke tum raz se atani jalati ho — kyunke woh woh kar raha hai jo tum nahin kar saktin. woh khul kar lakh raha hai. aur tum chahap kar tanqeed kar rahi ho."*

is bar jawab aanay mein der hui.

phir sirf do lafz aaye:

*"tum bhi."*

aayan ne yeh lafz prhe aur ise ehsaas hawa — woh shih thi. woh bhi chahap kar baat kar raha tha. woh bhi apani asl shanakhat chahapa raha tha. aur woh bhi kisi aur ko nicha dakha kar apani bratri sabat krane ki koshash kar raha tha.

woh donon ek jaise thay.

aur yahi unhein sab se zyada drata tha.

---

raat gaye hasn ne kal ki.

"yar, tum pichlay kuch dinon se ajib lag rahe ho. sab theek hai?"

aayan ne jawab nahin diya. woh soch raha tha ke kiya woh hasn ko bataye — is naqad ke bare mein, is bhas ke bare mein, is ajib se rashate ke bare mein jo alfaz se ban raha tha.

"aayan?"

"haan sab theek hai. bas… kisi se baat ho rahi hai."

"kisi se?"

"haan. internet par."

hasn hans diya. "tum? tum toh logon se baat krane se bhi ktrate ho. woh kaun hai?"

"pata nahin."

"kiya matalab?"

"matalab, mein nahin janta ke woh kaun hai. aur woh nahin jaanti ke mein kaun hoon."

khamoshi. phir hasn ne kaha:

"tum is se milna chahte ho."

"nahin!"

"aayan, tum jhoot bol rahe ho. mein tumhein paanch saal se janta hoon. tum kisi ke bare mein atana nahin sochate jab tak ke woh tamhare liay aham na ho."

aayan khamosh raha.

"is ka nam kiya hai?"

"naqad."

"asli nam?"

"pata nahin."

hasn ne aah bhari. "tumhein pata hai, tum donon ek doosray se chahap kar baatein kar rahe ho. yeh koi rashata nahin, yeh ek khil hai. jab tak tum asli chehray nahin dekho ge, yeh sab be mani hai."

"shayad."

"shayad nahin, aayan. yaqeen se kaho."

lekin aayan yaqeen se kuch nahin kha sakta tha.

kyunke ise pata nahin tha ke woh zara se baat kar raha hai. aur ise yeh bhi nahin pata tha ke yeh be manٰi khil jalad hi is ki zindagi ka sab se aham rashata ban jaئe ga.

---

**bab chaharam khatam**
