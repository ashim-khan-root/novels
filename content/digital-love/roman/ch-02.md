"yar amaan, mein ne kaha nahin — mujhe nahin chahiye shadi."

"har larki yahi kehti hai, phir pachis ke baad roti hai ke rashate nahin aate."

zara ne aankhein ghama dein. woh jaanti thi ke yeh bhas kabhi khatam nahin hogi. is ne apana phone n-kala aur skrol krane lagi. amaan kuch aur kha rahi thin, lekin woh sn nahin rahi thi.

*"raz ki nayi kitab nambr wan — das laakh kapayan frokhat"*

log raz ke bare mein paagal thay. is ki tehreer ko dayawata bana rahe thay. har doosri post mein is ke alfaz ko "zindagi badal dine wala" kaha jaa raha tha.

aur zara ko yeh sab dekh kar jalan ho rahi thi.

nahin, jalan shih lafz nahin tha. yeh is se bhi zyada pichida tha. woh raz ki tehreer ko prh kar andar se kuch mhasos karti thi — aur ise is se nafrat thi.

ise is se nafrat thi ke is ki kitabein is ke dil ko chhu jati hain, jabke woh chahti hai ke woh na chahawaئin.

aur sab se zyada, ise is se nafrat thi ke woh khod woh nahin lakh sakti jo woh likhta hai.

"amaan, mein apane kamray mein jaa rahi hoon."

"phir se wahi? tum raat bhar jaagti ho aur phir subah athati nahin—"

"haan amaan, mein jaanti hoon."

is ne maan ke mathe par bosa diya aur kamray mein chali gai.

*"sirf alfaz ka hajom aur jazbaat ki hira phiri. yeh kitab humein yeh b-aur krati hai ke gherai wohein hai jahan sirf dukh ho. lekin kiya waqai adab sirf dukh ka nam hai? kiya khushi ko bayan karna kam fan hai?"*

is ne jamale ko ghora. ise laga jaise yeh sirf raz par tanqeed nahin thi — yeh khod is ke apane dil ki baat thi jisay woh kabhi kisi se nahin kha saki thi.

woh lakhati rahi.

*"is kitab ko jo bhi prhe ga, woh ek bar zaroor sochay ga — kiya waqai yahi nambr wan hai? agar yeh nambr wan hai, toh phir hum ek aisi duniya mein ji rahe hain jahan acha adab nahin, sirf achi marakitang kamayab hoti hai."*

is ne antr batan dabaya.

ab rayawayo post ho chaka tha. woh ise delete nahin kar sakti thi.

aur sach poochein toh woh delete karna bhi nahin chahti thi.

kiya waqai yeh sirf jalan thi?

is raat woh der tak so nahin saki. woh raz ke bare mein sochati rahi — is shakhas ke bare mein jis ka chehra kisi ne nahin dekha, magar jis ne hazaaron dilon par hukumat kar li thi.

aur woh sochati rahi — kiya mein waqai is se nafrat karti hoon? ya is se jisay woh majah mein jagata hai?

ise yaqeen tha ke ise pata hoga. aur ise yaqeen tha ke woh khamosh rahe ga.

kyunke baray lakhari tabasare nahin karte.

ya kam az kam woh aisa sochati thi.
