title: "b-ab 16 — aakhari kitab"
weight: 16
type: roman

---

ek mahina baad kitab manazr aam par aai.

is ka nam tha *"dijital mohabbat"* — aur masnaf ka nam tha *aayan hidr*. pehli bar is ne apane asli nam se likha tha.

kitab mein koi tswir nahin thi. koi wazahat nahin thi. sirf ek saroraq tha — ek khali kife ki tswir, jahan do cup kaafi rakhe thay.

aur ek didikishan:

*"is larki ke nam jo mujhe skha gai ke sachaئi sab se bari mohabbat ki khani hai."*

kitab ne kisi bhi marakitang ke b-ghair frokhat hona sharoa kar diya. log ise prh rahe thay — woh log jo raz ko jante thay, aur woh log jo nahin jante thay.

yeh in ki khani thi.

har woh raat jo unhon ne gamanam guzaray thay. har bhas, har hansi, har khamoshi. har woh lafz jo unhon ne ek doosray se kaha tha. woh sari kitab is sachaئi ke bare mein thi jo in ke dramayan thi — aur is jhoot ke bare mein jis ne ise tore diya.

lekin sab se aham baat — kitab mein koi alazam nahin tha. koi bhana nahin tha. sirf is ki ghalati ka aatraf tha.

---

zara ne kitab is waqt dekhi jab woh kisi bak astor mein gai thi.

woh kuch aur dekh rahi thi ke is ki nazar ek astind par pri — *dijital mhabat*. is ne kitab uthai aur is ka saroraq dekha. phir masnaf ka nam dekha.

aayan hidr.

is ka dil zor se dharaka. woh kitab waps rakhna chahti thi lekin is ke haath ne ise pakar rakha tha. is ne ise kharida.

woh ghar gai aur kitab kholi. pehle safahe par didikishan tha. is ne ise parha aur is ka gla bhar aaya.

is ne pori raat jaag kar kitab parhi.

subah jab woh khatam hui toh is ki aankhon se aansoo bha rahe thay. lekin woh ghase ke aansoo nahin thay. woh smajah ke aansoo thay.

is ne smajah liya tha ke woh kyun khamosh tha. is ne smajah liya tha ke woh kyun chahapa raha tha. is ne smajah liya tha ke is ne jo kuch bhi kiya, woh mohabbat ki waja se kiya — aur dar ki waja se.

is ne kitab band ki aur apana phone athaya. phir is ne ise neeche rakh diya.

woh abhi tayar nahin thi. lekin woh is ke qareeb thi.

---

**bab shanazadham khatam**
