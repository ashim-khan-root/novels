---

asi din shaam ko zara daftar mein fileein dekh rahi thi ke is ki nazar ek purane aidishan par pri — raz ki pehli kitab ka pehla aidishan. is ne ise athaya aur alata plat kar dekha.

achank is ki nazar ek jamale par thahar gai.

*"kuch raz aise hote hain jo chahapane se nahin, zahar krane se hafazt pate hain."*

yeh wahi jumla tha.

wahi jumla jo is ne apane gamanam dost se kaha tha — is raat jab unhon ne apane waladin ke bare mein baat ki thi. is ne ise bataya tha ke is ke walad ne ise chhor diya, aur is ne jawab mein yeh jumla kaha tha.

aur ab yeh jumla raz ki pehli kitab mein tha.

zara ki saans rak gai.

is ne kitab band ki aur ise dobara khola. wahi jumla. wahi alfaz. is ne apana phone n-kala aur apani aur apane gamanam dost ki guftugu kholi — asi raat ki baat chit.

jumla balakl wahi tha.

woh kursi par bith gai. is ka damagh ghom raha tha.

kiya is ka gamanam dost raz tha?

ya kiya raz ne is ki guftugu sn li thi?

ya yeh sab mehez atafaq tha?

lekin ise pata tha — yeh atafaq nahin tha. kuch bhi ho sakta hai, lekin yeh jumla kisi aur ka nahin ho sakta. yeh is ka tha, aur sirf wahi janta tha jis se is ne yeh kaha tha.

woh uthi aur b-ghair kuch sochay aayan ke disk ki traf chal di.

---

woh apani sit par baitha kuch lakh raha tha. zara ne darwazay par dastak di b-ghair andar qadam rakha.

"aayan."

is ne oopar dekha. is ke chehray par pareshani thi — aur kuch aur bhi. kuch aisa jo zara pehle kabhi nahin dekha tha.

"kiya hawa?"

"tum raz ko jante ho."

yeh bayan tha, sawal nahin.

aayan khamosh raha. is ki aankhon mein dar tha.

"tum raz ko jante ho, aur tum ne mujhe bataya nahin."

"zara—"

"mein jaanti hoon ke tum jante ho. tamhara chehra dekh kar pata chal gaya. tum ne lanachang ke din apana rang kho diya tha. tum raz ko jante ho, aur tum ise chahapa rahe ho."

woh mari aur chali gai.

aayan ne ise jate dekha — aur ise laga jaise woh ise hamesha ke liay kho raha hai.

lekin asl sach toh abhi tak ise pata nahin tha.

woh sirf atana jaanti thi ke aayan raz ko janta hai.

woh nahin jaanti thi ke aayan khod raz hai.
