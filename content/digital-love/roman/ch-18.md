---

chhah mhine baad.

aayan ne apani nayi kitab par kaam khatam kiya tha — is bar kisi bhis mein nahin. is ke nam se, is ke chehray se, is ki asl shanakhat se. ise dar tha, lekin woh dar ab is par hawi nahin tha.

is ne phone athaya aur ek paigham likha:

*"alaslam alikam, kiya hum dobara taaraf kar sakte hain?"*

is ne paigham bhija aur phone neeche rakh diya. is ka dil zor se dharak raha tha.

chand sikand baad jawab aaya:

*"walaikum alaslam, khush aamadid."*

aayan maskraya. is ne phone athaya aur taئap kiya:

*"mein aayan hoon. ek lakhari. kuch acha hoon, kuch bura. lekin jhoot nahin bolata — ab nahin."*

jawab aaya:

*"mein zara hoon. ek larki jo kabhi sirf tanqeed karti thi, ab mohabbat karna sikh rahi hai. aur mein bhi jhoot nahin bolati — ab nahin."*

aayan ne phone apane dil ke paas rakha aur khirki se bahar dekha.

sheher roshan tha. log apani masarofayat mein thay. aur woh in sab mein se ek tha — lekin ab ise pata tha ke woh kaun hai.

is ne raat ko zara ko kal ki. unhon ne ghanton baatein kin — is bar apani asl aawaz mein, apani asl shanakhat se. koi bhis nahin tha. koi raz nahin tha. sirf do log thay — aur in ke dramayan sachaئi thi.

---

agle din woh asi kife mein mile — jahan unhon ne pehli bar waqai ek doosray ko dekha tha.

yeh pehli dit nahin thi. yeh ek nayi sharoaat thi.

aayan ne is ke haath mein ek kitab rakhi — khali safahon wali note bak.

"yeh kiya hai?"

"tamhari pehli kitab."

zara ne ise dekha. "mujhe nahin lagta ke mein—"

"mein janta hoon. tum drati ho. lekin mein bhi drata tha — aur dekho, mein ne likha."

is ne kitab ke pehle safahe par ek jumla likha tha:

*"woh larki jis ne kabhi sirf tanqeed ki, ab apani khani khod lakhe gi."*

zara ne woh jumla parha aur aansoo nahin rok saki.

"shukriya," is ne kaha. "yeh… bahut hai."

"kaafi nahin. lekin sharoa hai."

woh donon maskraئe. suraj gharob ho raha tha aur kife ki roshni sunheri thi.

kuch bhi kamal nahin tha. woh donon abhi bhi sikh rahe thay — bharosa karna, khulna, drane ke bawajod mohabbat karna.

lekin woh sikh rahe thay.

aur yahi sab se aham tha.

**khatam shad**

*dijital mohabbat, haqiqi jhoot — ek khani in donon ke bare mein janhon ne chahap kar mohabbat ki, aur khul kar jine ka rasta dhond liya.*
