---

zara ko nokri mal gai thi.

aur woh nokri is ki zindagi ka sab se bara mazaq sabat hone wali thi.

woh is chhoti se pablashang kampani ke daftar mein khari thi, apane naye aafas ko dekh rahi thi. deewaron par kitabon ke postr lage thay. kuch mashahor thin, kuch gamanam. meezon par kaghazaat bakhare thay aur har traf kitabon ki khushbu thi.

yeh is ka khwab tha.

siwaye ek chiz ke — is ka pehla asaئanamant raz ki nayi kitab ki marakitang thi.

wahi raz. wahi kitab jisay is ne kuch din pehle b-raah rast tanqeed ka nashana banaya tha. wahi kitab jis ne ise raaton ko jagane par majabor kar diya tha.

aur ab woh ise frogh dine wali thi.

"aap ki meez yahan hai, muhtarama nor."

zara ne sar halaya aur apani sit par bith gai. is ne apane big se lip tap n-kala aur ise khola. ise laga jaise taqadir is se koi khil khil rahi hai.

---

ek ghanta baad manijar ne kaha ke tim meeting hai.

"chaliay, mein aap ko sab se malawata hoon."

zara manijar ke peeche chali gai. conference rom mein paanch chhah afrad baithe thay. sab ne ise dekha aur maskraئe. is ne bhi maskra kar jawab diya.

phir is ki nazar ek shakhas par pri.

woh kaunay mein baitha tha, apani note bak mein kuch lakh raha tha. is ne oopar nahin dekha, hatayٰ ke jab aai ka taaraf hawa. is ne sirf sar halaya aur phir se lakhane laga.

zara ne ise dekha aur is ke andar kuch bharaka.

"yeh hain aayan hidr — hamare aiditoril kanaslatant."

aayan ne ab oopar dekha. is ki aankhein zara se milein.

is ne sirf atana kaha: "hilo."

aur phir waps lakhane laga.

zara ko smajah nahin aaya ke yeh be aatanaئi thi ya sharam. lekin is ne faisla kar liya — woh is shakhas ko psnd nahin karti.

---

meeting ke doran zara ne dekha ke aayan shaz o nadr hi bolata hai. jab bhi koi is se raئe mangata, woh chand alfaz mein jawab deta — makhatsar, magar wazn dar. is ki mojodagi mein kamray ka draja hararat badal jata tha. woh khamosh tha, lekin is ki khamoshi mein ek taqat thi.

zara ko yeh psnd nahin aaya.

kyunke woh khod hamesha bolati thi, aur woh log jo nahin bolate, in par ise bharosa nahin hota tha.

meeting ke baad jab woh apani sit par waps jaa rahi thi, is ne aayan ko rahadari mein khare sna. woh phone par baat kar raha tha:

"haan, mein janta hoon ke raz ki nayi kitab lanach ho rahi hai. lekin mujhe nahin lagta ke humein koi bara ayawant karna chahiye."

zara rak gai. woh raz ki kitab ke bare mein baat kar raha tha.

is ne snana jari rakha.

"yeh sab bahut zyada ho raha hai. raz ko apani shanakhat zahar krane ki zarorat nahin. kitab khod bolani chahiye."

zara ne aankhein skirin. yeh shakhas raz ka dafaa kar raha tha. aur woh ise raz ki shanakhat zahar na krane ka mashora de raha tha.

kyun?

woh aage brhi aur aayan ke samne khari ho gai.

"aap raz ko jante hain?"

aayan ne phone neeche kiya aur ise dekha. is ki aankhon mein ek jahataka tha — magar woh fori tore par ghaئab ho gaya.

"kyun poochh rahi hain?"

"kyunke aap is ki shanakhat chahapane ki baat kar rahe thay. aur chunke mein is ki kitab ki marakitang kar rahi hoon, mujhe janane ka haq hai."

aayan ne ise dekha. ek lamha. do lamhe.

phir is ne kaha: "mein raz ko nahin janta. lekin mein is ki tehreer ko janta hoon. aur mujhe lagta hai ke kuch chizin frokhat krane se zyada aham hain."

woh mar kar chala gaya.

zara ise dekhti ra gai.

ise nahin pata tha ke is shakhas ke saath woh har roz laray gi. aur ise yeh bhi nahin pata tha ke wahi shakhas woh tha jis se woh har raat baat karti thi.

lekin taqadir ko apane khil khilane dijaye.

woh donon ek doosray ke qareeb aanay wale thay — chahe woh chahin ya na chahin.
