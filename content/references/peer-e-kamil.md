The story of two individuals from vastly different worlds whose paths cross, leading to spiritual transformation. A landmark Urdu novel exploring faith, identity, and redemption. Salaar and Imama's journey from arrogance and blind adherence to enlightenment has made this one of the most beloved Urdu novels of all time.
