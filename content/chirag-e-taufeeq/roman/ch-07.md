# charaghِ tofiq (Chirag-e-Taufeeq)

---

teen din zawaya aur thٰ ne akthe kaam kiya.

woh subah swire ktab khane aati — thٰ pehle se mojod hota. woh khamoshi se apane kaam sharoa kar dete — woh fileein chahantati, purane kaghazaat parhti, tarikhi dastavezaat dekhti — aur woh apane brash aur rangon ke saath lakri aur kitabon ki bhali kurta rehta.

in ke dramayan bahut baatein nahin hoti thin. magar jo khamoshi thi — woh bojahal nahin thi. woh is qism ki khamoshi thi jo do logon ke dramayan is waqt banti hai jab woh ek doosray ko smajahane lagte hain.

teesray din ki shaam — zawaya ko kuch mila.

ktab khane ke sab se purane shilaf ke peeche — ek chhoti si chahapi hui jib mein — ise ek purana dabiz lafafa mila. is par mhar thi — maghalia slatant ki mhar — aur is par likha tha: *waqaf nama.*

is ne ise khola toh is ke haath kanap gaye.

"thٰ —" is ki aawaz mein josh tha — "thٰ, yeh dekho!"

thٰ dorata hawa aaya — aur jab is ne kaghazaat dekhe toh is ke chehray par wahi jahalak aai jo zawaya ke chehray par thi.

"yeh wahi hai jo hum dhond rahe thay," is ne dheeray se kaha — jaise awanachi aawaz mein bolane se kaghaz ghaئab ho jaئe ga.

waqaf nama saaf tha — solah so aksth mein, shahajahan ke daur mein, is zameen ko waqaf qarar diya gaya tha. is par teen gawahon ke dastakhat thay — aur qazi ka mhar tha. yeh kabhi nahin bichi jaa sakti thi. kabhi nahin gri jaa sakti thi.

zawaya ne thٰ ki traf dekha — is ki aankhein chamak rahi thin. "hum ne kar dakhaya."

"abhi nahin," thٰ ne kaha — magar is ke chehray par muskurahat thi. "yeh sirf pehla qadam hai. magar yeh qadam — yeh bahut bara hai."

woh donon khamoshi se kaghazaat dekhte rahe — bahar andhera ghera ho raha tha, magar ktab khane mein roshni thi.
