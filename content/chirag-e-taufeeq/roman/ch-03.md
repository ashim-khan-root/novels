# charaghِ tofiq (Chirag-e-Taufeeq)

---

bas ne chhah ghante baad rohan por ke chhotay se astishan par brik lagai.

zawaya ne neeche utar kar dekha — yeh jagah aslam aabad se bahut makhatalaf thi. yahan koi bland amaratin nahin thin. koi shore nahin tha. sirf purani ainton ke ghar thay, tang glayan, aur hawa mein yasmin aur mitti ki malawat thi.

khala ise line aai thin — chhoti si khaton, safed dopte mein, chehray par woh sukoon jo zawaya ko smajah nahin aata tha. unhon ne zawaya ko gle se laga liya — aise jaise kabhi sawal nahin poocha tha ke woh kyun aai hai.

"ao beta. ghar mein phool rakhe hain."

ghar chhota tha — ainton ka, purana, magar saaf sthara. shan mein yasmin ka poda tha aur ek purani charapaئi jahan khala bith kar Quran parhti thin. zawaya ne apana big ek kaunay mein rakha — aur pehli bar kaئi dinon mein is ne saans liya.

shaam ko khala ise qasaba dakhane le gaئin. rohan por ki glayan tang thin — magar har mor par ek nayi chahapi hui thi. ek purani masjid jis ka minar tirha tha magar phir bhi khara tha. ek dakan jahan ek boorha aadmi haath se kapra rangata tha. bachchay gali mein khil rahe thay — aur in ki hansi zawaya tak phanachi, jaise woh kisi bholi hui chiz ko yaad kar rahe hoon.

aur phir woh is ke samne aaya — rohan por ka shahi ktab khana.

teen so saal purani amarat. lakri ke bland mharab, jin par shahajahani traz ki kanda kari thi. darwaza bhaari tha, magar is ke neeche se roshni jahank rahi thi. zawaya wahan khari rahi — ise smajah nahin aa raha tha ke yeh amarat ise kyun apani traf khinach rahi hai.

"yeh ktab khana teen so saal purana hai," khala ne kaha. "yahan ek naujawan hai — thٰ — jo is ki maramat kar raha hai. bahut smajahadar larka hai. kal tum is se mal sakti ho."

zawaya ne kuch nahin kaha. woh sirf darwazay ko dekhti rahi — is lakri ko jis par sadayawan ke haathon ke nishaan thay. woh soch rahi thi — teen so saal. aur is ki apani zindagi mehez tayaئas bras mein tot gai.

raat ko woh so nahin saki. woh shan mein baithi yasmin ki khushbu mein khawaئi rahi. is ke haath mein woh purana naqasha tha — jis par is ne dastakhat nahin kiay thay — aur woh soch rahi thi — shayad yeh jagah ise koi jawab de.

shayad nahin bhi. magar is ke paas jaane ke liay aur koi jagah nahin thi.
