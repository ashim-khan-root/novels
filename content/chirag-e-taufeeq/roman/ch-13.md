# charaghِ tofiq (Chirag-e-Taufeeq)

---

teen raatein — zawaya ne nahin swaya. woh ktab khane mein baithi rahi, waqaf nama is ke samne, aur is ke dil mein hazaar sawal.

woh sochati rahi — agar Allah hai toh yeh kyun ho raha hai? agar sach hai toh fath kyun nahin milti? agar woh shih hai toh tanha kyun hai?

jawab nahin milta tha — aur har sawal is ke imaan ko kamzor kar raha tha.

teesri raat — woh ktab khane se nkli aur bahar shan mein bith gai. aasman saaf tha — stare jahalamala rahe thay — aur hawa mein yasmin ki khushbu thi. ise hazarat sofi alim ki baatein yaad aaein:

*rako. sno. aur apane dil ko baat krane do.*

is ne aankhein band kin. woh pehli bar khamosh hui — khali hui — b-ghair kisi sawal ke, b-ghair kisi shakawe ke.

phir woh uthi — aur wazo kiya.

is ne thajad ki namaz parhi — zindagi mein pehli bar sachay dil se. is ke aansoo bha rahe thay — magar woh ab kamazori ke nahin thay. woh is ke dil ki safaئi thay.

jab is ne salam phira toh ise laga jaise is ke andar ka bojah utar gaya ho. ise koi jawab nahin mila — magar ise sawal ki zarorat nahin rahi.

woh khirki ke paas gai — aur subah ki pehli roshni ko aate dekha. is ke haath mein waqaf nama tha — aur is ke dil mein yaqeen tha. ise nahin maloom tha ke adalat mein kiya ho ga — magar woh jaanti thi ke woh laray gi.

aur yahi is din ki sab se bari fath thi.
