# charaghِ tofiq (Chirag-e-Taufeeq)

---

do din baad — thٰ ko grafatar kar liya gaya.

zawaya ktab khane mein thi jab is ne bahar gaarion ki aawaz sni. is ne khirki se jahanka — teen police win — aur aath maslh ahalakar. is ka dil bith gaya.

woh bahar bhagi — magar thٰ pehle hi in ke dramayan tha. is ke haathon par hathakrayan thin — aur woh is se kuch daur khara tha, balakl khamosh. is ne zawaya ko dekha — aur zara sa sar halaya — jaise kha raha ho *mat dro.*

"is par kiya alazam hai?" zawaya ne poocha — is ki aawaz mein ghussa tha aur is ke peeche khauf.

"jali dastavezaat banane ka," ek afasar ne kaha — be prawaئi se — "is ne tarikhi kaghazaat jali banaئe."

yeh wahi alazam tha jo in par khod lag sakta tha — magar wahara ne pehle hamala kiya tha.

zawaya ne thٰ ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein khauf nahin tha. is ne bahut dheeray se kuch kaha — sirf is ke labon ki harakt se is ne prh liya:

*waqaf nama. chahapa do.*

police ise le gai — aur zawaya wahan khari rahi, akile, is ktab khane ke samne jahan woh donon ne mal kar sab kuch banaya tha.

woh raat zawaya ne nahin khaya, na piya, na swaya.

woh ktab khane mein baithi rahi — wohein jahan woh aur thٰ akthe kaam karte thay — aur is ne is ki khali kursi dekhi. is ke brash — is ki chai ka cup — is ki chhori hui kitab.

is ne Allah se pehli bar sach mein dua mangi — bachapan ke baad pehli bar — magar ise yaqeen nahin aaya ke woh sni jaa rahi hai.

"kyun?" is ne khamoshi se poocha — andhire mein — kisi se bhi, sab se bhi — "mein ne sach kiya — toh yeh sab kyun hawa?"

koi jawab nahin tha. sirf woh kitabein thin — aur woh waqaf nama jo is ke sine se laga hawa tha.

subah hone tak woh baithi rahi — aur jab suraj nkla toh ise pata tha ke ise ab khod larana ho ga. kisi ke aanay ka antazar nahin tha.
