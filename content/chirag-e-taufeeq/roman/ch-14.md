# charaghِ tofiq (Chirag-e-Taufeeq)

---

adalat ka kamra — zawaya ke liay yeh aslam aabad ke is aafas jisa tha jahan is ne dastakhat nahin kiay thay. sirf fraq yeh tha — is bar woh akile nahin thi. is ki maan gilari mein baithi thi — aur is ki khala is ke paas thin. aur agarche thٰ abhi jail mein tha — magar is ki mojodagi zawaya ke dil mein thi.

wahara ka vakeel ek tajaraba car shakhas tha — mehnga sot, tez zaban, aur woh aatamad jo sirf paisa deta hai. is ne zawaya ko dekh kar maskraya — jaise woh pehle hi jeet chaka ho.

judge ne muqadma sharoa kiya — aur zawaya ne kaghazaat pish kiay. waqaf nama, naqashe, tarikhi dastavezaat — sab kuch jo is ne aur thٰ ne akthe kiya tha.

wahara ke vakeel ne aatraz kiya — "yeh sab jali hai, yawar aanar. yeh aurat pehle bhi jhootay alazamat mein pakri jaa chaki hai — aslam aabad mein is par ghaban ka muqadma hai."

zawaya ne mhasos kiya jaise ise thapr maara gaya ho. is ne gilari mein baithi maan ko dekha — is ki aankhon mein aansoo thay — magar woh maskra rahi thi. is ne sar halaya — jaise kha rahi ho *mat dro.*

zawaya ne gehri saans li. "yawar aanar, aslam aabad ka muqadma jhoota hai — asi kampani ne mire khalaf jhootay alazamat lagaئe kyunke mein ne in ki jali amarat ke manasobe par dastakhat krane se ankar kar diya tha."

"aur aap ke paas saboot hai?" judge ne poocha.

zawaya ne apane big se woh purana naqasha n-kala — jis par is ne dastakhat nahin kiay thay — aur is ke saath woh rikaradang jisay is ne chahapa kar rakha tha. woh rikaradang jahan shaib saaf kha raha tha — *ham ne sakhat mein thodi kami ki hai — manafa dogana ho jaئe ga.*

adalat mein khamoshi chaha gai.

wahara ke vakeel ka chehra badal gaya. "yeh — yeh rikaradang jali hai —"

"yeh rikaradang jali nahin hai," zawaya ne kaha — is ki aawaz pehle se zyada paki thi — "aur yawar aanar, mein yeh bhi sabat kar sakti hoon ke wahara kanastrakshanaz ne teen saal pehle asi tarah ki ek amarat grate huye das afrad ko halak kiya tha — muqadma daba diya gaya tha."

is ne adalat mein woh purani file rakhi — jo ise thٰ ki thaqiq se mili thi. judge ne ise dekha — aur phir wahara ke vakeel ki traf dekha.

"adalat teen din ke liay malatawi ki jati hai. aur is doran — wahara kanastrakshanaz ke khalaf aarazi pabandi ka hakam jari kiya jata hai."

hathora laga — aur zawaya ne sna. is ne sna — aur pehli bar ise yaqeen aaya ke sach jeet sakta hai.

woh gilari mein gai — aur is ki maan ne ise gle se laga liya. is ke aansoo bha rahe thay — magar woh khushi ke thay.

aur is ne thٰ ke bare mein socha — jo jail mein tha — aur is ne dil mein dua ki ke woh bhi jalad aazad ho.
