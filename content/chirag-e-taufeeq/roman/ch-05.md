# charaghِ tofiq (Chirag-e-Taufeeq)

---

agle din zawaya phir ktab khane gai.

is ne khod se kaha — woh sirf kitab dikhane jaa rahi hai. woh raz jo thٰ ne bataya tha — is ne ise be chin kar diya tha. jhoot nahin tha. magar sach yeh bhi tha — woh is shakhas ko dekhna chahti thi jo kitabon se is tarah baat kurta hai jaise woh zanda hoon.

woh ktab khane mein dakhal hui toh thٰ ek khirki ke paas baitha tha — is ke haath mein ek chhota sa brash tha aur woh lakri ke ek purane takhate par kaam kar raha tha. is ki ungliyan nazk thin — magar pakhata. har harakt mein sabr tha, jaldi nahin.

woh is ke samne bith gai — aur ise dekhti rahi.

thٰ ne ise dekh kar sar halaya — aur kaam jari rakha. kuch der khamoshi rahi — phir is ne b-ghair oopar dekhe kaha:

"kal raat mein ne is kitab ki jalad kholi — aur mujhe andar ek khat mila. teen so saal purana."

zawaya ne aage jahak kar dekha — thٰ ne apane haath se ek purana, zarad kaghaz n-kala — is par farasi mein likha tha.

"kiya likha hai?"

"yeh ke is ktab khane ki zameen waqaf hai — kabhi nahin bichi jaa sakti. yeh ek ahad hai — Allah aur logon ke dramayan."

zawaya ne khat parha — is ki lakiron mein ek aur waqt ki mhant thi. is ne thٰ ki traf dekha — is ke chehray par wahi sukoon tha jo kal tha, magar ab is ki aankhon mein kuch aur tha — jaise woh is se kuch kehna chahta ho magar kha nahin raha tha.

"tum yahan kyun ho?" zawaya ne poocha — b-ghair sochay. "tum prhe lakhe ho — tumhein dabaئi mein nokri thi — mein ne sna. tum yahan kyun kaam kar rahe ho?"

thٰ ne brash rakha — aur khirki se bahar dekha. bahut der tak. phir bola — is ki aawaz mein woh bojah tha jo sirf wahi janta tha:

"dabaئi mein mein ek fram mein tha — bari tankhawaa, bara ahada. magar ek din mujhe pata chala ke hamari fram jali dizaئan bana rahi hai — amaratin jo mhafoz nahin thin. mein ne kaha — yeh ghalat hai. unhon ne kaha — tumhein prawaa nahin krani chahiye."

is ne zawaya ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein wahi dard tha jo woh apane andar phachanti thi.

"mein ne astafayٰ de diya. aur waps aa gaya — is ktab khane ko bhal krane. kyunke kam az kam yeh amarat sachi hai."

zawaya ne ise dekha — lamba — aur pehli bar is ne mhasos kiya ke woh akile nahin thi. aur yeh ehsaas is ke liay kisi bhi mashore se zyada qimati tha.
