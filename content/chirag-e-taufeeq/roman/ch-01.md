# charaghِ tofiq (Chirag-e-Taufeeq)

---

baarish ki dheemi bochahar aslam aabad ki bland o ba-la amarat par shishe ke hashaye bana rahi thi. pandrhawin manazl ke roshan dafatri kamray mein, zawaya maratazayٰ ke samne neela naqasha phila hawa tha. is ki sarakh aankhon mein thakan thi, magar haath ki grafat qalam par pakhata thi.

woh pichlay teen din se is naqashe ko dekh rahi thi. simant ki maqadar — kam. stil ka grad — kamzor. banayad ka hasab — ghalat. yeh amarat agar khari hui toh paanch saal bhi nahin thahare gi.

aur woh chahte thay ke woh is par dastakhat kare.

"tumhein pata hai is projikt mein ktana paisa laga hai?" shaib ki aawaz ne is ki toja tori. woh is ke samne khara tha — mehnga sot, mhangi ghari, magar aankhon mein sasta pan.

"pata hai," zawaya ne kaha — is ki aawaz mein thakan thi magar larazash nahin. "aur yeh bhi pata hai ke agar yeh amarat gri toh paanch so khandan malabe tale dab jaein ge."

shaib hans pra — ek hansi jis mein woh sab kuch tha jo ise lagta tha ke woh janta hai. "tum nawal parhti ho bahut? yeh haqiqi duniya hai, zawaya. yahan koi nahin grata. hum ne sakhat mein thodi kami ki hai — atani ke pata bhi na chale — aur manafa dogana ho jaئe ga."

"aur agar pata chal gaya?"

"tamhari dagri aur nokri — donon ka janaza nikal jaئe ga."

zawaya khamosh rahi. is ne apane haath dekhe — woh kanap nahin rahe thay. ise khod hairat hui. woh soch rahi thi — is ki maan, jo akile ise parha rahi thi, ek skol tichar ki pinashan par. qaraz. qastin. agar woh yeh nokri kho de gi toh...

shaib ne meez par haath maara. "aqal astamal karo larki! tamhari maan ki pinashan atani nahin ke woh tamhare korat kisaz ka kharach utha sake!"

zawaya ne qalam athaya. naqasha is ke samne tha. dastakhat krane ke liay sirf ek harakt kaafi thi.

is ke zhan mein aqabal ka sher gonaja — woh sher jo is ke walad ne ise ek bar skhaya tha, jab woh bachchi thi:

*khodi ko kar bland atana ke har taqadir se phale*
*khada bande se khod pooche bata tiri raza kiya he*

is ne qalam rakh diya.

"mein astafayٰ de rahi hoon," is ne kaha. is ki aawaz atani praskon thi ke shaib ko yaqeen nahin aaya.

"kiya?"

"tum ne sna."

is ne apana big athaya — aur bahar chali gai. peeche shaib ki cheekhein thin, magar woh pehle hi bahut daur thi.

---

agle teen din — woh is ke liay qayamat thay.

kampani ne is par ghaban ka alazam lagaya. jhootay saboot — is ke dastakhat wali ek jali file — pish ki gai. pakstan konasl aaf aarakitikts ne ise aarazi tore par matal kar diya. is ki tswir akhbar mein aai — "khaton aarakitikt par tees laakh ki ghaban."

is ki maan ne phone kiya — is ki aawaz mein aansoo thay magar woh ro nahin rahi thin. "beta, kiya sach hai yeh?"

"jhoot hai amaan."

"mein jaanti hoon. magar log nahin jaanein ge."

maan ne phone band kar diya — aur zawaya pehli bar roya.

is ne socha — agar woh dastakhat kar deti toh aaj woh kursi par baithi hoti, paisay is ke akaؤant mein hote, is ki maan ko fikr na hoti.

magar woh zanda na hoti.

chaar din baad — woh rohan por jaane wali bas mein thi. is ka kala big is ke paas tha, is ka dil is ke sine mein bhaari tha, aur is ke haath mein sirf ek chiz thi — woh naqasha jis par is ne dastakhat nahin kiay thay. ise nahin maloom tha ke yeh naqasha ise kahan le jaئe ga.

magar woh jaanti thi — is ne shih kiya tha.

bas chal pri. aslam aabad peeche ra gaya — aur aage rohan por tha, jahan ise kuch bhi nahin maloom tha — siwaye is ke ke is ke paas khone ke liay kuch nahin bacha tha.
