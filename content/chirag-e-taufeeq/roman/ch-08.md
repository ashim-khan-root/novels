# charaghِ tofiq (Chirag-e-Taufeeq)

---

woh raat khamosh thi — magar zawaya so nahin paai thi.

waqaf nama is ke takaye ke neeche tha — is ne ise khala ko nahin bataya tha, kisi ko nahin bataya tha. sirf thٰ ko pata tha — aur woh bhi ise apane paas rakhane par razi nahin tha.

"yeh mhafoz nahin hai tamhare paas," is ne kaha tha. "kal hum ise vakeel ke paas le jaein ge."

magar woh raat mhafoz nahin thi.

subah teen bajay — khirki ke shishe se patthar takraya — aur zawaya jaag gai. is ka dil zor se dharak raha tha. woh uthi — aur farsh par ek kaghaz dekha, patthar se bandha hawa.

is ne kaghaz khola — is par sirf ek jumla likha tha:

*wa dastavez waps karo — warana tumhein pata hai kiya ho ga.*

zawaya ne kaghaz dekha — is ke haath kanap rahe thay — magar woh dri nahin. ise ghussa aaya. asi ghase ne ise taqat di — jaise aslam aabad mein mila tha.

woh uthi — aur sidhi ktab khane gai.

subah ki pehli namaz ka waqt tha — aur thٰ masjid mein tha. woh bahar bith kar is ka antazar karti rahi — kaghaz is ke haath mein — patthar ki yaad is ki hathili par.

jab thٰ aaya toh is ne kuch nahin kaha — sirf kaghaz ise thama diya.

thٰ ne parha — aur is ka chehra sakht ho gaya. "tum dri hui ho?"

"nahin," zawaya ne kaha — aur yeh sach tha. "mein dri nahin hoon — magar mein jaanti hoon ke woh kuch bhi kar sakte hain."

"hum kal adalat jaein ge," thٰ ne kaha. "aaj nahin — kyunke aaj tum aaram karo gi. mein yahan hoon."

zawaya ne is ki traf dekha — aur pehli bar ise laga ke shayad woh waqai mhafoz hai.
