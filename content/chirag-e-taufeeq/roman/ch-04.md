# charaghِ tofiq (Chirag-e-Taufeeq)

---

subah ki pehli roshni rohan por ki ainton par pri toh zawaya pehle se jaag chaki thi.

woh khamoshi se shan mein baithi chai pi rahi thi jab khala ne bahar se aa kar kaha — "woh ktab khane mein hai. jaa, is se mal."

zawaya ne sar halaya — magar is ke qadam nahin uth rahe thay. woh kisi se nahin milna chahti thi. woh bas yawan hi baithna chahti thi — jab tak kuch na badalay. magar woh jaanti thi — kuch nahin badalay ga jab tak woh khod na badalay.

ktab khane ka darwaza bhaari tha — magar is ne dhakil diya.

andar ki khamoshi ne ise ghir liya. kitabein — har traf kitabein — purani jaladon mein, grad aalod, magar kisi ne unhein saaf kiya tha, tarteeb diya tha. roshni awanachi khirkion se aa rahi thi aur grad ke zare is mein nach rahe thay.

ek seerhi par — oopar — ek shakhas khara tha. safed kurta. haath mein ek purani kitab. aur woh ise is tarah dekh raha tha jaise woh koi qimashi chiz ho.

is ne mar kar zawaya ko dekha — aur maskraya — ek chhoti si muskurahat, be taklaf. "aaiay."

zawaya ne sar halaya — aur kuch nahin kaha.

woh seerhi se atra — aur is ke samne aa kar khara ho gaya. is ki amar pachis ke lag bhag thi — aankhon mein gherai thi, magharor nahin, magar praatamad.

"thٰ skandr," is ne apana haath brhaya — magar bahut rasmi nahin, jaise woh ise janta ho.

"zawaya maratazayٰ," is ne haath thama — aur forً chhor diya.

"mein janta hoon," thٰ ne kaha. "aap ki khala ne bataya tha."

"kiya bataya?"

"yeh ke aap aslam aabad se aai hain — aur yeh ke aap ne kuch bahut mushkil kiya hai."

zawaya ne ise dekha — is ke lhaje mein tajasas tha magar tras nahin. "mushkil ki tareef makhatalaf hoti hai."

"sach hai." thٰ ne kitab meez par rakhi — aur is ki traf dekha. "mire liay mushkil yeh hai ke mein is kitab ki jalad ko atani ahatayat se bhal karon ke is ka asl rang waps aa jaئe — magar yeh kabhi wisi nahin hogi jisi pehle thi."

zawaya ne kitab ko dekha — purana, phata hawa, magar is ki jalad par sonay ki naqash o ngar thi. "phir tum yeh kyun kar rahe ho?"

"kyunke asl rang waps na aaye — magar is ka matalab yeh nahin ke woh khubsurat nahin ho sakti." is ne zawaya ki aankhon mein dekha — "kabhi kabhi toti hui chizon ki apani khobasorati hoti hai."

zawaya khamosh rahi — ise laga jaise woh is ke bare mein baat kar raha hai, magar is ne yaqeen se nahin kaha.

woh ktab khane mein ghomane lagi — kitabon ke dramayan — jabke thٰ apani seerhi par waps chala gaya. is ne is se koi sawal nahin poocha. ise koi mashora nahin diya. woh sirf apana kaam kurta raha — aur zawaya ko apani jagah di.

yeh woh pehla shakhas tha jis ne ise jagah di thi — b-ghair kisi sharat ke.

jab woh bahar nkli toh thٰ ne is ki traf nahin dekha. is ne sirf atana kaha — "kal ao toh mein aap ko yeh kitab dakhaؤan ga — is ki jalad mein ek raz chahapa hai."

zawaya raki — magar mari nahin. "kiya raz?"

"aa kar dekhein."

woh maskraئi — pehli bar kaئi dinon mein. aur bahar nikal gai.
