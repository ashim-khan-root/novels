# charaghِ tofiq (Chirag-e-Taufeeq)

---

thٰ zawaya ko hazarat sofi alim ke paas le gaya — ek aisa shakhas jis ke bare mein zawaya ne sirf atana sna tha ke woh bahut borhe hain aur bahut sidhe hain.

in ka ghar ktab khane se do glayan pehle tha — ek chhota sa, sada makaan, jis ke shan mein ek anar ka darakht tha aur is ke neeche chataئi bachahi thi. hazarat sofi alim wohein baithe thay — safed darhi, safed kapre, aur aankhon mein woh chamak jo sirf amar aur abadat deti hai.

unhon ne zawaya ko dekha — aur maskra diay. "ao beta."

zawaya bith gai — thٰ is ke paas. ise nahin maloom tha ke kiya kehna hai. woh aisi jagahon par kabhi nahin aai thi.

hazarat ne is ki traf dekha — lamba — aur phir kaha: "tum apane aap se bhag rahi ho."

zawaya ne sar athaya — hiran. "kiya matalab?"

"tum sochati ho ke tum aslam aabad se bhagi ho — magar tum asl mein apane aap se bhagi ho. woh naqasha jis par tum ne dastakhat nahin kiay — woh sirf tamhare pishe ka amathan nahin tha. woh tamhari roh ka amathan tha."

zawaya khamosh rahi — is ne kabhi is tarah nahin socha tha.

"aur tum paas ho gaئin," hazarat ne kaha — in ki aawaz mein narami thi — "magar tum abhi jaanti nahin ke tum ne kiya paya hai."

"toh mujhe kiya karna chahiye?" zawaya ne poocha — is ki aawaz mein khalos tha.

"rako. sno. aur apane dil ko baat krane do — pehle tum ne ise khamosh kar diya tha, ab ise bolane do."

hazarat ne in donon ko dua di — aur zawaya ko laga jaise is ke dil par se koi bojah utar gaya ho. jaise woh pehli bar saans le rahi ho.

bahar nikal kar is ne thٰ se kaha — "kyun nahin sab log aise hote?"

"kyunke sab log nahin dhondate," thٰ ne kaha. "magar tum dhond rahi ho — bhagate huye bhi."

zawaya ne is ki traf dekha — aur kuch nahin kahi. magar is bar is ne ise dekha — waqai dekha — aur woh jaan gai ke woh is shakhas par bharosa kar sakti hai.
