# charaghِ tofiq (Chirag-e-Taufeeq)

---

agle do hafate — zawaya aur thٰ ne akthe gazare, subah se shaam tak.

ktab khane ka pichla kamra ab in ka war rom ban chaka tha. deewaron par naqashe lage thay — rohan por ka 1661 ka naqasha, 1875 ka naqasha, 1947 ka naqasha. har naqashe par zawaya ne nishaan lagaئe thay — sarakh panon se, neeli lakiron se.

thٰ purani kitabon mein tareekh khangalata rehta — aur zawaya jadid qanoni dastavezaat tayar karti. is ne adalati drakhawast ka masoda chaar bar likha — har bar thٰ ise parhta, aur woh ise dobara lakhati.

"kyun nahin bas yahi jama kar dete?" is ne poocha — thakan se.

"kyunke agar hum jalad bazi karein ge toh woh humein kachal dein ge," thٰ ne kaha — aur is ke chehray par woh sukoon tha jo zawaya ko prishan kurta tha. "humein ek hi moqa mile ga — aur woh paka hona chahiye."

zawaya ne ise dekha — is ki aankhon ke neeche siyah halaqe thay, magar woh thaka nahin lag raha tha. is ke samne chai ka cup thanda pra tha — woh pichlay kaئi ghanton mein ek ghont bhi nahin piya tha.

"tum kabhi thakte nahin?" zawaya ne poocha.

thٰ ne sar athaya — aur maskraya. "thakta hoon. magar jab mein dekhta hoon ke tum nahin thaktin — toh mujhe bhi chalane ki taqat mal jati hai."

zawaya ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhein mal gaئin. kuch lamhe aise thay jab woh donon khamosh rahe — magar khamoshi mein woh baatein thin jo alfaz mein nahin kahi jaa saktin.

phir thٰ ne nazarein jahaka lein — aur kaghazaat snabhalane laga. "hamare paas kaafi saboot hai. kal hum adalat mein jaa sakte hain."

zawaya ne ise dekha — aur is ke dil mein ek ajib sa ehsaas pida hawa — jaise woh kisi chiz ka hasa ban gai ho jo is se bari thi.
