# charaghِ tofiq (Chirag-e-Taufeeq)

---

woh subah zawaya ko apani maan ka phone aaya — aur is ki duniya phir se hal gai.

"beta —" is ki maan ki aawaz laraz rahi thi — "kal raat koi hamare ghar aaya. kaha — agar tum ne rohan por ka case na chhora toh..."

"kiya amaan? kiya kaha?"

"kaha — tamhari beti ko hum zanda nahin chhorein ge."

zawaya ke haath se phone grane wala tha. is ne apane aap ko snabhala — magar is ke andar sab kuch tot raha tha. "amaan, aap fikr na karein — mein sab theek kar don gi —"

"beta, mein dar gai hoon," is ki maan ne kaha — aur pehli bar is ki aawaz mein woh kamazori thi jo zawaya ne kabhi nahin sni thi. "tum waps aa jao — hum yeh sab chhor dein ge —"

zawaya ne phone band kiya — aur bahut der woh asi tarah baithi rahi, khali aankhon se deewar ko dekhti.

woh thٰ se mili toh is ka chehra dekh kar woh smajah gaya. "kiya hawa?"

"woh miri maan ko dhamaki de rahe hain," zawaya ne kaha — is ki aawaz be jaan thi — "mein yeh nahin kar sakti — mein unhein khatre mein nahin daal sakti —"

thٰ khamosh raha. woh is ke samne bith gaya — aur is ka haath tham liya. "tum dar gai ho."

"nahin — mein bas —" is ki aankhon mein aansoo aa gaye — woh aansoo jo is ne kabhi kisi ko nahin dakhaئe thay — "mein apani maan ko nahin kho sakti — woh mire paas sirf ek hi chiz hai —"

thٰ ne ise khamoshi se sna. is ne ise nahin kaha ke woh drana ghalat hai. is ne ise nahin kaha ke unhein larana chahiye — kyunke woh janta tha ke baz awaqat khamoshi sab se bari taqat hoti hai.

jab woh khamosh hui toh is ne kaha — bahut dheeray:

"mein tamhari maan ko yahan le aaon ga. woh hamare saath rahein gi — jab tak case khatam na ho jaئe. tum akile nahin ho, zawaya."

zawaya ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein paani tha — magar pehli bar aansoo kamazori nahin thay. woh taqat thay.

"kyun?" is ne poocha — bahut dheeray — "tum yeh sab kyun kar rahe ho? mire liay?"

thٰ ne is ki aankhon mein dekha — aur jo kaha woh sach tha, bana kisi banawat ke:

"kyunke tum ne aslam aabad mein woh kiya jo mein dabaئi mein nahin kar ska — tum dat gaئin. aur mein tumhein kabhi akile nahin grane don ga."
