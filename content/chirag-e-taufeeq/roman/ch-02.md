# charaghِ tofiq (Chirag-e-Taufeeq)

---

woh teen hafate zawaya ki zindagi ke badatrin din thay.

har subah woh athati — aur ise yaad aata ke woh be rozagar hai. har raat woh soti — aur ise yaad aata ke is ki sakh khatam ho chaki hai. dramayan mein woh bas ghorati rehti — chhat ko, deewar ko, apane khali haathon ko.

is ne drajanon jagahon par drakhawastin bhejein. kisi ne jawab nahin diya. woh log jinhein woh kabhi apana sathi samajhti thi — woh ab is ke phone nahin athate thay. is ke nam ke saath ek tig lag gaya tha: *raski.* ise nokri nahin mile gi.

is ki maan ne kuch nahin kaha. woh subah swire skol jati, shaam ko waps aati, aur zawaya ke liay chai le kar aati. is ne kabhi nahin poocha — nokri mal gai? kabhi nahin kaha — tumhein dastakhat kar dine chahaئin thay. woh sirf chai la kar rakhti — aur zawaya ko dekh kar maskra deti.

yeh muskurahat zawaya ke dil par chhuri ki tarah lagti thi.

ek din is ki maan ne is ka haath thama — jab woh chai pi rahi thi, khirki ke paas, bahar baarish ho rahi thi.

"beta," is ne kaha — is ki aawaz mein woh narami thi jo sirf maؤan mein hoti hai — "tum jante ho, mein ne tumhein is liay prhaya nahin ke tum bara aadmi b-nau. mein ne is liay prhaya ke tum sacha aadmi b-nau."

zawaya ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein paani tha magar woh maskra rahi thi.

"roti kyun ho?" amaan ne is ke aansoo ponachahe — "tum ne woh kiya jo shih tha. Allah tumhein kabhi tanha nahin chhoray ga."

"magar amaan — qaraz —"

"qaraz ada ho jaئe ga. azt nahin hoti."

---

asi raat zawaya ko apani khala ka phone aaya — rohan por se.

"beta, mein ne sna hai tum mushkil mein ho. yahan aa jao — kuch din. hawa badalay gi, dil badalay ga."

zawaya ne socha. is ke paas khone ke liay kuch nahin tha. shayad kuch pane ke liay bhi nahin — magar thaharana bhi mumkin nahin tha.

"aa rahi hoon, khala."

---

agli subah woh bas mein thi.

aslam aabad is ke peeche ra gaya — pahad, amaratin, woh aafas jahan is ne teen saal kaam kiya tha. sab peeche ra gaya. is ne apane phone ki skrin dekhi — koi message nahin, koi kal nahin. is ki pori zindagi ek khali skrin mein smat gai thi.

is ne hid phone lagaئe aur aqabal ka klam snane lagi — wahi nazm jo is ke walad psnd karte thay:

*staron se aage jahan aur bhi hin*
*abhi ishq ke amathan aur bhi hin*

bas aage brhati rahi — aur zawaya ko nahin maloom tha ke woh ks chiz ki traf jaa rahi hai. sirf atana jaanti thi — woh waps nahin jaa sakti.
