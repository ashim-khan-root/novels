# charaghِ tofiq (Chirag-e-Taufeeq)

---

teen din baad — thٰ raha ho gaya.

zawaya adalat ke bahar khari thi jab woh bahar nkla — wahi safed kurta, wahi muskurahat, magar aankhon mein thodi thakawat thi. is ne zawaya ko dekha — aur woh donon khamosh rahe. bahut der — phir thٰ ne kaha:

"tum ne kar dakhaya."

"hum ne kiya," zawaya ne kaha — aur is bar woh sach thi.

woh saath ktab khane waps aaye — aur jab thٰ ne darwaza khola toh ise laga jaise ghar aa gaya ho. lakri ki khushbu, kitabon ki khamoshi, aur woh roshni jo awanachi khirkion se aati thi — sab waise hi tha jaise is ne chhora tha.

woh donon wohein bith gaye — wohein jahan se unhon ne sharoa kiya tha — aur bahut der tak baatein karte rahe. pichlay dinon ke bare mein , adalat ke bare mein , is bare mein ke kaise Allah ne unhein is raste par daal diya tha.

"mein sochata hoon," thٰ ne kaha — is ki aankhein chhat ki traf thin — "shayad Allah ne humein is liay malaya — kyunke hum donon tanha thay — aur donon sach dhond rahe thay."

zawaya ne is ki traf dekha — aur woh jaanti thi ke woh sach kha raha hai. is ne is ka haath thama — pehli bar b-ghair kisi hachakachahat ke.

"mein tum se shadi karna chahti hoon," is ne kaha — bahut sadgi se — jaise koi bahut purani baat kha rahi ho.

thٰ ne is ki traf dekha — aur maskraya. "mein ne socha tha tum kabhi nahin kaho gi."

"mein ne bhi yahi socha tha."

---

nkah ktab khane mein hawa — hazarat sofi alim ke samne, zawaya ki maan aur khala ke saath, aur chand qasabe walon ke saath.

koi bari taqarib nahin thi. koi dhol nahin tha. sirf sada se kapre thay, aur dil mein shakar tha. jab nkah parha gaya toh zawaya ne mhasos kiya — yeh wahi sukoon tha jo ise thajad ki raat mila tha. yeh ghar wapsi thi — is ke apane aap ki traf, is ke rab ki traf, aur is shakhas ki traf jis ke saath woh sach dhondane nkli thi.

raat ko woh donon ktab khane ki chhat par baithe thay — aasman par stare thay, aur rohan por khamosh tha.

"kiya tumhein pata hai," zawaya ne kaha — is ka sar thٰ ke kandhay par tha — "is din jab mein bas mein baithi thi — mujhe lagta tha mein sab kuch kho chaki hoon."

"aur ab?"

"ab mujhe lagta hai — mein ne sab kuch paya."

thٰ ne is ki traf dekha — aur is ki muskurahat mein woh sukoon tha jo sirf sach se milta hai.

"yeh charagh kabhi nahin bajahe ga," is ne kaha — aur is ki aawaz mein woh yaqeen tha jo pore rohan por ko roshan kar sakta tha.

aur woh donon khamoshi se baithe rahe — kyunke ab khane ke liay alfaz nahin thay. sab kuch kha diya gaya tha.

---

**khatam shad**

*charaghِ tofiq — ek aisi mohabbat ki khani jo sach se sharoa hui, sabr se gazari, aur Allah ke bharose par khatam hui.*
