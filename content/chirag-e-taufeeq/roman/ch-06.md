# charaghِ tofiq (Chirag-e-Taufeeq)

---

do din baad zawaya ko pata chala — aur is ne ghase mein ktab khane ka darwaza khol diya.

"tum jante thay," is ne kaha — is ki aawaz mein ghussa tha, aur is ke peeche dard — "tum jante thay ke wahara kanastrakshanaz is ktab khane ko gra rahi hai — aur tum ne mujhe bataya nahin?"

thٰ ne apani seerhi se neeche dekha — is ke chehray par hairat nahin thi. "tumhein ks ne bataya?"

"qasabe mein sab jante hain. sab siwaye mire." zawaya ne meez par haath maara — "tum ne mujhe kyun nahin bataya?"

thٰ atra — aur is ke samne aa kar khara ho gaya. "kyunke tum abhi theek nahin thin."

"mein theek nahin thi? yeh mira faisla hai — mire bare mein tum kiya jante ho?"

"mein janta hoon ke tum apane saath huye zulam se abhi nkli ho," thٰ ne kaha — is ki aawaz mein ghussa nahin tha, sirf sach — "aur mein janta hoon ke agar tum ghase mein koi faisla karo gi — toh tum wahi ghalati dharaؤ gi jo tum ne aslam aabad mein nahin ki — magar kar sakti thin."

zawaya ne ise ghora — ise laga jaise is ne ise thapr maar diya ho. "tum kiya kha rahe ho?"

"mein kha raha hoon — tum ne aslam aabad mein drast kiya. tum ne ghase mein nahin kiya — tum ne asol par kiya. aur asi liay tum bach gaئin." thٰ ne is ki aankhon mein dekha — "magar ab agar tum ghase mein laro gi — toh tum haar jao gi."

zawaya khamosh rahi — kyunke woh sach tha. is ke andar aag thi, aur woh jalana chahti thi — magar thٰ ise rok raha tha.

"toh tum kiya chahte ho — mein khamosh bithon?"

"mein chahta hoon tum antazar karo. saboot jama karo. phir hamala karo." thٰ is ke atane qareeb tha ke woh is ki saans mhasos kar sakti thi — magar is ne ise chahawa nahin. "aslam aabad mein tum akile thin. yahan tum akile nahin ho."

zawaya ne is ki traf dekha — is ke ghase ke neeche kuch aur tha — ek gramajoshi jisay woh tslim nahin karna chahti thi.

"tum theek ho," is ne kaha — bahut dheeray — jaise alfaz is ke halaq se mushkil se nikal rahe thay — "magar mujhe waqt chahiye — ise qabol krane ke liay."

"tamhare paas waqt hai," thٰ ne kaha. "hamare paas waqt hai."

woh mari aur bahar chali gai — magar is bar is ke qadam pehle se halake thay.
