## manazr 1 — woh alfaz

haron is raat so nahin ska.

saba ke alfaz is ke kaanon mein gonaj rahe thay: *"pehle bhi koi tha. aur woh chala gaya."*

woh kaun tha? woh kyun chala gaya? kiya woh mar gaya tha — ya woh chala gaya tha kyunke woh nahin ra sakta tha?

aur sab se bara sawal — woh maham se ktani mohabbat kurta tha?

haron ko laga jaise woh kisi kanawin mein gr raha hai. jatani gherai mein jata, atana hi andhera hota jata. ise pata tha ke ise rak jaana chahiye — lekin woh rak nahin sakta tha.

is ne Quran khola. lekin is bar aayat ise smajah nahin aaein. woh sirf alfaz thay — be jaan, be roh.

subah hui toh woh utha aur ghar se nkla. ise khod nahin pata tha ke woh kahan jaa raha hai — lekin is ke qadam ise maham ke ghar ki traf le gaye.

woh gali ke kaunay par khara ho gaya.

is ne maham ko dekha — woh balakoni mein khari thi, is ke haath mein chai ka cup tha, lekin woh pi nahin rahi thi. woh daur dekh rahi thi, kisi aisi chiz ko dhond rahi thi jo wahan nahin thi.

haron ne ise aawaz nahin di.

woh mara aur waps chala gaya.

---

woh din is ne ghar mein gazara.

is ki maan ne poocha, "kiya hawa? bimar ho?"

"nahin amaan, bas thak gaya hoon."

lekin woh thaka nahin tha — woh alajah gaya tha.

is ne socha — shayad woh ghalat tha. shayad saba ne mbalagha kiya tha. shayad maham ka mazi atana bara nahin tha jatana woh soch raha tha.

lekin phir ise woh tswir yaad aa gai — jis mein se kisi ko kat diya gaya tha. aur woh qabr — jis par koi nam nahin tha.

aur saba ke aansoo.

woh aansoo jhootay nahin thay.

---

agle do din, haron ne maham se malane se griz kiya.

woh is ke ghar nahin gaya, ise phone nahin kiya, is ke paigham ka jawab nahin diya.

maham ne paigham bhija: *"kiya hawa? tum theek toh ho?"*

is ne jawab nahin diya.

doosray din: *"agar mein ne kuch ghalat kiya hai toh batao."*

is ne phir jawab nahin diya.

woh jaanti thi ke kuch ghalat hai — lekin woh nahin jaanti thi ke kiya. aur haron ise bata nahin sakta tha kyunke woh khod nahin janta tha ke woh kiya mhasos kar raha hai.

kiya woh majaroh tha? kyunke maham ka mazi mein koi aur tha?

kiya woh dra hawa tha? kyunke woh sach janane se drata tha?

kiya woh ghase mein tha? kyunke ise laga ke ise dhoka diya gaya?

woh in mein se koi nahin tha — ya shayad sab kuch tha.

---

teesray din, maham khod is ke ghar aa gai.

woh darwazay par khari thi — is ki aankhon mein aansoo thay jo woh rok rahi thi, is ke hont kanap rahe thay, is ke haath be jaan thay.

haron ne darwaza khola toh is ne kaha — bahut aahasta se, jaise woh grane wali ho — "tum majah se kyun daur ho rahe ho?"

haron ne jawab nahin diya.

"mein ne kuch ghalat kiya? batao. mein smajah jaon gi."

"tum ne kuch ghalat nahin kiya," haron ne kaha. is ki aawaz bhaari thi.

"phir kyun?"

haron ne is ki aankhon mein dekha — aur is ne dekha is khauf ko jisay maham chahapa nahin sakti thi. woh drati thi — ise khone se, ise dhoka dine se, is sach se jisay woh chahapa rahi thi.

"kiya tum mujhe chhor rahe ho?" is ne poocha, aur is ki aawaz tot gai.

"nahin," haron ne kaha. "lekin mujhe thoda waqt chahiye."

maham ne nazarein nichi kar lein. woh smajah gai — lekin is ne kuch nahin kaha.

woh mari aur chali gai.

haron ne ise jate dekha — aur is ke sine mein dard tha jaise koi chhuri chabho rahi ho.

## manazr 2 — woh aai

woh raat haron ne ghar ki chhat par gazari.

is ne aasman ki traf dekha — lahor ka aasman, jahan taaray dhandale thay lekin chaand saaf tha. is ne socha — agar woh rasta chanana hai toh ise sidha hona chahiye. lekin ise pata nahin tha ke sidha rasta kaun sa tha.

subah chaar bajay hoon ge jab is ne seerhion par qadamon ki aawaz sni.

woh mara — aur saba thi.

woh chhat par aai aur is ke qareeb khari ho gai. is ne is ki traf nahin dekha — woh aasman ki traf dekh rahi thi.

haron ko kuch smajah nahin aaya. woh ise kabhi is ghar mein nahin aai thi — aur ab woh raat ko, chhat par, is ke paas khari thi.

"tumhein sach chahiye?" is ne poocha. is ki aawaz mein woh sardi nahin thi — sirf ek bhaari thakawat thi.

haron ne is ki traf dekha.

"toh sno. lekin is ke baad tum waps nahin jaa sako ge."

woh bith gai — is ke qareeb, lekin atani daur ke in ke dramayan fasla tha. is ne apane ghatanon ko pakar liya aur is tarah bith gai jaise woh bahut thaki hui thi — bahut zyada thaki hui.

"maham ko ek larkay se mohabbat thi," is ne kaha. "is ka nam slim tha. woh fotografr tha. woh bahut acha tha. mein ne in ki mangani krawaئi thi — lekin woh maham se mohabbat kurta tha."

saba ne ek lambi saans li.

"mein ne unhein roka nahin. mein ne unhein jaane diya. kyunke maham ki khushi mire liay sab kuch thi."

"aur phir?"

"aur phir woh mar gaya. hadasa — motr we par. woh raat jab woh bhagane wale thay, maham nahin gai — woh dar gai. woh akela gaya — aur waps nahin aaya."

saba ki aankhon mein aansoo thay — lekin woh bha nahin rahe thay.

"maham ne is ke baad kabhi khod ko maaf nahin kiya. woh sochati hai ke agar woh is raat gai hoti toh woh zanda hota. aur woh theek kehti hai."

khamoshi.

"mein ne ise bachaya," saba ne kaha. "is ke marane ke baad, maham tot gai thi. mein ne ise snabhala — ise khalaya, ise slaya, ise jine par majabor kiya. mein is ki maan ban gai, is ki behen, is ka saia."

is ne haron ki traf dekha — pehli bar, sidhi aankhon mein.

"aur ab tum aaye ho. aur woh phir se ji rahi hai. lekin agar tum ne ise chhora — agar tum ne ise phir se tora — toh woh dobara nahin uthe gi. aur mein woh manazr dobara nahin dekh sakti."

haron khamosh raha. is ke paas alfaz nahin thay.

"reho ya jao — tamhari marazi," saba ne kaha. is ki aawaz balakl sarad thi. "bas aadhe dil se mat reho. woh ek bar tot chaki hai — doosri bar nahin bachchay gi."

woh uthi. is ne haron ki traf dekha — aur is ki aankhon mein woh thakawat thi jisay woh paanch saal se athaئe huye thi.

"bas. yahi sach hai."

woh mari aur chali gai.

haron chhat par akela ra gaya.

## manazr 3 — wapsi

saba ki baaton ne ise badal diya.

ise smajah aa gaya — maham sirf ek larki nahin thi jo is se mohabbat karti thi. woh ek zakham thi jo bhar raha tha. ek aisi roh jo kabhi mar chaki thi aur dobara jina sikh rahi thi.

aur saba — woh woh deewar thi jis ne ise grane se bachaya tha.

woh agle din maham ke ghar gaya.

is ne darwaza khatakhataya — toh maham ne khola. is ki aankhein soji hui thin, is ka chehra peela tha. woh raat bhar roi thi — yeh is ke chehray par likha tha.

"tum aa gaye?" is ne poocha. is ki aawaz mein hirani thi — aur ek chahapi hui umeed.

"haan."

"mein ne socha tum nahin ao ge."

haron ne is ka haath pakar liya. "mein aaya hoon. aur mein nahin jaon ga."

maham ki aankhon mein aansoo aa gaye. lekin is bar woh gham ke nahin thay — woh rahat ke thay.

woh andar gaye. maham ne is ka haath nahin chhora. woh sofe par bith gaye — aur maham ne apana sar is ke kandhay par rakh diya.

"mein drati thi," is ne kaha. "bahut drati thi ke tum mujhe chhor do ge. jaise sab chhor dete hain."

"mein tumhein nahin chhoron ga."

"wada?"

"wada."

maham roya — bahut der tak. aur haron ise snabhale raha, is ke baalon ko shalata raha, ise yaqeen dalata raha ke woh nahin jaئe ga.

woh is raat bahut der tak saath rahe. baatein kin — mazi ke bare mein nahin, balke mastaqabl ke bare mein. is bare mein ke woh kiya karein ge, kahan jaein ge, kaise jaئin ge.

maham ne kaha, "kiya tum mujhe ek khani snaؤ ge?"

"kisi khani?"

"ek achi khani. jis ka akhatatam acha ho."

haron maskraya. "ek bar ek larka tha jo bahut daur chala gaya tha. woh bhatak gaya tha, woh kho gaya tha, woh smajah gaya tha ke wapsi mumkin nahin hai."

maham ne aankhein band kar lein.

"aur phir ek din, ise ek larki mili — jis ki aankhon mein woh ghar tha jisay woh bhol gaya tha. aur woh waps aa gaya."

"kiya woh phir khawaya?"

"nahin. kyunke is bar ise rasta mal gaya tha."

maham ne aankhein kholin aur ise dekha. is ki aankhon mein chandani thi.

"mein..." is ne nazarein nichi kar lein. phir is ne kaha — bahut aahasta se — "mein tum se mohabbat karti hoon."

haron ka dil tham gaya.

yeh pehli bar tha jab is ne yeh alfaz sne thay — sna se bhi nahin sne thay. sna ne kabhi mohabbat nahin ki thi — woh sirf is ke saath thi jab tak ise zarorat thi.

lekin maham — woh sach mein mohabbat karti thi.

aur is ne kaha — b-ghair sochay, b-ghair kisi dar ke — "mein bhi tum se mohabbat kurta hoon."

woh maskraئi. aur is muskurahat mein woh sab kuch tha jo haron ne kabhi dhonda tha.

## hasa 4 — woh chiz

woh raat gehri thi jab haron maham ke ghar se nkla.

is ka dil halka tha, is ke qadam masthakam thay, is ke andar ek sukoon tha jo is ne mhinon baad mhasos kiya tha. woh maham se mohabbat kurta tha — aur ise yaqeen tha ke yeh sach tha.

woh gali se guzar raha tha ke is ka paaon kisi chiz se takraya.

is ne neeche dekha — gali ki roshni mein kuch chamak raha tha. is ne jahak kar dekha — ek chaabi thi. purani, zang aalod, motr saئikl ki chaabi.

woh ise utha kar dekhta raha — phir is ki ngahin is chiz par gaئin jo chaabi ke saath thi.

woh tswir gali mein pri thi — shayad kisi ne phink di thi, shayad kisi ne kho di thi, shayad kisi ne chahapaئi thi aur ab woh mal gai thi.

haron ne ise athaya.

is ne dekha — aur is ki saans rak gai.

tswir mein do log thay.

saba — aur slim.

woh ek saath khare thay. saba is ki traf jahaki hui thi, is ka haath is ke kandhay par tha, aur woh donon maskra rahe thay. sach mein maskra rahe thay — jaise in ke dramayan koi raz nahin tha, koi dard nahin tha, sirf khushi thi.

yeh woh tswir nahin thi jo maham ke ghar mein phari gai thi. yeh koi aur thi.

aur is mein saba thi — maham nahin.

saba aur slim. saath.

haron ka haath kanap gaya.

is ne saba ki baaton par yaqeen kiya tha. is ne man liya tha ke slim maham se mohabbat kurta tha, aur saba ne sirf apani behen ki khatr qarabani di thi.

lekin yeh tswir —

yeh tswir kuch aur kha rahi thi.

woh donon ek doosray ke qareeb thay, in ki aankhon mein woh chamak thi jo sirf mohabbat mein hoti hai. woh sirf mangitr nahin thay — woh ek doosray se pyar karte thay.

toh phir saba ne jhoot kyun bola tha?

woh raat gali mein khara raha — zang aalod chaabi ek haath mein, tswir doosray haath mein, aur is ka dil hazaaron sawalon se bhara hawa tha.

saba ne kaha tha ke woh slim se mohabbat nahin karti thi.

lekin tswir jhoot nahin bolati thi.

toh phir — saba kyun jhoot boli thi?

aur agar woh slim se mohabbat karti thi, toh is ne ise maham ko kyun diya?

aur sab se bara sawal — slim waqai kyun mara tha?

haron ne tswir ko jib mein rakha aur chaabi ko mathi mein band kar liya.

woh ghar waps aaya toh is ka dil bojahal tha.

is ne socha tha ke woh sach janta hai.

lekin ab ise ehsaas hawa — ise kuch nahin ptha tha.

aur jo kuch bhi sach tha — woh is se kahein zyada ghera aur kahein zyada dradanak tha jatana is ne socha tha.

woh bistar par lit gaya — lekin is ki aankhein khuli thin. is ke haath mein woh chaabi thi, aur is ke zhan mein woh tswir thi.

saba aur slim.

saath.

maskrate huye.

aur woh jaan gaya — ise phir se sach dhondana ho ga.

is bar, pora sach.
