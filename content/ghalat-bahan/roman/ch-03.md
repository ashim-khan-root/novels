## manazr 1 — woh aurat

woh saba thi.

haron ko is ka nam pehle hi pata tha — is ki maan ne bata diya tha — lekin is ne ise pehchan liya is se pehle ke koi nam is ke zhan mein aata. woh chal. woh kapre. woh aankhein jin mein koi jazbaat nahin thay lekin sab kuch chahapa hawa tha.

is ne apana taaraf nahin kraya.

is ne haron ki traf dekha bhi nahin. is ne bas maham ka haath pakra — mazaboti se, jaise maham bachchi ho jo kho jaئe gi — aur ise ghasit kar le gai.

maham ne peeche mar kar dekha. is ki aankhon mein mazrat thi. ek halki si sharmindagi. gawaya kha rahi ho: *ia woh nahin hai jo tum soch rahe ho.*

saba ne peeche mar kar nahin dekha.

haron wohein khara raha. bazaar ka rash is ke arad grad tha — sabzi frosh ki aawazein, bachon ki hansi, daur se aanay wali motr saئikl ki aawaz — lekin woh in sab se alag tha.

ise sirf woh nazar yaad thi.

baraf. hasab. antabaa.

---

woh ghar waps aaya toh is ki maan bawarachi khane mein thin. is ne kuch nahin kaha. lekin woh sara din is ke bare mein sochata raha — saba ke bare mein.

woh kaun thi? woh atani sarad kyun thi? woh kyun chahti thi ke woh maham se daur rahe?

raat ko woh chhat par leta tha. is ne asman ko phone kiya.

"asman, mujhe saba ke bare mein batao."

phone par khamoshi thi. phir asman ne kaha, "ks saba?"

"wahi. pros wali. bari behen."

phir khamoshi. lambi khamoshi.

"tum kyun poochh rahe ho?"

"bas."

asman ne aah bhari. "yar, mein ne tum se kaha tha — in larkion se daur reho. khas tore par is se."

"kyun?"

"is ka mazi hai. bahut bhaari mazi."

"ks qism ka mazi?"

asman ne jawab dine ki bajaye kaha, "tum ne kabhi grayawan ka ghar dekha hai? woh gali ke aakhar mein wala purana makaan?"

"haan."

"wahan paanch saal pehle ek larka rehta tha. slim. tum ne ise nahin jaana — tum is waqt ke ail mein thay. woh saba ka mangitr tha."

haron ka dil rak gaya.

"aur?"

"aur woh mar gaya."

"kaise?"

"ek hadasa. motr we par. is ki motr saئikl darakht se jaa takraئi. kehte hain ke woh bahut tez chala raha tha."

asman ne kuch aur kaha — lekin is ki aawaz mein kuch tha, jaise woh pori baat nahin kha raha tha.

haron ne poocha, "aur saba?"

"saba is ke marane ke baad badal gai. pehle bhi sakht thi — lekin ab toh jaise baraf ban gai hai. kabhi kisi se baat nahin karti, kabhi kahein nahin jati, bas masjid aur ghar."

haron khamosh raha.

"sno haron," asman ne kaha, "tum maham se dosti rakho, lekin saba se daur reho. woh aurat khatranak hai."

haron ne phone band kiya aur aasman ki traf dekha.

khatranak?

woh sirf ek aurat thi jis ne apana mangitr khawaya tha. ek aurat jo apani behen ki hafazt kar rahi thi.

lekin phir woh brafani nazarein kyun?

## manazr 2 — masjid

ek hafta guzar gaya.

haron ne maham ko nahin dekha. woh jaan bojah kar is gali se nahin guzara jahan is ka ghar tha. is ne khod ko masroof rakha — purane doston se mila, apane skichaz makamal kiay, apane walad ke saath bith kar baatein kin.

lekin woh saba ko nahin bhol ska.

is ki woh nazar. asman ka woh lhaja. aur woh nam — slim — jis ne hawa mein latak kar ise sochane par majabor kar diya tha.

jamaa ka din tha.

haron jamaa ki namaz ke liay masjid gaya. namaz khatam hui toh woh waps aanay laga — lekin raste mein is ne ek aawaz sni.

ek aurat ki aawaz. Quran prh rahi thi.

woh rak gaya.

yeh woh aawaz nahin thi jo woh snana chahta tha. yeh woh aawaz thi jis ne ise jakr liya.

woh sora alazhayٰ prh rahi thi. is ki aawaz mein atana dard tha ke haron ke qadam apane aap rak gaye.

"wَalazُّhٰi"

qism hai din ki roshni ki.

"wَalَّyْlِ aِzَa sَjٰi"

aur raat ki jab woh chaha jaئe.

woh aahasta aahasta is traf brha jahan se aawaz aa rahi thi. masjid ke pichlay hase mein ek kamra tha — bachon ke liay Quran ki klas.

is ne darwazay mein jahanka.

woh wahan thi. saba.

is ke haath mein Quran tha aur is ki aankhein band thin. woh prh rahi thi — lekin woh sirf prh nahin rahi thi, woh har lafz ke saath ji rahi thi. is ka chehra, jo hamesha baraf ki tarah jama rehta tha, ab paghala hawa tha. is ki aankhon ke kanaron par nami thi.

"aَlَmْ yَjِdْkَ yَtِyْmًa fَٰwٰi"

kiya is ne tumhein yatim nahin paya tha, phir tumhein panaa di?

haron ne mhasos kiya ke woh saans roke khara hai.

is aawaz mein atani tanhai thi, atana dard, atani mohabbat — ke woh bhol gaya ke woh kahan hai.

saba ne aankhein kholin.

aur ise dekh liya.

is ka chehra ek lamhe mein band ho gaya. baraf waps aa gai. is ne Quran band kiya, bachon se kaha, "aaj ke liay atana kaafi hai," aur bahar nikal aai.

woh haron ke paas se gazari — atani qareeb ke is ke karte ka kanara is se chhu gaya — lekin is ne ise ek nazar bhi nahin di.

woh kamray se bahar nikal kar gali mein chali gai.

haron wohein khara raha. is ne mhasos kiya — is ki ungliyan be thasha kanap rahi thin.

woh is aurat ko smajah nahin sakta tha.

ek traf woh Quran prh rahi thi jaise is ka dil takre takre ho raha ho. doosri traf woh ise dekh kar baraf ban gai.

woh kaun si aurat thi jo ek saath atani naram aur atani sakht ho sakti thi?

---

is shaam, haron ghar waps aaya toh woh prishan tha. is ki maan ne poocha, "kiya hawa?"

"kuch nahin, amaan."

"tum jhoot bolate ho toh tamhari aankhein tumhein pakar deti hain."

haron khamosh raha.

"kuch hawa hai masjid mein?"

"nahin... mein ne saba ko dekha. Quran parha rahi thi."

is ki maan ke haath rak gaye. unhon ne kuch nahin kaha — lekin in ka chehra kuch kha gaya.

"kiya hawa amaan?"

"kuch nahin, beta."

"aap bhi wahi kehti hain jo asman kehta hai. sab kuch chahapate ho."

is ki maan ne is ki traf dekha. lambe arase baad pehli bar, in ki aankhon mein kuch aur tha — woh nazakt jo woh kabhi nahin dakhati thin.

"hum nahin chahapa rahe, beta. hum tumhein bacha rahe hain."

"ks se?"

is ki maan ne jawab nahin diya. woh bawarachi khane mein chali gaئin.

haron baitha raha.

*"hum tumhein bacha rahe hain."*

ks se? saba se? maham se? ya is mazi se jo in donon ke dramayan chahapa hawa tha?

## manazr 3 — kitab

agle din, atawar tha.

haron apane kamray mein baitha tha jab is ne darwazay ki dastak sni. woh bahar nkla toh is ki maan ne darwaza khola tha. aur dhaliz par saba khari thi.

is ke haath mein ek kitab thi.

"yeh lijaye, amaan jaan. aap ki kitab."

is ki maan ne kitab li. "bahut shukriya, beta. andar ao gi?"

"nahin amaan jaan, mein bas yeh kitab dine aai thi."

saba ki ngahin farsh par thin. is ne haron ki traf nahin dekha — jaise woh wahan tha hi nahin.

"chai pi loo," is ki maan ne asarar kiya.

ek lamhe ki khamoshi. phir saba ne kaha, "theek hai."

woh andar aai. lekin woh sofe par nahin baithi — woh darwazay ke qareeb hi khari rahi, jaise kisi bhi lamhe bhagane ke liay tayar ho.

is ki maan chai la-ne bawarachi khane mein chali gaئin.

saba aur haron. kamray mein. akile.

khamoshi. lambi khamoshi.

haron ne kaha, "tum Quran bahut khubsurat parhti ho."

saba ne sar athaya. pehli bar is ne ise dekha — lekin is ki aankhon mein koi jazbaat nahin thay. sirf ek jami hui sardi.

"tum masjid mein aaye thay?"

"haan. mein ne tumhein sna. sora alazhayٰ prh rahi thin."

saba ne koi jawab nahin diya. is ka chehra balakl be has tha.

"tamhari aawaz mein bahut dard tha," haron ne kaha. b-ghair sochay.

saba ki aankhon mein kuch chamaka — ek lamhe ke liay — phir woh bajah gaya.

"tum mujhe nahin jante," is ne kaha. is ki aawaz balakl sarad thi. "aur tumhein mujhe janane ki zarorat bhi nahin hai."

haron ko kuch khane ka moqa nahin mila. is ki maan chai le kar aa gaئin.

saba ne chai pi. bahut tizi se. is ne do ghont piye aur payali neeche rakh di.

"bahut shukriya, amaan jaan. mujhe jaana hai."

woh uthi. lekin jab woh darwazay tak phanachi, woh raki.

is ne mar kar dekha — haron ko nahin, is ki maan ko.

"amaan jaan," is ne kaha, "ek baat keon?"

"haan beta, kyun nahin."

saba ki nazar ab haron par thi. pehli bar — sidhi aankhon mein.

"achay log jaldi bharosa kar lete hain."

is ki aawaz mein koi ghussa nahin tha. koi dhamaki nahin thi. sirf ek sach tha — ek talakh sach jisay woh ji rahi thi.

is ne haron ki traf dekha — bas ek nazar — aur phir bahar chali gai.

darwaza band hawa toh kamray mein khamoshi thi.

haron ki maan ne is ki traf dekha. "tum ne ise kiya kaha?"

"kuch nahin. bas yeh kaha ke woh acha Quran parhti hai."

is ki maan ne sar halaya. aahasta se. jaise koi aisi baat smajah gai ho jo haron ko nahin smajah aai.

## hasa 4 — sawal

haron is raat so nahin ska.

woh saba ke jamale ke bare mein sochata raha: *achahe log jaldi bharosa kar lete hain.*

kiya woh ise khabradar kar rahi thi? kiya woh ise dhamaki de rahi thi? kiya woh apani behen ki hafazt kar rahi thi — ya woh khod kisi chiz se drati thi?

woh utha aur bahar nkla. raat ka waqt tha, glayan snasan thin. woh b-ghair sochay is gali ki traf chal pra jahan maham aur saba rehti thin.

in ke ghar ke samne woh rak gaya. andar roshni thi — ek kamray mein, oopar wali manazl par. khirki khuli thi aur parday hawa mein lhara rahe thay.

is ne dekha — saba khirki ke paas baithi thi. is ke haath mein Quran tha lekin woh prh nahin rahi thi. woh khirki ke bahar dekh rahi thi — asi gali mein jahan haron khara tha.

kiya is ne ise dekh liya tha?

is ka chehra andhire mein dhandala tha. lekin haron ne mhasos kiya — woh janta tha ke saba ise dekh rahi hai. aur woh kuch nahin kar rahi thi. bas dekh rahi thi.

woh waps chala aaya.

---

agle din, is ne maham ko dikhane ka faisla kiya. woh jaanna chahta tha — is ki behen ke bare mein sach.

woh is gali mein gaya jahan woh rehti thin. is ne dekha maham bahar khari thi — phool de rahi thi podon ko. woh ise dekh kar maskraئi.

"aap?"

"mein tum se milna chahta tha."

is ki muskurahat thodi si phiki pr gai. "kyun?"

"bas. chalo kahein baithte hain."

woh qareeb hi ek parak mein gaye. binach par baithe. kuch der khamoshi rahi. phir haron ne kaha, "tamhari behen kal hamare ghar aai thi."

maham ka chehra badal gaya. is ki aankhon mein dar tha.

"is ne kuch kaha?"

"haan. is ne kaha — achay log jaldi bharosa kar lete hain."

maham ne nazarein nichi kar lein. is ke haath kanap rahe thay.

"maham," haron ne aahasta se kaha, "tamhari behen kyun aisi hai? woh atani sarad kyun hai?"

maham ne kuch der jawab nahin diya. phir is ne kaha, "woh bahut achi hain."

waqafa.

"sirf... thodi sakht hain."

haron ne is ki aankhon mein dekha. maham ki aankhein kuch aur kha rahi thin — lekin is ke alfaz kuch aur thay.

"tum kuch chahapa rahi ho," haron ne kaha.

maham ne sar halaya. "nahin."

"maham —"

"nahin," is ne kaha, is bar mazaboti se. "mein kuch nahin chahapa rahi."

lekin is ki aankhon ne ise jhoota sabat kar diya.

haron ne mazid asarar nahin kiya. woh khamosh baitha raha — is aurat ke bare mein sochata raha jis ne ise khabradar kiya tha, aur is aurat ke bare mein jo ise jhoot bol rahi thi.

woh donon behnein thin.

lekin woh ek doosray se atani makhatalaf kyun thin?

aur sab se aham sawal — woh mazi kiya tha jis ne unhein aisa bana diya tha?

---

woh raat bahut der tak jaagta raha. is ne Quran khola — wahi sora alazhayٰ jo saba ne parhi thi. is ne parha:

"fََmَّa alْyَtِyْmَ fَlَa tَqْهَrْ"

toh yatim par skhati na karo.

"wََmَّa alasَّaaئِlَ fَlَa tَnْهَrْ"

aur saئal ko na jaharako.

is ne aaitin band kin aur saba ke bare mein socha.

ktani ajib thi woh aurat. ek traf woh ise daur bhaga rahi thi, doosri traf woh Quran ki woh aayat prh rahi thi jo yatimon aur saئalon ke bare mein thin.

woh kaun sa yatim tha jisay is ne panaa di thi?

woh kaun sa saئal tha jisay is ne nahin jaharaka tha?

ya shayad woh khod yatim thi — is ke baap ka saia sar se uth gaya tha, is ka mangitr mar gaya tha, aur woh apani behen ki hafazt ke liay akile khari thi.

haron ne aankhein band kin.

woh is aurat se drata tha. lekin woh is ki traf khanacha bhi jaa raha tha — is tarah jaise koi baraf ki traf khinchay, yeh jante huye ke woh thandi hai, lekin yeh bhi jante huye ke is ke neeche paani hai, aur paani ke neeche kuch aur bhi hai.

kuch aisa jo woh smajah nahin ska tha — likan mhasos kar sakta tha.

---

agle din, woh masjid gaya. saba ko dikhane ke liay nahin — lekin jab is ne is ki aawaz sni, toh woh rak gaya.

woh phir Quran prh rahi thi. is bar sora marim.

"kَهَfۡ ۚ wَalarَّqِyۡmِ"

ashab khaf aur ashab raqim.

is ki aawaz mein wahi dard tha — lekin is bar kuch aur bhi tha. astafayٰ. sabr. woh sabr jo sirf is aurat ko aata hai jis ne zindagi ke sab se baray dhoke ko qabol kar liya ho.

haron masjid ke shan mein bith gaya. is ne aankhein band kin aur sirf sna.

woh is aurat ko nahin smajah sakta tha. lekin woh ise snana chahta tha.

kyunke is ki aawaz mein woh sab kuch tha jo is ke chehray par nahin tha — dard, mohabbat, tanhai, aur woh khamosh sach jisay woh kisi ko nahin bata sakti thi.

jab woh khatam hui toh haron ne aankhein kholin. woh bahar nikal rahi thi — lekin is bar is ne ise dekh kar sar nahin phira.

is ne sirf ek nazar daali — wahi brafani nazar — aur chali gai.

lekin is bar, baraf ke neeche kuch aur tha. kuch aisa jo haron ne pehli bar dekha tha.

thakan.

woh aurat thak chaki thi. atana bojah athaئe huye ke woh grane wali thi — lekin woh grati nahin thi, kyunke agar woh gr gai toh is ki behen bhi gr jaئe gi.

haron ne apane aap se kaha: *min ise jaanon ga.*

chahe woh ise daur dhakile, chahe woh baraf ban jaئe, chahe woh ise khabradar kare — woh is aurat ko jaane b-ghair nahin rahe ga.

kyunke baraf ke neeche jo kuch tha, woh ise dekhna chahta tha.

aur shayad — sirf shayad — woh ise bachana chahta tha.
