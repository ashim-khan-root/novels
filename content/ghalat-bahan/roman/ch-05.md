## manazr 1 — woh tswir

haron ne sab se pehle woh tswir dekhi thi — maham ke ghar wali, jis mein se kisi ko kat diya gaya tha.

is din is ne kuch nahin poocha tha. lekin woh tswir is ke zhan mein thi — ek khali jagah jahan koi hota tha, lekin ab nahin tha.

ek hafta guzar gaya.

haron aur maham roz milte thay. chai pite, baatein karte, kitabon ke bare mein, khwabon ke bare mein, mastaqabl ke bare mein. maham khul rahi thi — har roz thodi aur khul rahi thi, jaise koi kli jo dheeray dheeray khalati hai.

lekin haron ki aankhein woh khali jagah dhondati rehti thin.

ek din, woh maham ke ghar tha. maham chai bana rahi thi. woh kamray mein akela tha — aur woh tswir deewar par lagi hui thi.

woh utha aur qareeb gaya.

frim purani thi. shishe par grad jami hui thi. tswir mein teen log thay — maham, saba, aur ek aurat jo shayad in ki maan thi. lekin ek traf woh jagah thi jahan se kisi ko kata gaya tha. kaghaz phata hawa tha — zor se, ghase se, ya shayad dard se.

woh sirf do log ra gaye thay. teesra sirf ek saia tha — haath ka ek hasa, kandhay ka ek kanara, jo katate waqt ra gaya tha.

woh haath marad ka tha.

haron ko yaqeen tha.

"kiya dekh rahe ho?"

maham ki aawaz ne ise chonka diya. woh peeche mara — maham darwazay mein khari thi, chai ki tre haath mein, is ka chehra be rang.

"yeh tswir," haron ne kaha. "is mein se kisi ko kata gaya hai."

maham ne tre meez par rakhi. is ke haath kanap rahe thay.

"koi nahin," is ne kaha. bahut jaldi. bahut tizi se. "koi nahin tha."

"maham —"

"yeh purani tswir hai. mein bhol gai thi ke yeh yahan hai."

lekin is ki aankhein jhoot bol rahi thin. woh wohein khari thi, lekin is ka chehra bata raha tha ke woh kahein aur thi — mazi mein, is din jab woh tswir phari gai thi.

haron ne mazid nahin poocha. woh waps bith gaya. chai pi. lekin is ka zhan is khali jagah par tha — aur is haath par jo kata gaya tha.

woh raat jab woh ghar waps aaya toh is ne apani maan se poocha, "amaan, pros wali larkion ke bare mein kuch aur batao."

is ki maan ne is ki traf dekha — lambe arase tak.

"kiya poochna chahte ho?"

"in ke ghar mein ek tswir hai — jis mein se kisi ko kat diya gaya hai. kaun tha woh?"

is ki maan ka chehra band ho gaya.

"beta, kuch sawal aise hote hain jin ke jawab nahin hote."

"matalab?"

"matalab yeh ke tum in larkion ke mazi mein mat jao. woh tumhein ngl jaئe ga."

haron khamosh raha. lekin is ne faisla kar liya tha — woh jaane ga. chahe kuch bhi ho.

## manazr 2 — prosn

agle din, haron ne pros ki ek boorhi aurat se baat ki — muhtarama rashida, jo pachas saal se is gali mein rehti thin, aur har ghar ki khani jaanti thin.

muhtarama rashida ne jawab nahin diya. unhon ne tsabih uthai aur aankhein band kar lein.

"bas chala gaya, beta. aur is ke jaane ke baad, yeh gali kabhi pehle jisi nahin hui."

haron wahan aur kuch der baitha raha — lekin ise mazid kuch nahin mila. muhtarama rashida ne apani balakoni band kar li thi, aur apane saath woh raz bhi le gai thin jo unhon ne kabhi kisi ko nahin bataya tha.

"sab ko pata tha. lekin kisi ne kuch nahin kaha. kyunke saba... saba ne kuch nahin kaha. woh chap rahi. is ne apani mangani tore di — aur kaha ke ise slim se mohabbat nahin hai."

"nahin, yar. woh jhoot bol rahi thi. is ne apani behen ki khatr jhoot bola."

haron ke sine mein dard utha.

"aur phir?"

"aur phir woh raat aai. jab slim aur maham ko bhagana tha. maham nahin gai. woh dar gai. aur slim chala gaya — akela. aur waps nahin aaya."

asman ki aawaz mein kuch tha — woh kuch jo woh kha nahin raha tha.

"kiya tumhein lagta hai ke is ne khodakshi ki?"

khamoshi. lambi khamoshi.

"yar, woh sawal hai jis ka jawab sirf ek shakhas jaanti hai."

"saba?"

"haan. saba is raat wahan thi. woh slim se malane gai thi — ise manane, ya ise rokane, ya shayad ise batane ke woh jaane de rahi hai. aur is ne dekha ke slim ne kiya kiya."

haron ne aankhein band kin.

"saba hi wahad gawah hai," asman ne kaha. "aur is ne kabhi kisi ko nahin bataya ke is raat kiya hawa."

## manazr 3 — qabrastan

agle din haron masjid gaya — lekin is ka dil nahin laga. woh bahar nkla aur b-ghair sochay qabrastan ki traf chal pra.

woh qabrastan sheher ke purane hase mein tha — jahan darakht purane thay aur qabron par ghas ag aai thi. woh qabron ke dramayan chalta raha, pata nahin kse dhond raha tha.

aur phir is ne ise dekha.

saba.

ek qabr ke samne baithi thi. is ke kandhay jahake huye thay, is ka sar nicha tha. woh ro rahi thi — khamoshi se, jaise kisi ko pata na chale.

woh qabr balakl sada thi. koi nam nahin tha — sirf ek patthar, b-ghair kisi likhai ke.

haron kuch der khara raha. phir is ne aage brhane ka faisla kiya.

is ke qadamon ki aawaz sn kar saba ne sar athaya.

is ka chehra ek lamhe mein badal gaya. aansoo khashak ho gaye, baraf waps aa gai, aur is ki aankhon mein ghussa tha.

"tum yahan kyun ho?"

yeh sawal nahin tha — alazam tha.

haron rak gaya. "mein..."

"yeh jagah tamhari nahin hai. yeh qabr tamhari nahin hai. aur yeh gham bhi tamhara nahin hai."

"saba —"

"jao yahan se."

is ki aawaz kanap rahi thi — lekin yeh kamazori nahin thi, yeh is ghase ka aakhari kanara tha jisay woh snabhale huye thi.

haron ne ek qadam peeche hataya. phir doosra.

lekin jab woh mara, saba ne kaha — bahut aahasta se, jaise woh khod se baat kar rahi ho — "woh chala gaya. aur mein yahan hoon. hamesha yahan."

haron rak gaya. is ne peeche mar kar dekha — saba ne phir se aankhein jahaka li thin, is ke aansoo phir se bha rahe thay.

woh ise akela chhor kar chala gaya.

lekin woh sari raat is ke alfaz dharata raha: *"woh chala gaya. aur mein yahan hoon. hamesha yahan."*
