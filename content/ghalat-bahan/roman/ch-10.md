## woh mulaqaat

subah hui toh haron maham ke ghar gaya.

maham ne darwaza khola — is ka chehra peela tha, is ki aankhein soji hui thin. woh raat bhar nahin soi thi — yeh is ke chehray par likha tha.

woh sab kuch jaanti thi.

haron ne ise kuch nahin bataya tha — lekin behnein ek doosray ko jaanti hain. woh jaanti thi ke is ki behen ne aakharakar sach bata diya tha.

woh andar aai — aur donon b-ghair kuch kahe bith gaye. khamoshi lambi thi.

phir maham ne kaha — "tum mujhe chhor do ge?"

haron ne is ki traf dekha. is ki aankhon mein khauf tha — wahi khauf jo is raat bhi tha jab woh slim ke paas na jaa saki.

"mein nahin janta," haron ne kaha. sach.

maham ne nazarein nichi kar lein. is ki aankhon mein aansoo thay — lekin woh behenay nahin paa rahe thay.

"mein dar gai thi," is ne kaha. "is raat — aur aaj bhi. mein hamesha drati hoon. saba woh hai jo drati nahin."

"woh bhi drati hai," haron ne kaha. "lekin is ka dar ise rokta nahin."

maham ne sar athaya. is ki aankhon mein kuch tha — shayad pehli bar, woh smajah rahi thi ke haron kiya kha raha hai.

"tum saba ko chano ge?"

sawal sidha tha — aur dradanak.

haron ne kuch der socha. phir is ne kaha — "mein kisi ko nahin chanon ga."

"kyun?"

"mohabbat intikhab nahin hoti, maham."

maham khamosh rahi.

"mein tum se mohabbat kurta hoon," haron ne kaha. "lekin pehle khod ko maaf kar loo."

woh utha — aur darwazay tak gaya.

maham ne kaha — "kiya tum phir kabhi ao ge?"

haron ne peeche mar kar dekha. "jab tum khod se mal loo gi — tab mein tum se malon ga."

woh bahar nikal gaya. maham kamray mein akeli ra gai — is ke aansoo aakharakar bha nikalay.

lekin is bar, woh sirf gham ke nahin thay — woh smajah ke bhi thay.

## woh qabr

haron qabrastan gaya.

woh janta tha ke saba wahan ho gi — aur woh ghalat nahin tha.

woh is qabr ke paas baithi thi — jis par koi nam nahin tha. is ke haath mein Quran tha, aur woh prh rahi thi — khamoshi se, lekin is ke hont harakt kar rahe thay.

haron is ke paas jaa kar bith gaya.

saba ne is ki traf nahin dekha — lekin is ne ise roka bhi nahin. woh parhti rahi, aur haron khamosh baitha raha.

kuch der baad, saba ne Quran band kiya aur ise apani god mein rakh liya. is ne haron ki traf dekha — aankhon mein baraf nahin thi, ghussa nahin tha, sirf ek khamosh sawal tha.

"tum ne maham ko chhor diya?"

"mein ne kisi ko nahin chhora," haron ne kaha. "mein ne ise bataya — ise pehle khod se milna ho ga."

saba ne sar halaya — jaise woh smajah gai ho.

"aur tum?" is ne poocha. "tum ne khod se mal liya?"

haron khamosh raha. phir is ne kaha — "mein sikh raha hoon."

woh kuch der khamosh baithe rahe — qabr ke paas, is shakhas ki yaad mein jis ne in teenon ki zandagayan badal di thin.

phir haron ne kaha:

"tum ne ise maara nahin, saba."

saba ne is ki traf dekha.

"tum ne ise rokane ki koshash ki — aur jab na rok saki toh apani behen ko bachaya."

saba ki aankhon mein nami thi.

"mein ne kisi ko nahin bachaya," is ne kaha — wahi jumla jo is ne hamesha kaha tha. "mein sirf zanda hoon."

"kabhi kabhi zanda rehna hi bacha lena hai," haron ne kaha.

saba ne is ki traf dekha — lambe arase tak. is ki aankhon mein kuch tha — shayad yaqeen, shayad umeed, shayad woh chiz jo is ne paanch saal mein mhasos nahin ki thi.

sukoon.

## akhatatam
