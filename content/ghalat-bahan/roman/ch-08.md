## manazr 1 — woh sawal

haron ne do din saba ki baatein sochane mein gazare.

woh is ke alfaz dharata raha — ke is ne slim se mohabbat ki, ke is ne ise chhor diya, ke slim donon behenon se mohabbat kurta tha aur intikhab na kar ska. lekin kuch na kuch fat nahin bith raha tha.

saba ki aankhon mein woh thakawat — kiya woh sirf gham ki thi ya koi aur raz chahapa rahi thi?

is ne faisla kiya — woh maham se baat kare ga. lekin is bar narami se, b-ghair kisi alazam ke, b-ghair kisi tswir ke.

woh maham ke ghar gaya. is ne darwaza khola toh maham maskraئi — lekin is ki muskurahat mein ek khauf tha, jaise woh jaanti thi ke kuch hone wala hai.

"kiya hawa?" is ne poocha.

"mujhe tum se baat krani hai," haron ne kaha. "bitho."

woh bith gaye. maham ke haath kanap rahe thay — is ne unhein chahapane ki koshash ki, lekin haron ne dekh liya.

"maham," haron ne aahasta se kaha — atani aahasta ke is ki aawaz mein koi dhamaki na ho, koi ghussa na ho — "mujhe sach batao. slim kaun tha?"

maham ka chehra safed ho gaya.

is ne haron ki traf dekha — aankhon mein khauf tha, aur is ke peeche woh chiz thi jisay woh paanch saal se chahapa rahi thi.

"tumhein ks ne bataya?" is ne saragoshi ki.

"kisi ne nahin. mein ne khod dhonda."

maham ne nazarein nichi kar lein. is ne ek lambi saans li — aur phir woh boli.

## manazr 2 — maham ka sach

"slim saba ka mangitr tha," maham ne kaha. is ki aawaz be jaan thi, jaise woh koi khani sna rahi ho jo is ki apani nahin thi. "ami ne in ki mangani krawaئi thi. saba khamosh thi — is ne kabhi nahin kaha ke woh is se mohabbat karti hai ya nahin. lekin woh is se shadi krane wali thi."

"aur phir?"

"aur phir mein is se mili."

maham ne aankhein band kar lein.

"woh hamare ghar aaya kurta tha — saba se malane. lekin woh majah se baatein kurta tha. pehle thodi — phir zyada. aur mein... mein is ki traf khinach gai."

is ki aawaz mein sharam thi — aur sach.

"yeh ghalat tha," is ne kaha. "mein janta tha ke woh miri behen ka hai. lekin mein nahin rak saki. aur woh bhi nahin rak ska."

"kiya saba ko pata tha?"

maham ne sar halaya. "woh sab kuch jaanti thi. is ne dekha — humein dekha — lekin kuch nahin kaha. woh khamosh rahi. is ne apani mangani tore di — amaan ko bataya ke ise slim se mohabbat nahin hai."

"aur tum ne yaqeen kar liya?"

"nahin. lekin mein ne yaqeen krane ka drama kiya. kyunke agar mein man leti ke saba ne apani khushi mire liay qaraban ki hai — toh mein is qarabani ko zaئa nahin kar sakti thi."

maham ki aankhon mein aansoo thay — lekin woh bha nahin rahe thay.

"hum ne bhagane ka faisla kiya," is ne kaha. "slim aur mein. raat ko — aadhi raat ko — bas astap par. hum lahor chale jaein ge, nayi zindagi sharoa karein ge."

"mein tayar thi. mein ne apana big bandh liya tha — sirf ek big, kuch kapre, kuch paisay. mein ghar se nkli — lekin darwazay par phanach kar mein rak gai."

is ki aawaz kanap gai.

"saba so rahi thi. is ka chehra be sukoon tha — jaise woh khwab mein bhi mire bare mein soch rahi ho. is ne mire liay apani mangani tore di thi, apani mohabbat qaraban ki thi, aur mein ise chhor kar jaa rahi thi."

"mein nahin jaa saki."

"mein waps apane kamray mein aa gai — aur sari raat roti rahi. mein ne slim ko phone nahin kiya — mein dar gai thi. mein ne socha — subah bata don gi, maafi mang lon gi, woh smajah jaئe ga."

maham ne aankhein kholin. in mein sirf khali pan tha.

"lekin jab subah hui toh woh mar chaka tha."

## manazr 3 — woh raat

"mein ne sna ke saba is raat bahar gai thi," maham ne kaha. is ki aawaz mein ab koi jazbaat nahin thay — sirf ek khashak sach tha. "woh is se malane gai — ise batane ke mein nahin aa saki, ise smajahane ke woh sabr kare."

"lekin slim ko yaqeen nahin aaya. ise laga ke saba ne mujhe rok liya hai — ke woh jhoot bol rahi hai. is ne saba par ghussa kiya — bahut ghussa kiya — aur phir chala gaya."

maham ke haath kanap rahe thay.

"saba ne mujhe kabhi nahin bataya ke is raat kiya hawa — kiya bola, kiya sna. lekin mein jaanti hoon — is ne apane aap ko kabhi maaf nahin kiya."

"woh kyun?"

"kyunke woh sochati hai ke agar woh na gai hoti — agar woh khamosh rahi hoti — toh slim zanda hota."

maham ne haron ki traf dekha — aankhon mein alataja thi.

"lekin yeh sach nahin hai. yeh miri ghalati thi."

"kiya?"

"agar mein is raat jaa sakti — agar mein dri na hoti — toh woh zanda hota. woh zanda hota, aur mein is ke saath hoti, aur saba toti na hoti."

woh tot gai — is ke aansoo bha nikalay, is ka jism kanap gaya, aur woh haron ke samne gr gai.

"yeh miri ghalati thi," woh dharati rahi. "miri ghalati thi."

haron ne ise athaya — apane bazawaؤan mein liya, ise snabhala, ise thame rakha.

"yeh tamhari ghalati nahin hai," is ne kaha. "yeh kisi ki ghalati nahin hai."

lekin maham ne is ki baat nahin sni — woh is ke sine se lag kar ro rahi thi, aur is ke aansoo is ke karte ko bhago rahe thay.

haron ise snabhale raha — lekin is ka zhan kahein aur tha.

kuch fat nahin bith raha tha.

agar slim sirf maham se mohabbat kurta tha — toh woh tswir kyun? saba aur slim — saath, maskrate huye, atane qareeb?

agar saba ne apani mohabbat qaraban kar di thi — toh woh slim ki qabr par kyun roti thi? atana roti thi jaise is ne apana sab kuch kho diya ho?

aur sab se brh kar — saba ne kaha tha ke slim ne is se kaha: *"mein tum se bhi mohabbat kurta hoon."*

agar woh sirf maham se mohabbat kurta tha toh saba se yeh kyun kaha?

haron ne maham ko slaya — woh is ke kandhay par so gai thi, thaki hui, be jaan. is ne ise sofe par lataya, is ke oopar kambal daala, aur aahasta se bahar nkla.

lekin is ka zhan nahin thamata tha.

donon behenon ne ise apani apani khani snaئi thi — aur donon ki khanayawan mein sach tha, lekin donon mein kuch na kuch chahapa hawa tha.

saba ne kaha: *min is se mohabbat karti thi. is ne majah se kaha — mein tum se bhi mohabbat kurta hoon.*

maham ne kaha: *wa sirf majah se mohabbat kurta tha. saba ne apani mohabbat qaraban kar di.*

donon sach — donon mein fraq.

kiya saba jhoot bol rahi thi? ya maham is haqiqat ko chahapa rahi thi ke slim ne is ki behen se bhi mohabbat ki thi?

ya shayad — sirf shayad — slim donon se mohabbat kurta tha, aur yahi woh sach tha jisay koi qabol nahin karna chahta tha.

haron ghar waps aaya toh is ne namaz parhi — tawil namaz, lambe sajade, aur dua ke Allah ise sidha rasta dakhaئe.

lekin jab woh sajade se utha toh is ke zhan mein sirf ek sawal tha:

*kaya sach waqai samne aana chahiye?*

kyunke agar pora sach aa gaya toh shayad teenon zandagayan tabaa ho jaein — maham ki, saba ki, aur is ki bhi.

kiya woh is ke liay tayar tha?

woh nahin janta tha.

lekin woh janta tha ke sach chahapa nahin ra sakta — der ho ya swire, woh nikal hi aata hai.

aur jab nikalay ga toh woh ise dekhe ga — chahe woh ise tabaa hi kyun na kar de.

bahar raat gehri thi. lahor ke taaray dhandale thay, lekin chaand saaf tha. is ne chaand ko dekha aur socha — wahi chaand paanch saal pehle bhi tha jab slim mara tha. aur wahi chaand kal bhi ho ga jab woh faisla kare ga.

is ne jib se woh chaabi n-kali — zang aalod, purani, be car.

aur ise mathi mein band kar liya.

sach ab chahapa nahin rahe ga.

woh ise dhond kar rahe ga.

aur jab mal jaئe ga — toh woh is ka samana kare ga.
