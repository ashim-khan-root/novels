woh bistar par leta tha — magar is ka zhan bahut tizi se dor raha tha. ise woh sab kuch yaad aa raha tha jo sishan mein hawa tha — woh raporat karad, is ke baap ka chehra, woh alfaz, aur phir woh aansoo jo is ne mhinon baad b-haye thay.

aayan ne kuch nahin kaha — woh doctor rhan ko dekh raha tha, aur is ke zhan mein woh sawal tha jo ise pehle din se prishan kar raha tha.

"aap ne yeh sab kyun kiya?"

doctor rhan ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein kuch badala, jaise woh koi darwaza kholane wala ho jo is ne kabhi nahin khola tha.

"yeh program — aap ne ise kyun banaya? aap kyun logon ki madad kar rahe hain?"

doctor rhan khamosh raha — lamba khamoshi. phir is ne kaha:

"kyunke mein bhi kabhi tamhari tarah tha."

aayan ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein hairat thi, magar woh hairat jis mein sawal thay.

doctor rhan ne aahasta se kaha: "tum ne socha ke mein sirf ek profisar hoon jo dosaron ki madad kar raha hai? nahin — mein bhi is raste se guzara hoon jis se tum guzar rahe ho. mein ne bhi wahi dekha hai jo tum dekh rahe ho — woh andhera jis se nklane ka koi rasta nazar nahin aata."

"mein ne yeh program is liay banaya kyunke mein janta hoon ke andhire mein akile rehna kisa hota hai. aur mein nahin chahta ke koi aur atani der tak is andhire mein rahe jatani der mein raha."

aayan khamosh tha — woh doctor rhan ko dekh raha tha, aur is ne pehli bar mhasos kiya ke is shakhas ke peeche bhi ek khani hai — shayad atani hi takleef da jatani is ki apani thi.
