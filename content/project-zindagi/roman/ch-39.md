---

bas ne sheher ke kanare par ise utaar diya — aur aayan wahan khara tha, asi sarak par jahan se woh aath maa pehle gaya tha.

woh sheher tabadil nahin hawa tha — wahi glayan, wahi dakanin, wahi log. magar aayan ko lag raha tha jaise woh pehli bar yeh sab dekh raha ho. har chiz wazh thi — rang, aawazein, khoshabawaئin.

woh apane ghar ki traf brha — har qadam ise qareeb le jaa raha tha, magar har qadam is ke dil ki dhadkan brha raha tha.

aur phir — woh ghar ke samne khara tha.

purana darwaza, neeli deewarein, chhat par lagi purani laئat — sab wahi tha. kuch nahin badala tha — siwaye is ke ke ab is ghar mein is ka baap akela rehta tha.

is ne darwaza khatakhataya — aur is ka dil zor zor se dharak raha tha.

darwaza khula.

is ke walad — borhe, safed baal, aankhon par aink, aur wahi purana kurta — darwazay mein khare thay. unhon ne aayan ko dekha — ek lamha, do lamhe — jaise unhein yaqeen nahin aa raha tha.

phir in ki aankhon mein aansoo aa gaye.

aayan ne kuch nahin kaha — woh sirf aage brha aur apane walad se lapt gaya, jaise woh bachcha ho jo andhire se dar kar apane baap ki god mein panaa leta hai.

"abّo —"

is ke walad ne ise mazaboti se thama — in ke haath kanap rahe thay:

"beta — tum waps aa gaye."

---

woh ghar mein dakhal hawa toh sab wahi tha — wahi sofa, wahi meez, wahi parday. yahan tak ke farsh par woh dagh bhi wahi tha jo is ne bachapan mein chai gra kar banaya tha.

woh apane kamray mein gaya — sab kuch wisa hi tha jaise is ne chhora tha. kitabein, kapre, woh purani gatar jo woh b-jaana kabhi nahin sikh ska.

is ki meez par asman ki tswir thi — wahi tswir jo woh bhagate huye chhor gaya tha. woh ise dikhane se drata tha — magar ab is ne tswir uthai, ise dekha, aur woh maskraya.

"mein waps aa gaya hoon, asman — aur is bar, mein tumhein apane saath le kar aaya hoon."

---

shaam ko woh aur is ke walad chhat par baithe thay — chai ke cup in ke haath mein thay, aur samne sheher ki roshanayan phili hui thin.

is ke walad ne bahut kuch nahin poocha — woh bas baithe rahe, jaise woh sirf apane bite ke saath khamoshi mein baithna chahte thay.

magar phir unhon ne kaha:

"tum makhatalaf lag rahe ho, beta."

aayan ne in ki traf dekha:

"hoon bhi — abّo, mein ne wahan bahut kuch sikha. mein ne sikha ke qasor war hona aur zama dar hona do makhatalaf chizin hain. asman ki maut miri ghalati nahin thi — aur mein ne brason yeh smajahane mein gazar diay ke yeh miri ghalati hai."

is ke walad khamosh rahe — in ki aankhon mein nami thi, magar woh rawaئe nahin.

"mein ne wahan aise logon se mulaqaat ki jo apane dar ka samana kar rahe thay — aur mein ne in se sikha ke dar humein kamzor nahin banata, balke yeh humein insaan banata hai."

is ke walad ne is ka haath thama — in ki aawaz kanap rahi thi:

"tamhari maan ne hamesha kaha tha ke tum bahut mazabot ho — aur woh shih thin. magar tum jante ho, beta — mazabot hone ka matalab yeh nahin ke tum kabhi nahin grate. mazabot woh hai jo gr kar bhi uth khara hota hai."

---

woh raat gehri thi jab aayan apane kamray mein gaya — magar woh so nahin ska.

woh khirki ke paas khara tha — bahar chaand tha, pora chaand, jaise woh ise dekh kar maskra raha ho.

is ne apani diary n-kali — aur is ne woh safha khola jahan is ne pehle din likha tha: "aaj mein ek aisi jagah jaa raha hoon jahan se shayad waps na aa skon."

is ne is ke neeche likha:

"aaj mein ghar waps aaya — aur mein pehli bar samjha ke ghar sirf ek jagah nahin hai. ghar woh jagah hai jahan tamhare antazar mein koi ho — aur jahan tum khod se mal sako."

is ne diary band ki — aur aankhein band kar lein.

pehli bar aath saalon mein — is ne sukoon ki neend swi.
