agle din sab ne doctor rhan ka mhasara kar liya.

anam ke sishan ke baad, kuch badal gaya tha. woh sawal jo sab ke zhan mein thay magar kisi ne nahin pooche thay — woh ab bahar aa rahe thay.

"yeh program kiya hai?" maئara ne poocha — is ki aawaz mein azm tha.

"hum aap ko bata chake hain," doctor rhan ne kaha. "yeh ek nafasayati program hai jo —"

"yeh kaafi nahin hai," hamara ne ise kat diya. "aap ne kaha tha ke yeh humein apane mazi ka samana karna skhaئe ga. magar kyun? humein ks chiz ke liay tayar kiya jaa raha hai?"

doctor rhan khamosh raha. is ne sab ki traf dekha — ek ek kar ke — aur phir kaha:

"mein tum se kuch nahin chahapa raha. lekin kuch sawalon ke jawab sirf wahi paa sakta hai jo aakhar tak phanache."

woh uth kar chala gaya — aur sab khamosh ra gaye.

---

aayan is raat so nahin ska.

woh bistar par leta tha magar is ka zhan doctor rhan ke alfaz par tha: "kuch sawalon ke jawab sirf wahi paa sakta hai jo aakhar tak phanache."

aakhar tak — is ka matalab kiya tha? aur agar woh aakhar tak nahin phanacha toh kiya ho ga?

woh utha aur kamray se bahar nkla — is ke qadam ise purani laئabriri ki traf le gaye. woh janta tha ke woh wahan kyun jaa raha hai — woh jawab dhond raha tha, aur woh janta tha ke jawab is amarat mein kahein chahapa hawa hai.

is ne seerhiyan charhate huye socha: agar doctor rhan ke paas in ke bare mein fileein hain, toh in fileon mein aur bhi kuch ho ga. aur woh fileein kahein hoon gi — shayad asi amarat mein, shayad doctor rhan ke aafas mein, shayad is kamray ke peeche jahan mashin thi.

woh teesri manazl par phanacha — woh jagah jahan se har roz sishan sharoa hote thay. is ne chaaron traf dekha — koi nahin tha, raat ka waqt tha, amarat khali thi.

is ne darwaza khola — woh kamra jahan mashin thi — aur andar chala gaya.

wahan ek aur darwaza tha — chhota, band. aayan ne ise dhakila — band tha. is ne apani jib mein kuch dhonda — ek purani kangan — ise darwazay ke qalabe mein daala — is ne kabhi yeh nahin kiya tha, magar is ne filmon mein dekha tha — aur darwaza khul gaya.

woh andar chala gaya — aur is ki saans rak gai.

---

woh kamra chhota tha — magar is ki deewaron par fileein thin, drajanon fileein, har ek par ek nam.

aayan ne unhein dekha — kuch purane thay, kuch naye. is ne ek file uthai — is par ek nam tha jo woh nahin janta tha. ise khola — andar ek shakhas ki pori zindagi thi: is ki tabiat, is ke doctor, is ki kal hastri, is ke misajaz, is ki lokishan hastri.

is ne aur fileein athaئin — sab ek jisi thin. har file mein is shakhas ki pori zindagi ka naqasha tha — jaise woh brason se dekhe jaa rahe hoon.

aur phir ise apani file mili.

is ka nam is par likha tha — "aayan khan" — aur is ke neeche ek nambr: "Subject 03 — Risk Level: Critical."

is ne file kholi — aur ise dekh kar is ka khoon thanda ho gaya.

is ki pori zindagi wahan thi — is ki talim, is ke nambr, is ke dost, is ke woh lamhat jo is ne kabhi kisi ko nahin bataye thay. is ki maan ki bimari, is ke baap ke saath is ki kshamaksh, is ke bhai ki maut — sab kuch.

aur aakhar mein ek jumla tha:

"mozoa nambr 03 — aayan khan. khatre ki sth: intehai. agle chhah mhinon mein khod ko nuqsan phanachane ka amakan: 89 fisad."

aayan ne file pakar li — is ke haath kanap rahe thay.

89 fisad.

woh janta tha ke woh theek nahin hai — magar yeh nambr... is ne kabhi socha bhi nahin tha ke is ki halat atani kharab hai. is ka matalab tha — woh ise bachane ke liay nahin la rahe thay. woh ise is liay laئe thay kyunke woh marane wala tha.

woh file pakaray khara raha — is ka dil zor zor se dharak raha tha, is ka damagh khali tha, is ke pairon tale zameen nahin thi.

woh bhag sakta tha. woh abhi yahan se nikal sakta tha — waps apani zindagi mein, waps apane kamray mein, waps is khamoshi mein jo ise maar rahi thi. magar agar woh bhag gaya toh woh sab kuch bhol jaئe ga — yeh jagah, yeh log, yeh sabaq — aur phir woh waps asi jagah aa jaئe ga jahan se woh sharoa hawa tha.

asi jagah jahan 89 fisad amakan tha ke woh khod ko khatam kar le ga.

is ne file waps rakhi — aur kamray se bahar nikal gaya. is ke qadam bhaari thay, is ka dil bhaari tha, magar is ke zhan mein ek baat wazh thi:

woh nahin bhage ga.

woh theek ho ga.

chahe is mein ktana hi waqt lage — woh theek ho ga.
