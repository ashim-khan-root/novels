title: "b-ab 44 — bharan"

---

teen hafate guzar gaye thay — aur zid har sishan aata tha. woh zyada nahin bolata tha, magar woh aata tha — aur aayan ke liay yahi kaafi tha.

magar ek din zid nahin aaya.

aayan ne antazar kiya — paanch minute, das minute, aadha ghanta. is ne phone kiya — koi jawab nahin aaya. is ne zid ki maan ko phone kiya — aur is ne jo sna is ne ise hala kar rakh diya:

"zid ne — is ne khodakshi ki koshash ki hai. woh hospital mein hai."

---

aayan hospital phanacha toh zid ke waladin bahar baithe thay — in ke chehray safed thay, in ki aankhon mein aansoo thay.

zid ki maan ne aayan ko dekha — aur is ne kaha:

"hum ne socha tum is ki madad kar rahe ho — magar woh aur bhi andar chala gaya."

woh alfaz chaqoo ki tarah chabhe — aur aayan ke purane zakham phir se khul gaye.

woh hospital ki banach par bith gaya — is ka sar is ke haathon mein tha, aur is ke andar ki aawaz ise kha rahi thi:

"tum na-kaam ho gaye — jaise hamesha hote ho. tum ne asman ko nahin bachaya — aur ab tum zid ko bhi nahin bacha sake."

---

woh ghar waps aaya toh is ke walad ne ise dekh kar poocha kiya hawa — magar aayan ne kuch nahin kaha. woh apane kamray mein gaya, darwaza band kiya, aur deewar ki traf dekhta raha — wahi khamoshi, wahi andhera, wahi qasor.

magar phir — is ki nazar asman ki tswir par pri.

aur ise woh din yaad aaya — jab is ne apane bhai ki qabr par kaha tha ke "mein ne jaane diya."

is ne gehri saans li — aur woh utha.

is ne doctor rhan ko phone kiya — aur ise sab bataya.

doctor rhan ne sna — phir kaha:

"kiya tum ne zid ko chhor diya?"

"nahin — magar —"

"kiya tum is ke saath nahin thay jab ise tamhari sab se zyada zarorat thi?"

"tha — magar —"

"aayan — tum kisi ko nahin bacha sakte. tum sirf in ke saath ra sakte ho. aur tum ne woh kiya — tum is ke saath thay."

---

agle din aayan waps hospital gaya — zid ab hosh mein tha, magar woh kisi se baat nahin kar raha tha.

aayan is ke kamray mein dakhal hawa — zid ne ise dekha, aur is ki nazar nichi kar li:

"tum kyun aaye?"

aayan is ke paas bith gaya:

"kyunke tum ne mujhe skhaya tha ke kabhi kabhi sab se bari madad sirf kisi ka saath hoti hai — aur mein tumhein akela nahin chhor sakta."

zid ki aankhon mein aansoo aa gaye:

"mein ne socha — agar mein na hoon toh sab ko sukoon mal jaئe ga."

aayan ne is ka haath thama:

"mein janta hoon — mein ne bhi yeh socha tha. magar yeh sach nahin hai. tamhare b-ghair — yeh duniya kam hai, zid. tumhein yaqeen nahin hai toh mujhe dekho — mein wahan tha jahan tum ab ho, aur mein aaj yahan hoon, tamhare saath."

---

shaam ko aayan ne apani diary mein likha:

"aaj mein ne sikha ke kisi ko bachana mire bas mein nahin — mein sirf in ke saath ra sakta hoon, in ka haath tham sakta hoon, aur unhein bata sakta hoon ke woh akile nahin hain.

zid zanda hai — aur woh lar raha hai. aur mein is ke saath hoon.

shayad — yahi sab se bari madad hai jo koi kisi ko de sakta hai."
