subah hui toh aayan ne socha — shayad yeh sab khwab tha.

is ne aankhein malatin aur bistar par krot li — magar is ka haath kisi chiz se laga. kaghaz. krim rang ka kaghaz. woh khat ab bhi wahan tha, is ke takaye ke paas, jahan is ne rakha tha jab woh so gaya tha.
