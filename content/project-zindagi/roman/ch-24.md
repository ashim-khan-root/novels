---

kuch log hote hain jin ke chehray par tum sukoon dekhte ho — magar in ki aankhon mein toofan chahapa hota hai. doctor rhan in mein se ek tha.

aaj woh aayan ko apani khani sna raha tha — pehli bar, kisi ko.

woh donon is ke aafas mein baithe thay — aayan ne file waps rakh di thi, magar doctor rhan janta tha ke is ne dekh liya hai. is ne poocha: "tum ne apani file dekhi hai. ab tum miri file bhi dekh chake ho. ab batao — kiya smajahe?"

aayan ne kuch nahin kaha — woh sirf snana chahta tha.

doctor rhan ne aankhein band kin — aur phir is ne batana sharoa kiya.

---

saat saal pehle, doctor rhan zindagi ke is mor par tha jahan sab kuch kamal lagta tha.

woh ek kamayab mahar nafasayat tha — is ka nam jaridon mein tha, is ki risarach parhi jati thi, log is ki baaton ko wazn dete thay. is ki biwi khubsurat thi, is ki beti — zara — is ki aankhon ki ptali thi.

woh saat saal ki thi — bari aankhein, hamesha hansti rehti thi, aur woh apane abbu ki ungli pakar kar har jagah ghomati thi.

"abbu, aap mujhe parak le kar chalein ge?"
"abbu, aap mire saath parhein ge?"
"abbu, mein bari ho kar aap jisi banon gi — doctor!"

doctor rhan ki zindagi mein sab kuch tha — magar jo kuch nahin tha, woh waqt tha. woh hamesha masroof rehta — conferenceein, risarach, mariz. is ne socha: jab yeh sab khatam ho jaئe ga, tab mein is ke saath waqt gazaron ga. magar woh waqt kabhi nahin aaya.

woh is raat bach gaya — ek sathi doctor, jo is ki halat janta tha, ne ise dhond liya. woh ise asptal le gaya — aur doctor rhan ki jaan bach gai.

magar woh mar chaka tha — andar se.

woh asptal mein do hafate raha — aur jab bahar aaya toh is ne apane aap se wada kiya: woh is tarah nahin maray ga jaise woh marna chahta tha. woh kuch aisa kare ga jis se is ki beti ki maut be-car na ho.

"mein ne khod ko bachane ke liay yeh mashin banai," doctor rhan ne kaha — is ki aankhein nam thin, magar is ne aansoo nahin aanay diay. "aur jab mein bach gaya toh mein ne socha — shayad mein auron ko bhi bacha skon."

---

woh donon khamosh baithe rahe — kaafi der.

phir aayan ne kaha — is ki aawaz mein woh chiz thi jo ahatram thi, ya shayad shakar:

"aap ne mujhe kabhi nahin bataya tha."

"mein ne kabhi kisi ko nahin bataya," doctor rhan ne kaha. "magar tumhein jaanna chahiye — kyunke tum wohein ho jahan mein kabhi tha. aur mein nahin chahta ke tum woh rasta chano jo mein ne chana tha."

aayan ne doctor rhan ki traf dekha — aur is ne pehli bar ise apani aankhon mein dekha. is ne woh udaasi dekhi jo kabhi khatam nahin hogi, woh zakham jo kabhi nahin bhare ga — magar is ke saath woh taqat bhi dekhi jo is zakham ke bawajod ise aage brhane deti thi.

"mein woh rasta nahin chanon ga," aayan ne kaha.

doctor rhan maskraya — ek chhoti si, udaas muskurahat.

"mein janta hoon," is ne kaha. "asi liay mein ne tumhein mantakhab kiya tha."
