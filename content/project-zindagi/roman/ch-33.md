---

kal aayan ka aakhari sishan tha — aur woh raat pehle aai.

kaman rom mein sab jama thay — maئara, hamara, sara, anam, zinab, aur aayan. doctor rhan bhi wahan tha — magar is bar woh doctor nahin tha, woh bhi ek frad tha jaise woh sab.

woh chap chap baithe thay — koi baat nahin kar raha tha, magar khamoshi bojahal nahin thi. woh khamoshi praskon thi, jaise woh sab smajah gaye thay ke baz chizin alfaz se nahin kahi jatin.

magar phir hamara ne khamoshi tori:

"hum mein se har ek ne dat ka samana kiya — magar kisi ne woh nahin bataya jo waqai dil mein hai."

is ne sab ki traf dekha:

"yeh aakhari raat hai — aaiay ek doosray ko batayein jo hum ne kabhi nahin bataya."

---

maئara ne pehle kaha:

"jab mein ne apani behen ko jate dekha toh mein ne socha ke mein mar jaon gi — magar mein nahin mari. mein zanda rahi, magar mujhe hamesha lagta tha ke mein adhori hoon. is raat mein ne khod ko aaئine mein dekha — aur mein ne dekha ke mein sirf zanda nahin bachchi thi, mein pori thi. sirf mein ne yeh kabhi kisi ko nahin bataya — kyunke mujhe dar tha ke agar mein ne bata diya toh woh mujhe kamzor samjhein ge."

hamara ne kaha:

"mein ne brason apani dor ko apani pehchan bana liya tha — jab woh chali gai toh mein ne socha ke mein kuch nahin hoon. magar phir mein ne sikha ke pehchan woh nahin jo tum karte ho — pehchan woh hai jo tum ho. yeh mein ne aaj tak kisi ko nahin bataya tha."

sara boli:

"mein ne brason yeh socha ke agar mein is din na rakti — agar mire haath na rakte — toh woh bachchi bach sakti thi. mujhe pata tha ke yeh sach nahin hai, magar mein yeh jhoot khod ko b-aur krati rahi — kyunke jhoot sach se zyada aasaan tha."

---

anam khamosh baithi thi — is ki aankhon mein aansoo thay, magar woh ro nahin rahi thi.

is ne kehna sharoa kiya — bahut aahasta se:

"b-laal —"

woh raki — is ka gla bhar aaya.

"b-laal mira pehla dost tha. yahan. is projikt mein. is ne mujhe skhaya ke mire chehray ke dagh miri kamazori nahin hain — woh miri taqat hain. woh pehla shakhas tha jis ne mujhe aaئine mein dekh kar bataya ke tum khubsurat ho — aur is ne yeh is liay nahin kaha ke woh mujhe khush karna chahta tha, balke is liay ke woh waqai yeh samajhta tha."

is ki aawaz kanapi:

"magar mein ne ise kabhi nahin bataya — ke woh mire liay ktana aham tha. mein ne socha ke waqt hai, kal bata don gi, prason bata don gi — aur phir ek din woh chala gaya. aur is ki yaad bhi mat gai."

woh roi nahin — magar is ki khamoshi is ke aanson se zyada bolati thi.

"tum log — tum woh wahad log ho jo ise jante ho. aur tum wahad log ho jo smajah sakte ho ke mein kiya kho chaki hoon."

---

aayan sn raha tha — is ki aankhein nam thin, magar woh ro nahin raha tha.

woh utha — aur darwazay ki traf brha.

"kahan jaa rahe ho?" maئara ne poocha.

"phone karna hai," aayan ne kaha — aur is ki aawaz mein woh kuch tha jo pehle nahin tha: narami. "apane abّo ko."

---

woh bahar braamade mein khara tha — raat thandi thi, stare chamak rahe thay, aur is ke haath mein phone tha.

is ne daئal kiya — ek bar, do bar.

"hilo?"

is ke walad ki aawaz — boorhi, thaki hui, magar phir bhi wahi aawaz jis ne ise bachapan mein slaya tha.

"abّo — mein hoon."

khamoshi — lambi, bhaari, magar gram.

"aayan?"

"ji — mein theek hoon."

is ke walad ne kuch nahin kaha — magar is ki aawaz mein aansoo thay jab is ne kaha:

"mein ne bahut dinon mein tamhari aawaz nahin sni thi, beta."

aayan ki aankhon se aansoo behenay lage — magar is ne apani aawaz snabhal li:

"mein janta hoon, abّo. aur mein maafi chahta hoon — har is chiz ke liay jo mein ne kahi nahin, har is chiz ke liay jo mein ne kiya nahin."

"tumhein maafi mangane ki zarorat nahin, beta. tum mire bite ho — yahi kaafi hai."

aayan khamosh raha — is ke aansoo bha rahe thay, magar is ka dil pehli bar brason mein halka tha.

"abّo —"

"haan, beta?"

"kal — mein is se malane jaa raha hoon."

is ke walad ko smajahane mein der nahin lagi — woh janta tha ke aayan ks ki baat kar raha hai.

"tum tayar ho?"

"nahin — magar mujhe lagta hai ke mein pehle se kahein zyada tayar hoon."

is ke walad ne gehri saans li — aur jab is ne bola toh is ki aawaz mein dua thi:

"tum waps aa sakte ho, beta. ghar antazar kar raha hai."

---

fatha khatam hui toh aayan ne kuch der phone band nahin kiya — woh khamoshi snta raha, woh khamoshi jis mein is ke walad ki mohabbat thi, woh mohabbat jo brason ki khamoshi ke bawajod kabhi khatam nahin hui thi.

jab woh andar aaya toh sab ne is ki traf dekha — is ka chehra gilatha, magar is ki aankhon mein woh chiz thi jo pehle kabhi nahin thi: aman.

woh bith gaya — aur khamoshi se bola:

"mein ne abّo se baat ki. pehli bar brason mein — sach mein baat ki."

"kiya kaha unhon ne?" sara ne poocha.

aayan maskraya:

"ke ghar antazar kar raha hai."

---

woh raat khatam hone wali thi — aur aayan janta tha ke yeh is ki zindagi ki sab se aham subah hogi.

woh apane kamray mein gaya — magar is ki aankh nahin lagi. woh chhat ko dekhta raha — is ke bhai ki tswir is ke samne thi, woh tswir jo is ne brason pehle chahapa di thi, magar aaj woh ise dekh raha tha.

"kal —" is ne khod se kaha:

"kal mein tum se malane aaon ga, asman. aur is bar — mein bhagon ga nahin."
