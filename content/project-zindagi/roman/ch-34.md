---

subah hui toh aayan pehle se jaag chaka tha — woh sara raat jaaga tha. magar is ki aankhon mein thakawat nahin thi — sirf azm tha.

woh kamray se bahar nkla toh sab pehle se mojod thay — jaise woh bhi jaag rahe thay, sara raat, yeh sochate huye ke aayan ks chiz ka samana krane jaa raha hai.

doctor rhan sishan rom ke bahar khara tha — is ke chehray par be chini wazh thi.

"tum tayar ho?"

aayan ne sar halaya — aur is ne mashin ki traf dekha.

woh kursi pehle se kahein zyada bari lag rahi thi — jaise yeh sishan baqi sab se makhatalaf ho. shayad — yeh tha bhi. har kisi ka aakhari sishan mushkil tha, magar aayan ka sishan sab se makhatalaf tha — woh is yaad ko dobara jine wala tha jis ne ise brason pehle tore diya tha.

woh kursi par baitha — is ne apane haath dekhe, woh nahin kanap rahe thay.

"sharoa karo," is ne kaha.

doctor rhan ne helmet is ke sar par rakha — is ki aankhon mein nami thi, magar is ne kuch nahin kaha. is ne sirf batan dabaya — aur mashin gonaji.

aur aayan — is din mein chala gaya jab is ki zindagi badal gai thi.

---

woh ek car mein tha.

samne wali sit par is ka bhai asman baitha tha — woh zanda tha. woh hans raha tha. woh mausiqi b-jaa raha tha aur kuch ga raha tha jaise woh paagal ho gaya ho.

"aayan bhai — sno na yeh gana. balakl tamhari tarah hai — udaas, magar khubsurat."

aayan ne ise dekha — is ka dil phatana chahta tha. yeh woh asman tha jisay woh aath saal pehle kho chaka tha — jis ki hansi woh bhol chaka tha, jis ki shakl woh yaad krane ki koshash kurta tha magar nahin kar pata tha.

magar ab woh is ke samne tha — asli, gram, zanda.

"asman —" aayan ne kaha, magar is ki aawaz nahin nikal rahi thi.

asman ne ise dekha — aankhon mein shararat thi:

"kiya hawa? dar gaye? mein ga raha hoon toh yeh drane ki baat nahin — mein bahut acha gata hoon."

woh hansa — aur woh hansi wahi thi jo aayan ke dil mein brason se gonaj rahi thi.

---

aur phir — sab kuch asi tarah hawa jaise is din hawa tha.

truck — tez, andhi, be lagam.

askrinach — dhat ka dhat se takrana, shishe ka bakharana, hawa ka zor se bahar nklana.

khamoshi — gehri, bhaari, maut jisi.

aayan ne cheekh kar pakara: "asman!"

koi jawab nahin aaya.

woh apane bhai ki traf mara — asman ki aankhein band thin, is ka chehra safed tha, aur is ke honton par hansi ki jagah khamoshi thi.

wahi manazr — aath saal pehle — wahi be basi — wahi kachara hawa dil.

---

magar is bar — aayan ne kuch makhatalaf kiya.

is ne apane bhai ka haath pakra — thanda, be jaan, magar woh ise chahorana nahin chahta tha. is bar woh nahin bhaga — is bar woh baitha raha, apane bhai ke paas, is ka haath thame.

is ke aansoo is ke galon par bha rahe thay — magar is ne aankhein nahin ponachahin. is ne asman ki traf dekha — aur is ne woh kaha jo ise aath saal pehle kehna chahiye tha:

"mein tumhein jaane deta hoon, asman."

is ki aawaz kanap rahi thi — magar woh raki nahin:

"mein ne brason socha ke agar mein is din gaari na chalata — agar mein tez na chalata — agar mein wahan se na gazarata — toh tum zanda rehte. magar ab mein janta hoon — tamhari maut miri ghalati nahin thi."

is ne apane bhai ka haath mazaboti se thama:

"tamhari maut ek hadasa tha — aur mein ise badal nahin sakta. magar mein apani zindagi badal sakta hoon. aur mein badal raha hoon."

is ne asman ke mathe ko choma — thandi jalad, woh khushbu jo woh bhol chaka tha.

"mein tumhein jaane deta hoon — magar mein tumhein kabhi nahin bholon ga."

---

mashin band hui.

aayan ki aankhein khulein — magar woh kuch der khamosh raha. is ne apane haath dekhe — woh kanap rahe thay. is ne apana chehra dekha — woh gilatha. magar is ne mhasos kiya — is ka dil pehli bar brason mein halka tha.

woh uth kar khara hawa — is ki tangin kanap rahi thin, magar woh gr nahin sakta tha. ab woh gr nahin sakta.

woh bahar nkla toh sab is ka antazar kar rahe thay — doctor rhan, maئara, hamara, sara, anam, zinab. sab ki aankhon mein sawal tha, fikr thi, umeed thi.

aayan ne in ki traf dekha — is ke hont hale:

"asman —"

is ki aankhon se aansoo behenay lage — magar woh maskra raha tha.

"asman — woh ab theek hai. aur mein bhi —"

woh aage brha — magar is ke ghatanon ne jawab nahin diya. is ki aankhon ke samne andhera chaha gaya — aur woh zameen par gr gaya.
