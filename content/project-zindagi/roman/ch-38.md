---

bas chal pri thi — magar abhi kuch hi daur gai thi ke peeche se aawaz aai:

"rako! rako!"

aayan ne mar kar dekha — zinab bas ke peeche dor rahi thi, is ka chehra sarakh tha, is ki saansein pholi hui thin.

driver ne bas rok di — aur zinab darwazay par aa kar khari ho gai. is ki aankhon mein azm tha, magar is ke chehray par dar bhi tha.

"aayan — mujhe tum se baat krani hai. akile mein."

---

woh bas se atra — aur donon sarak ke kanare khare ho gaye. zinab ki aankhein nichi thin — ise lag raha tha jaise woh koi bhaari bojah athaئe huye hai.

"zinab — kiya hawa?"

is ne gehri saans li — aur is ne kaha:

"mein doctor rhan ki bhanaji hoon."

aayan ne ise dekha — ise smajah nahin aaya:

"toh?"

"mein yahan jasosi krane aai thi — aithaks borad ne mujhe bhija tha."

---

zinab ne ise sari khani snaئi — kaise aithaks borad ko projikt zindagi par shak tha, kaise unhein doctor rhan ki beti ki maut ke bare mein pata chala, kaise unhon ne ise doctor rhan ke qareeb bhija — is ki ngrani krane ke liay.

"mein ne socha ke tum sab ko bataؤan gi — magar phir mein ne tum logon ko dekha. mein ne dekha ke yeh projikt tamhari zindagion mein kiya tabadili la raha hai. mein ne dekha ke maئara kaise bakhari hui thi aur kaise woh snabhal rahi thi — mein ne dekha ke sara kaise khamosh thi aur kaise woh bolane lagi thi — aur mein ne dekha ke tum kaise apane bhai ki maut ke baad pehli bar maskraئe thay."

is ki aankhon mein aansoo thay:

"aur mein ne faisla kiya — mein aithaks borad ko kuch nahin bataؤan gi. kyunke yeh projikt khatranak nahin hai — yeh zindagi bachata hai."

---

aayan khamosh tha — woh ise hazm krane ki koshash kar raha tha.

phir is ne poocha:

"doctor rhan jante hain?"

zinab ne sar halaya — aur woh maskraئi:

"woh pehle din se jante thay. is ne mujhe bataya: 'tum aai ho dikhane — toh dekho. aur jo dekho ise khod faisla karo.'"

aayan ne ise dekha — aur is ke chehray par muskurahat aa gai. doctor rhan — woh hamesha se sab kuch janta tha.

"toh ab tum kiya karo gi?"

zinab ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein azm tha:

"mein waps jaa rahi hoon — aithaks borad ko batane. magar yeh nahin ke yeh projikt band hona chahiye — balke yeh ke ise har jagah hona chahiye."

is ne aayan ka haath pakra:

"magar mujhe madad chahiye — tamhari madad. tum log — tum is projikt ki sab se bari masal ho. agar tum batao ge ke is ne tumhein kaise badala — toh koi bhi ise band nahin kar sake ga."

---

aayan ne zinab ko dekha — aur is ne socha: jo chiz ise andar lai thi woh jhoot tha, magar jo chiz ise bahar le jaa rahi thi woh sach tha. aur shayad yahi projikt zindagi ka asl majaza tha — ke jhoot bhi sach mein badal sakta hai, agar dil mein niki ho.

"mein tamhari madad karon ga," is ne kaha — aur is ki aawaz mein yaqeen tha. "projikt zindagi ko zanda rehna chahiye — aur mein is baat ko yaqini banaؤan ga."

zinab maskraئi — aur is ki aankhon mein nami thi:

"shukriya, aayan."

woh mari — aur waps amarat ki traf dor gai.

aayan ne ise jate dekha — aur phir woh bas mein swar ho gaya.

bas chal pri — aur is bar, woh waqai ghar jaa raha tha.

magar woh janta tha — is ka safr abhi khatam nahin hawa tha. yeh sirf ek naya aaghaz tha.
