---

agle din jab aayan ne dekha ke is ka baap is ke kamray ke bahar khara hai toh ise laga jaise is ne khwab dekha ho.

woh wahan khare thay — abbu, wahi abbu jo is ne pichlay paanch saalon mein hamesha dekhe thay: sidhi kamar, chehray par woh skhati jo kabhi nahin paghali, aankhon mein woh dori jo kabhi khatam nahin hui. magar aaj woh makhatalaf thay — in ke kapre thodai be-tarteeb thay, in ki aankhon ke neeche halaqe thay, aur woh is tarah khare thay jaise unhein yaqeen nahin aa raha ke woh yahan hain.

"abbu —"

abbu ne kuch nahin kaha. woh sirf ise dekhte rahe — apane bite ko, jisay unhon ne mhinon se nahin dekha tha, jo in se daur tha, in ki khamoshi mein khawaya hawa tha.

phir woh kamray mein aaye — aahasta, jaise unhein andar aanay ki ajazt nahin thi. unhon ne chaaron traf dekha — chhota sa kamra, be-tarteeb bistar, farsh par kitabein bakhari hui thin, aur meez par woh kaghaz thay jin par aayan ne kuch likha tha magar kabhi makamal nahin kiya.

woh kursi par bith gaye — aahasta, jaise woh thak gaye hoon.

"tamhari ami ne kaha ke tum theek nahin ho."

aayan ne kuch nahin kaha — woh darwazay ke paas khara raha, is ka dil zor zor se dharak raha tha.

"tum ne unhein phone kiya tha — tum ro rahe thay." ab ki aawaz mein woh skhati nahin thi jo hamesha thi — yeh naram thi, thaki hui, jaise woh bahut der se yeh alfaz kehna chaa rahe hoon.

"mein theek hoon," aayan ne kaha — magar is bar, woh alfaz khod ise bhi jhootay lage.

is ke baap ne is ki traf dekha — lamba, gehri nazar. "tum jhoot bolate ho — balakl apane baap ki tarah."

---

aayan ne pehli bar apane baap ko is tarah dekha — kamzor, thaka hawa, insaan.

abbu ne gehri saans li aur kaha — is ki aawaz kanap rahi thi:

"mein janta hoon ke mein ne tum se bahut kuch kaha — woh baatein jo baap ko bite se nahin khani chahiye. tamhari ami ko jo hawa — woh tamhari ghalati nahin thi. aur b-laal —"

woh rak gaye — is ka nam lete hi in ki aankhein bhar aaein.

"b-laal — woh bhi tamhari ghalati nahin thi."

yeh alfaz thay — woh paanch alfaz jo aayan ne paanch saalon mein apane baap se snane ke liay trase thay. woh alfaz jo is ne kabhi sne nahin thay, magar har roz snane ki umeed ki thi.

"mein ne tum se baat karna chhor di thi — is liay nahin ke tum qasor war thay. mein ne is liay chhor diya kyunke mein khod ko qasor war samajhta tha."

abbu ke aansoo bha rahe thay — woh apane aansoo nahin rok sakte thay, aur woh rokana nahin chahte thay.

"mein tamhara baap hoon — mujhe tumhein snabhalana chahiye tha. magar mein ne khod ko kho diya tha. b-laal ke baad, mein ne tumhein dekha — aur tum mujhe is ki yaad dalate thay. har bar jab mein tumhein dekhta, mujhe woh din yaad aa jata — aur mein ise bradashat nahin kar sakta tha."

"mein ne socha ke aap mujhe qasor war samajhte hain," aayan ne kaha — is ki aawaz toti hui thi.

"nahin, beta — mein khod ko qasor war samajhta tha."

---

woh donon ek doosray ko dekh rahe thay — baap aur beta, paanch saal ki khamoshi ke baad pehli bar sach mein ek doosray ko dekh rahe thay.

phir abbu khare huye — woh aayan ke paas aaye, aur unhon ne apane bite ko gle se laga liya.

yeh woh gle lagana tha jo paanch saal pehle hona chahiye tha — is din jab woh b-laal ki qabr par khare thay aur donon ro nahin sake thay. woh gle lagana jo is din hona chahiye tha jab aayan ne smastr delete kiya tha aur koi ise rokane nahin aaya tha. woh gle lagana jo har is din hona chahiye tha jab aayan ne socha tha ke woh akela hai.

aayan ro pra — aur is bar, is ne apane aansoo nahin chahapaئe. is ne apane baap ke kandhay par sar rakha aur roya — is tarah jaise woh paanch saal se ro nahin ska tha.

abbu bhi ro rahe thay — in ke aansoo in ke bite ke baalon mein gr rahe thay, aur woh is ke sar par haath phir rahe thay, jaise woh bachapan mein kiya karte thay jab woh dar jata tha.

"mein waps aa jaon ga, beta," abbu ne kaha — in ki aawaz mein woh azm tha jo sirf ek baap mein ho sakta hai. "mein waps aa jaon ga — aur is bar, mein nahin bhagon ga."

---

abbu chale gaye toh aayan kamray mein akile baitha raha — is ka chehra aanson se gila tha, magar is ke dil mein pehli bar sukoon tha.

paanch saal — woh paanch saal jo is ne apane baap ki khamoshi mein gazare thay, woh paanch saal jis mein is ne socha tha ke baap is se nafrat kurta hai, woh paanch saal jis mein woh akela tha — woh khatam ho gaye thay.

is ne apana phone athaya — aur apane baap ko ek message likha:

"abbu, mein tum se pyar kurta hoon."

pehli bar — is ne yeh alfaz kabhi nahin lakhe thay.

jawab aaya — forً:

"mein bhi tajah se pyar kurta hoon, beta. hamesha kiya — aur hamesha karon ga."

aayan ne phone apane sine se laga liya — aur woh maskraya. pehli bar brason mein, ise laga ke woh ghar aa gaya hai — sach mein, pori tarah, ghar.
