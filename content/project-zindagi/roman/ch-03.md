woh hansi thi jo aayan ko kabhi nahin bholati thi.

ek bachchay ki hansi — be-fikr, bewaja, behad. woh hansi jo subah swire ise jaga deti thi, jo is ke kamray mein dorati hui aati thi, jo kehti thi: "bhai, atho, der ho rahi hai."

b-la lakhan. is ka chhota bhai.

aayan se paanch saal chhota. aankhein bari bari, hansi hamesha tayar, aur dil atana bara jatana is ka qad chhota tha. woh aayan ke peeche peeche pharata tha — jahan aayan jata, woh wahan hota. jab aayan parhta, woh is ke paas bith kar dra kurta tha. jab aayan khilata, woh is ki tim mein shamal hone ki zd kurta tha.

"bhai, mujhe bhi le chalo na."
"bhai, yeh dekho mein ne kiya banaya."
"bhai, aap mujhe baئik skhaؤ ge na?"

bhai. woh lafz jo aayan ne paanch saalon mein kisi se nahin kaha. woh lafz jo ab is ke gle mein is tarah ataka hawa tha jaise koi haddi ho jisay woh ngl nahin sakta.

---

barah saal pehle ki baat hai.

aayan das saal ka tha aur b-la l paanch. bahar baarish ho rahi thi aur donon khirki ke paas baithe baarish dekh rahe thay. b-la l ne kaha: "bhai, baarish mein kyun nahin khilate?" aayan ne kaha: "kyunke ami dantin gi."

b-la l ne maskra kar kaha: "phir chahap ke khilin ge."

aur woh baarish mein bhag gaye — donon, haath pakaray, paani mein bhigate huye, hanaste huye. ami ne danta zaroor, magar is din ki hansi ab bhi aayan ke kaanon mein thi.

woh ise kabhi nahin bhola tha.

magar woh ise yaad bhi nahin kurta tha.

kyunke bholane ka matalab tha ke woh ise yaad kar sakta hai, aur yaad krane ka matalab tha ke woh ise khone ka dard dobara mhasos kare ga. is liay woh bhol gaya tha — ya bholane ki koshash ki thi. is ne apane bhai ki tswirin chahapa di thin, is ke khalone de diay thay, is ka nam kisi se nahin liya tha.

magar woh hansi — woh hansi is ke damagh mein rikarad ki tarah pri thi, har raat chalane ke liay tayar.

---

woh din.

aayan satrah saal ka tha aur b-la l barah. garmion ki chahatayan thin aur donon ghar par thay. ami bawarachi khane mein thin, abbu daftar gaye huye thay. b-la l aayan ke kamray mein aaya:

"bhai, parak chalein?"

aayan apane phone par tha. is ne b-ghair sar athaئe kaha: "haan haan, chalte hain."

"abhi chalein?"

"abhi nahin. thodi der mein."

b-la l is ke paas bith gaya — is ka sar aayan ke kandhay par tha, is ki aankhein is ki skrin par thin magar woh kuch dekh nahin raha tha. woh sirf apane bhai ke paas baithna chahta tha.

aayan ne ise nahin dekha.

woh phone par message kar raha tha — kisi dost ko, koi mamoli baat, koi chiz jo ab ise balakl bhi yaad nahin.

aadha ghanta guzar gaya. b-la l phir aaya: "bhai, ab chalein?"

aayan ne saans bhara. "chalo."

woh parak gaye. raste mein b-la l baatein kurta raha — is ne ek naya karad banaya tha, ise school mein anaam mila tha, woh bara ho kar doctor banana chahta tha. aayan sar halata raha, kabhi kabhar "haan" kehta raha, magar is ka dhayan phone par tha.

parak mein kuch bachchay krakt khil rahe thay. b-la l ne kaha: "bhai, mein bhi khilon?"

"khil le."

b-la l bhag kar bachon mein shamal ho gaya. aayan ek binach par bith gaya. is ne phone n-kala — ek kal thi, kisi dost ki.

woh uth kar thodi daur chala gaya — take shore na ho. is ne kal sharoa ki. baatein kurta raha. hansta raha. ise pata nahin tha ke ktana waqt guzar gaya.

phir is ne ek aawaz sni.

brik lagane ki aawaz.

bahut tez.

bahut qareeb.

is ne peeche mar kar dekha — aur is ki duniya rak gai.

---

b-la l sarak par pra tha.

is ke chehray par woh muskurahat nahin thi. is ki aankhein khuli thin magar woh dekh nahin rahi thin. ek gind sarak ke kanare pri thi — aur b-la l is ke peeche bhaga tha. aur aayan phone par baat kar raha tha.

aayan ne phone gra diya.

woh bhag kar b-la l ke paas gaya. is ne ise athaya — is ka jism gram tha, is ka sar bhaari tha, is ke haath mein abhi tak woh karad tha jo is ne banaya tha aur aayan ko dakhana chahta tha.

"b-la l! b-la l!"

koi jawab nahin.

koi hansi nahin.

sirf woh khamoshi jo is din se aayan ke peeche pri hui thi.

hospital.

wahi safed deewarein. wahi dawaئayawan ki bo. wahi bhagati narasin. magar is bar aayan is bistar ke paas nahin baitha tha — woh bahar khara tha, jab doctor bahar aaya aur is ne is ki aankhon mein dekh kar sar halaya — woh sar jo kehta hai: "hum kuch nahin kar sake."

ami ki cheekh.

abbu ka chehra — woh chehra jis mein koi aiksprishan nahin tha, sirf woh khali pan jo is din se in ka maqadr ban gaya.

aayan ne is din nahin roya.

woh khara raha — deewar ke saath, haath neeche, aankhein khashak. is ne apane bhai ki mit ko dekha jab ise bahar le jaya jaa raha tha. ek safed kapre mein lapta hawa, woh chhota sa jism, woh haath jis mein karad tha aur jis ne aayan ka haath bahut bar thama tha.

is ne nahin roya.

is ne kaha: "mein ne phone par baat ki. ek dost se. bas minute thay. mein ne socha —"

is ka baap is ki traf mara. is ki aankhon mein aansoo nahin thay — woh khashak thin, balakl apane bite ki tarah. magar in aankhon mein kuch aur tha.

woh kuch jo baap ne kabhi kaha nahin magar har roz kaha:

"tum ne ise maara."

---

aayan bistar par leta tha. chhat par daraar thi — wahi daraar jisay woh ab har raat dekhta tha. is ne apani aankhein band kin aur b-la l ko dekha — ise hansta hawa, ise karad dakhata hawa, ise kehta hawa: "bhai, aap mujhe baئik skhaؤ ge na?"

is ne aankhein khol dein.

woh das minute — woh das minute jo is ne phone par baat ki thi, woh das minute jis mein b-la l ne gind ke peeche bhag kar ise pakrane ki koshash ki thi aur koi nahin tha jo ise rokta — woh das minute is ki zindagi ka woh hasa thay jisay woh badalane ki hazaar bar koshash kar chaka tha. magar waqt kabhi nahin badalata — yeh sirf aage brhata hai, tumhein akela chhor kar, tamhare qasor ke saath.

woh bistar par leta raha. is ki aankhein khuli thin, chhat par woh daraar thi, aur is ke kaan mein ab bhi woh hansi thi jo kabhi nahin aaye gi.

paanch saal ho gaye thay.

magar aaj bhi jab woh aankhein band kurta tha toh ise woh chehra dakhaئi deta tha — hansta hawa, ise bhai kehta hawa, aur phir woh manazr jis ne sab kuch badal diya.

woh janta tha — woh kabhi is se bhag nahin sakta.

woh sirf is ka antazar kar sakta tha ke ek din woh ise dhond le.
