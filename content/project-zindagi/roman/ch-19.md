---

aayan ka teesra sishan tha — aur woh pehli bar b-ghair dar ke kursi par baitha.

woh janta tha ke woh ks yaad mein jaana chahta hai. is bar woh koi takleef da yaad nahin chane ga — woh kuch acha chane ga, kuch aisa jo ise yaad dalaئe ke zindagi mein khushi bhi hoti hai.

"aaj kaun si yaad?" doctor rhan ne poocha.

"woh din jab mira bhai pida hawa," aayan ne kaha — aur is ki aawaz mein pehli bar garmi thi.

mashin sharoa hui — aur aayan maskraya.

---

woh ek hospital tha — magar is bar wahan maut nahin thi, zindagi thi.

aayan, satrah saal pehle, das saal ka tha. woh hospital ki rahadari mein bhag raha tha — is ke chehray par betabi thi, is ki aankhon mein tajasas tha. is ke baap ne is ka haath pakra aur kaha: "aahasta, beta — tamhara bhai abhi pida hawa hai."

das saal ke aayan ne darwaza khola — aur is ne apani ami ko dekha, in ke haath mein ek chhota sa bachcha tha, lapta hawa, is ki aankhein band thin, is ke hont hal rahe thay.

ami ne maskra kar kaha: "ao, apane bhai se milo."

aayan aahasta se qareeb gaya — is ne is chhotay se haath ko chahawa, atani narami se jaise woh koi nazk chiz ho. bachchay ki unglion ne is ki ungli pakar li — aur aayan ka dil bhar aaya.

is ke baap ne ise kandhay par utha liya: "ab tum baray bhai ho."

aayan ki aankhon mein khushi thi — woh khushi jo sirf is waqt hoti hai jab tumhein pata chale ke tamhari zindagi mein koi naya shakhas aaya hai, koi jo tamhara ho ga, hamesha.

---

yaad badal gai — jaise yaadein badalati hain, ek se doosri mein phaslati hui.

ab woh paanch saal baad thay. b-laal paanch saal ka tha — woh is ke peeche peeche pharata tha, har jagah, har waqt.

"bhai, mujhe bhi le chalo na!"

aayan ne sar jahataka: "chhor, paagal hai kiya?"

magar woh maskraya — b-laal ki zd, is ki baatein, is ki woh hansi — sab kuch ise acha lagta tha, chahe woh kabhi manta nahin tha.

ek aur yaad: b-laal ne ise ek karad banaya tha — is par likha tha: "bhatrin bhai." is ka khat bara nahin tha, magar is ne atani mhant se likha tha ke kaghaz idhar idhar dhabe thay.

aayan ne karad liya — ise dekha — aur ise apani kitabon mein rakh liya. is ne kuch nahin kaha, magar is ne woh karad aaj tak rakha hawa tha.

woh yaadein — woh qimati, khubsurat yaadein — woh abhi tak is ke andar thin, mitti ke neeche dabi hui, magar marada nahin.

---

mashin band hui toh aayan ki aankhein khuli thin — aur woh maskra raha tha.

is ke galon par aansoo thay — magar woh aansoo gham ke nahin thay. woh shakar ke thay, mohabbat ke thay, is ehsaas ke thay ke is ne apane bhai ko ek bar phir dekh liya tha.

woh uth kar khara hawa — is ka dil halka tha, is ka jism halka tha, is ki saans aasaan thi. woh pehli bar brason mein mhasos kar raha tha jaise woh zanda hai.

aur phir darwaza khula — aur maئara andar aai.

is ka chehra safed tha. is ki aankhein dri hui thin.

"b-laal chala gaya."

aayan ne is ki traf dekha — is ka dil ek lamhe ke liay rak gaya. "kiya matalab?"

"woh — is ne program chhor diya. doctor rhan ne kaha ke woh kal raat chala gaya. aur ab —"

maئara ki aawaz kanap gai.

"ab ise kuch yaad nahin — humein bhi nahin."

---

woh bahar bhage — donon, seerhiyan atrate huye, sarak ki traf.

aur wahan b-laal khara tha — sarak ke kanare, phone par baat kar raha tha, hans raha tha. woh balakl aam aadmi lag raha tha — woh shakhas jis ne kabhi in ke saath sishan kiya tha, woh shakhas jis ne apane dost ki maut dekhi thi, woh shakhas jis ne saat saal baad ek khat kholane ka faisla kiya tha — woh sab kuch mat chaka tha.

maئara ne aawaz di: "b-laal!"

b-laal ne peeche mar kar dekha — is ki nazar in par pri, magar is ki aankhon mein pehchan nahin thi.

"kiya aap mujhe jante hain?" is ne poocha — balakl aam aawaz mein, balakl ajanabi ki tarah.

maئara ka munh khula ra gaya — woh kuch nahin kha saki.

b-laal ne kandhay achakaئe aur apani raah chala gaya — mobile par baatein kurta hawa, hansta hawa, is duniya mein jahan projikt zindagi kabhi tha hi nahin.

woh donon ise dekhte rahe — jab tak woh ghaئab nahin ho gaya.

aur phir maئara ro pri.

---

aayan is raat apane kamray mein baitha raha — is ka dil bhaari tha, is ka zhan khali tha.

b-laal chala gaya tha — aur woh khush tha. woh bhol gaya tha — aur woh khush tha. magar ks qimat par? is ne apane dost ko kho diya tha, apani yadon ko kho diya tha, apane aap ko kho diya tha — aur woh khush tha.

kiya yahi theek hona tha?

kiya yahi woh maqasad tha jis ke liay woh sab yahan thay — bhol jaana, mat jaana, kabhi na yaad karna?

aayan ne apane haath dekhe — woh kanap rahe thay. magar woh janta tha ke woh nahin bhage ga. woh nahin bhole ga. woh wahan rahe ga — chahe ise ktana hi dard kyun na ho.

kyunke dard ka matalab tha ke woh zanda hai. aur zanda rhane ka matalab tha ke woh theek ho sakta hai.

is ne apani diary mein likha:

"b-laal bhol gaya. magar mein nahin bholon ga. mein apane bhai ko yaad rakhon ga — chahe woh yaad mujhe ktana bhi takleef de. kyunke woh yaad hi toh hai jo mujhe bataati hai ke mein ne kabhi kisi se mohabbat ki thi."
