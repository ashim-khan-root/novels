---

sara hosh mein aa gai thi — magar woh badal chaki thi.

woh asptal ke bistar par leti thi — is ki aankhein khuli thin, magar woh kuch nahin dekh rahi thi. is ke hont hal rahe thay — jaise woh kisi se baatein kar rahi ho jo is ke swa kisi ko nazar nahin aata.

doctor rhan is ke paas baitha tha — is ka chehra thaka hawa tha, is ki aankhon ke neeche ghere halaqe thay, aur woh is tarah baitha tha jaise is ki sari tawanaئi khatam ho gai ho.

aayan kamray mein aaya — is ne sara ko dekha, phir doctor rhan ko dekha. "woh theek ho jaئe gi?"

doctor rhan ne sar halaya — magar is ki aankhon mein yaqeen nahin tha. "haan. jasmani tore par — woh theek hai. magar —"

"magar?"

"magar is ki woh yaad — jab is ne ise badalane ki koshash ki toh mashin ne hafazati tareeqa car faal kar diya — is ki yaad ko mazid ghera kar diya. ab woh yaad pehle se bhi zyada takleef da hai."

aayan ne sara ki traf dekha — woh ab bhi kuch nahin bol rahi thi, is ki aankhein khokhali thin, is ka jism be-jaan tha.

"yeh miri ghalati thi," doctor rhan ne kaha — is ki aawaz mein woh chiz thi jo sirf is waqt aati hai jab koi apane aap ko maaf nahin kar sakta. "mujhe ise doosra sishan nahin dena chahiye tha."

---

aayan doctor rhan ko aafas mein le gaya — is ne ise bithane par majabor kiya, aur phir is ke samne bith gaya.

"mashin mein kiya kharabi hai?" is ne poocha — is ki aawaz mein ghussa nahin tha, sirf sach janane ki khwahish thi.

doctor rhan ne gehri saans li — aur phir is ne woh batana sharoa kiya jo is ne kabhi kisi ko nahin bataya tha:

"mashin — woh kamal nahin hai. hum ne ise taqaribً paanch saal pehle banaya tha, magar hum ne kabhi ise makamal tore par tist nahin kiya."

"kyun nahin?"

"kyunke ise tist krane ka matalab tha — logon ko khatre mein dalana. aur mein — mein kisi aur ko nahin kho sakta tha."

is ne meez par mojod kaghazaat ki traf dekha — woh kaghazaat jin par mashin ke dizaئan thay, is ke faramole, is ki hadod.

"hum ne ise sirf teen logon par tist kiya — majah smit. aur har bar, is ne kaam kiya. magar har bar — woh log pehle se te shada yadon mein gaye, sada yaadein, gehri nahin."

is ne aayan ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein woh chiz thi jo antabaa thi:

"sara ne sab se gehri yaad mein jaane ki koshash ki — aur wahan is ne ise badalane ki koshash ki. mashin ne ise roka — magar is rokane ki qimat — woh is ki yaad ko aur ghera karna thi."

---

aayan khamosh raha — woh sara ke bare mein soch raha tha, is ki aankhon mein is khali pan ke bare mein, is dar ke bare mein jo is ne is ke chehray par dekha tha.

"toh phir — agar mein —"

"tumhein sab se gehri yaad mein jaana ho ga," doctor rhan ne kaha — is ki aawaz mein woh chiz thi jo haqiqat thi, jisay woh nahin badal sakta tha. "tamhare bhai ki maut — woh tamhari sab se gehri yaad hai. aur tumhein is ka samana karna ho ga."

aayan ka dil dharaka — is ke haath kanap gaye. woh janta tha ke yeh din aaye ga — magar ab jab woh is ke samne tha, toh ise laga jaise woh kisi bahut bari chiz ke kanare par khara ho.

"kiya — kiya mein ise badal sakta hoon?"

doctor rhan ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein woh chiz thi jo shayad hamadradi thi, ya shayad antabaa.

"nahin," is ne kaha — narami se, magar paki aawaz mein. "tum kuch nahin badal sakte. tum sirf ise dobara ji sakte ho — aur is bar, tum apane radamal ko badal sakte ho."

aayan ne sar halaya — woh smajah gaya tha. woh janta tha ke ise is ka samana karna ho ga — warana woh sara ki tarah is yaad mein phanas jaئe ga, aur shayad kabhi bahar nahin nikal sake ga.

woh uth kar khara hawa — is ki tangin kanap rahi thin, magar is ka azm pakhata tha.

"kab?" is ne poocha.

doctor rhan ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein fakhar tha, aur dar bhi.

"kal. agar tum tayar ho."

aayan ne sar halaya — aur pehli bar woh is chiz se nahin bhaga jis se woh sab se zyada drata tha.

woh tayar tha — ya kam az kam, woh tayar hone ki koshash kare ga.
