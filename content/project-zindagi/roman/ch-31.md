---

agle din doctor rhan ne aalan kiya: "aakhari sishan ka waqt aa gaya hai."

sab kaman rom mein baithe thay — sara ab bhi khamosh thi, maئara praskon thi, hamara pehle se zyada snajida tha, anam ki aankhon mein woh chamak thi jo pehle nahin thi, aur nayi larki — is ka nam zinab tha — ek kaunay mein baithi khamosh thi.

"har ek ko apani is yaad ka samana karna ho ga jis se woh sab se zyada drate hain — woh yaad jis ne unhein wahan phanachaya jahan woh aaj hain."

is ki nazar har ek par pri — ek ek kar ke.

"yeh aakhari marhala hai. is ke baad — tum aazad ho."

aayan ne gehri saans li — woh janta tha ke is ki yaad kiya hai. woh janta tha ke ise ks chiz ka samana karna ho ga. aur pehli bar, woh is se drata nahin tha — woh sirf tayar hona chahta tha.

---

pehle maئara ki bari thi.

woh kursi par baithi — is ka chehra praskon tha, is ke haath is ki god mein thay, aur is ki aankhon mein woh azm tha jo is ne pehle kabhi nahin dekha tha.

mashin sharoa hui — aur woh is din mein chali gai jab is ki behen ne ise alwida kaha tha.

is bar, woh sirf dekh nahin rahi thi — woh baat kar rahi thi. is ne apani chhoti behen ko dekha — jo is ke samne khari thi, is ki aankhon mein woh azm tha jo ise kahein aur le jaa raha tha — aur maئara ne kaha:

"mein tumhein nahin rokon gi."

is ki behen ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein hairat thi.

"mein ne brason socha ke yeh miri ghalati thi ke tum chali gaئin — ke mein tumhein rok sakti thi. magar mein ne tumhein roka nahin — is liay nahin ke mein nahin chahti thi, balke is liay ke tumhein wahan jaana tha jahan tum khush ho sako."

is ki aankhon mein aansoo thay — magar woh maskra rahi thi.

"mein ne tumhein jaane diya — aur yeh sab se bara thafa tha jo mein tumhein de sakti thi."

mashin band hui toh maئara ki aankhein khuli thin — magar woh ro nahin rahi thi. woh maskra rahi thi — ek sachi, naram, praskon muskurahat.

"mein ne ise jaane diya," is ne kaha — aur is ki aawaz mein woh sukoon tha jo sirf qabolit se aata hai. "pachis saal baad — mein ne ise waqai jaane diya."

---

phir hamara ki bari thi.

woh kursi par baitha — is ke chehray par woh muskurahat nahin thi jo is ki dhaal thi. woh snajida tha, magar is snajidagi mein dar nahin tha — sirf azm tha.

woh is din mein gaya — jab doctor ne kaha: "tum dobara kabhi nahin dor sako ge."

is bar, woh khamosh nahin raha. is ne doctor ki traf dekha — aur kaha:

"dorana sirf tangon se nahin hota."

doctor ne ise dekha — is ki aankhon mein hairat thi.

"tum ne kaha ke mein dobara nahin dor sakta — magar tum ne yeh nahin kaha ke mein dobara nahin jeet sakta. dorane ke b-ghair bhi jeeta jaa sakta hai — mein ne yeh sikh liya hai."

mashin band hui toh hamara khamosh tha — magar is ki aankhon mein woh chiz thi jo pehle nahin thi: aman.

woh utha aur sab ki traf dekha — aur woh maskraya.

"tum jante ho — mein ne aaj pehli bar mhasos kiya ke shayad mein is hadase ko badalana nahin chahta. kyunke agar woh hadasa na hota — toh mein tum logon se kabhi na milta. aur yeh dosti — yeh kisi bhi dor se zyada qimati hai."

---

ab aayan ki bari thi.

woh kursi ki traf brha — is ka dil zor zor se dharak raha tha, magar is ke qadam paki thay. doctor rhan ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein sawal tha, aur dua bhi.

"tum tayar ho?"

aayan ne mashin ki traf dekha — kursi, helmet, taarein. woh janta tha ke jab woh is kursi par baithe ga toh woh apane bhai ki maut ko dobara jaye ga — woh ise dekhe ga, woh ise sne ga, woh har chiz ko waise hi mhasos kare ga jaise is din kiya tha.

magar woh janta tha — woh is bar nahin bhage ga.

"haan," is ne kaha — aur is ki aawaz mein yaqeen tha. "mein tayar hoon."

woh kursi par bith gaya — doctor rhan ne helmet is ke sar par rakha — aur aayan ne aankhein band kar lein.

woh tayar tha.

apane bhai se malane ke liay.
