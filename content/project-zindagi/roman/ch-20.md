---

woh sab kaman rom mein baithe thay — magar is bar woh khamoshi bhaari thi.

b-laal chala gaya tha. woh in mein se ek tha — magar ab woh kabhi nahin tha. is ka nam kisi ko yaad nahin tha, is ka chehra kisi ki yaad mein nahin tha, is ki khani mat chaki thi.

sara ne khamoshi tori — is ki aawaz mein woh kantrol nahin tha jo pehle tha: "yeh khatranak hai."

sab ne is ki traf dekha.

"yeh program — yeh mhafoz nahin hai. hum apani yaadein kho sakte hain. hum apane aap ko kho sakte hain."

hamara ne sar halaya — is ki aawaz mein mazaq nahin tha: "yeh paagal pan hai. hum sab paagal hain ke yahan aaye."

anam ne kaha — is ki aankhein khashak thin: "magar hamare paas koi chara nahin. waps jaane ka matalab hai — wahi zindagi jo humein yahan lai."

maئara khamosh thi. woh apane haath dekh rahi thi — is ki aankhon mein khauf tha, woh khauf jo is din aaya tha jab is ne b-laal ko sarak par khara dekha tha, maskrate huye, unhein bhole huye.

aayan utha — woh janta tha ke ise ks se baat krani hai.

---

doctor rhan apane aafas mein baitha tha — is ke samne kaghazaat thay, magar is ki aankhein kaghazaat par nahin thin. woh daur kahein dekh raha tha, is ki aankhon mein woh udaasi thi jo aayan ne pehle bhi dekhi thi.

"aap ne kaha tha ke aisa nahin ho ga," aayan ne darwazay se andar aate hi kaha.

doctor rhan ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein hairat nahin thi. "mein ne kaha tha ke koi nahin chhoray ga. b-laal ne chhor diya — toh woh pehle hi is maqam par tha jahan bhol jaana behtar tha yaad rakhane se."

aayan ne is ki traf dekha — is ke chehray par ghussa tha, magar is ke andar sirf sawal thay. "kiya yahi aap ka program hai? logon ko bholana skhana?"

"nahin," doctor rhan ne kaha — is ki aawaz naram thi, magar is mein woh chiz thi jo aayan ko rok rahi thi. "yeh program logon ko jina skhata hai. magar kuch log — woh atana tot chake hote hain ke jine ka matalab sirf bhol jaana hota hai."

aayan khamosh raha.

"b-laal ne apane dost ki maut saat saal tak athaئe rakha — is ne kabhi ise prosis nahin kiya, kabhi ise qabol nahin kiya. jab mashin ne ise woh yaad dakhaئi toh woh ise bradashat nahin kar ska — aur is ne bholana chana."

doctor rhan ne gehri saans li.

"yeh is ka haq tha."

---

aayan doctor rhan ke samne bith gaya — is ka ghussa ab khatam ho chaka tha, is ki jagah ek aur chiz aa gai thi: tajasas.

"kt ne log bhol gaye?"

doctor rhan ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein woh chiz thi jo jawab nahin dena chahti thi.

"batayiay," aayan ne asarar kiya. "mein jaanna chahta hoon."

doctor rhan khamosh raha — lamba khamoshi. phir is ne apane peeche deewar par lage nishaan ki traf dekha — wahi darakht, wahi jarin, wahi shakhin.

"is se fraq nahin prata," is ne kaha. "jo chala gaya — woh chala gaya. jo ra gaya — ise aage brhana hai."

"magar —"

"aayan," doctor rhan ne ise roka — is ki aawaz mein woh chiz thi jo alataja thi, ya shayad antabaa. "kuch sawalon ke jawab tum khod dhondo ge — jab tum tayar ho ge. abhi nahin."

---

aayan waps kaman rom mein aaya — is ka zhan bhara hawa tha, magar is ke dil mein ek chiz wazh thi: woh nahin bhage ga. woh yaad rakhe ga — chahe woh yaad ise ktana bhi takleef de.

is ne kamray mein dekha — maئara ab bhi khamosh thi, hamara ab bhi prishan tha, sara ab bhi soch mein dobi hui thi. aur kaunay mein ek nayi kursi thi — jis par koi nahin baitha tha.

doctor rhan ne kaha tha: "jab ek jata hai toh doosra aata hai."

aayan ne is khali kursi ko dekha — aur is ke dil mein ek ajib sa dar pida hawa. kaun aaye ga is khali kursi par? aur kab tak woh khod is kursi par baitha rahe ga?

is ne apani aankhein band kin — aur dua mangi, pehli bar bahut dinon mein, ke woh atana mazabot ho ke ise bholana na pre.

kyunke bholane se bara koi azab nahin — aur woh azab bradashat nahin kar sakta tha.
