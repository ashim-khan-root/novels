---

ek saal guzar gaya tha — aur zindagi chal rahi thi.

hamara ka school ab sheher bhar mein mashahor tha — is ke bachchay dor jitate thay, magar woh unhein sirf dorana nahin skhata tha — woh unhein skhata tha ke zindagi mein dorane se bhi aham chizin hain.

sara ne hospital mein ek naya program sharoa kiya tha — doctoron ke liay mintal hilath sporat. woh doctoron ko skhati thi ke apana khayal rakhna bhi atana hi zaroori hai jatana marizon ka.

anam ki art tharapi ab ek bara sntr ban chaki thi — aur woh in logon ki madad kar rahi thi jo apane zakhamon ko rangon mein dhaal sakte thay.

maئara aur is ki behen ab har hafate milti thin — woh fasla jo pachis saalon mein bana tha ab sirf ek yaad tha. woh donon ab ek doosray ke liay woh sab kuch thin jo woh pehle nahin thin: behnein.

zinab aur doctor rhan ne projikt zindagi ko teen naye sheheron mein phila diya tha — aur zinab ab khod ek mahar thi, jo dosaron ko skha rahi thi ke kaise yaadein insaan ko tore sakti hain — aur kaise wahi yaadein insaan ko jor sakti hain.

doctor rhan — woh apani beti ki qabr par har atawar ko jata tha. magar ab woh ro kar nahin aata tha — woh maskra kar aata tha. kyunke ise pata tha ke is ki beti ne jo mashin is ke liay banai thi — woh ab sinkron logon ki zandagayan badal rahi thi.

---

aayan — woh ab ek makamal konaslar tha.

woh roz apane daftar jata tha — jahan is ki meez par asman ki tswir hoti thi, aur woh diary jis mein is ne apana safr likha tha. woh in logon ki madad kurta tha jo kabhi is ki tarah tot chake thay — aur woh unhein wahi skhata tha jo is ne sikha tha: ke totana aakhari marhala nahin hai.

zid ab bhi is ke paas aata tha — magar ab woh khamosh nahin rehta tha. woh bolata tha, hansta tha, aur kabhi kabhi woh aayan se kehta tha: "tum ne mujhe skhaya ke zanda rehna mumkin hai — aur mein tumhein kabhi nahin bholon ga."

---

aaj — ek saal baad — aayan ne woh lafafa khola jo is ne projikt zindagi mein apane pehle din likha tha.

woh khat jo is ne khod ko likha tha:
"pyaray aanay wale aayan,

agar tum yeh khat prh rahe ho toh tum is projikt mein kamayab ho chake ho — tum zanda ho, aur tum ne apane dar ka samana kiya hai.

mujhe nahin pata ke tum wahan kiya dekho ge — magar mein janta hoon ke tum mazabot ho. tum hamesha se mazabot thay — sirf tumhein yeh yaad nahin tha.

yaad rakhna — tum asman ki maut ke zama dar nahin ho. tum sirf apani zindagi ke zama dar ho. aur mein chahta hoon ke tum is zindagi ko makamal jayo — is ke har lamhe ko, is ke har dard ko, is ki har khushi ko.

tum se mohabbat kurta hoon,
purana aayan"

---

aayan ne khat parha — aur is ki aankhon mein aansoo thay, magar woh maskra raha tha.

is ne khat ko snabhal kar rakh diya — aur woh khirki ke paas gaya. bahar suraj dob raha tha, magar andhera ise drata nahin tha. kyunke woh janta tha ke suraj har subah phir nikalay ga — aur woh ise dikhane ke liay mojod ho ga.

woh qabrastan gaya — asman ki qabr par.

woh qabr ke paas baitha — magar is bar is ki aankhon mein aansoo nahin thay. woh maskra raha tha.

"mein ne tum se wada kiya tha — ke mein zanda rhon ga. aur mein ne woh wada nabhaya."

is ne qabr ke patthar ko haath lagaya:

"mein tumhein nahin bhola, asman — aur mein kabhi nahin bholon ga. magar ab tamhari yaad mujhe torati nahin — woh mujhe jorati hai. tum mire saath ho — har roz, har lamha, har saans mein."

woh uth kar khara hawa — aur is ne aasman ki traf dekha.

"shukriya, asman — mujhe zanda rehna skhane ke liay."

---

woh qabrastan se bahar nkla toh bahar roshni thi — suraj dob chaka tha, magar is ki jagah stare aa chake thay.

aayan ne unhein dekha — aur woh pehli bar samjha ke zindagi ka matalab sirf zanda rehna nahin — zindagi ka matalab hai har andhire ke baad roshni dekhna, har grane ke baad athana, aur har dard ke baad bhi mohabbat karna.

woh apane ghar ki traf brha — jahan is ke walad is ka antazar kar rahe thay, jahan is ki zindagi thi, jahan is ka mastaqabl tha.

aur woh maskraya — kyunke ise pata tha:

yeh sirf ek khani ka akhatatam nahin tha — yeh ek nayi zindagi ka aaghaz tha.

— khatam —
