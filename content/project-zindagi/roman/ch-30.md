---

sara waps aa gai thi — magar woh sara nahin rahi thi jo gai thi.

woh khamosh thi — pehle se zyada khamosh. woh kamray mein aati, ek kaunay mein bith jati, aur ghanton khamosh rehti. is ki aankhein khokhali thin, is ke haath kanapte rehte thay, aur woh mashin ke kamray ki traf dikhane se bhi drati thi.

aayan is ke paas gaya — is ne is ke samne bith kar is ki traf dekha. "sara —"

"nahin," is ne ise kat diya — is ki aawaz mein woh skhati thi jo pehle bhi thi, magar ab woh skhati toti hui thi. "mat karo. mat poocho."

"mein sirf —"

"tum nahin samjho ge!"

woh is par chali — is ki aankhon mein ghussa tha, magar is ghase ke peeche dar tha, aur is dar ke peeche woh chiz thi jisay woh kha nahin sakti thi.

"tum ne kisi ko marate nahin dekha! tum nahin jante ke kisi ki zindagi tamhare haathon mein ho aur tum kuch na kar sako — yeh kisa hota hai!"

aayan khamosh raha.

is ne sara ki traf dekha — aur ise apane bhai ki maut yaad aa gai. is ne ise marate dekha tha — is ki aankhon ke samne, is ke haathon mein, is ki god mein. magar is ne kabhi kisi ko nahin bataya tha.

sara ro pri — is ka ghussa khatam ho gaya, aur sirf dard ra gaya. woh roti rahi, aur aayan is ke paas baitha raha — khamosh, beharakt, is ke saath.

---

aayan wahan se nkla toh is ka dil bhaari tha.

woh kaman rom ki traf jaa raha tha — magar raste mein woh rak gaya. ek larki kursi par baithi thi — is ne ise pehle kabhi nahin dekha tha.

woh unees saal ki thi — chhoti si, ptali, is ki aankhon ke neeche ghere halaqe thay, is ke baal bakhare huye thay, aur woh is tarah baithi thi jaise woh kaئi dinon se soi nahin thi. is ke haath mein ek lafafa tha — krim rang ka kaghaz, jis par wahi darakht bana hawa tha.

aayan ka dil dharaka — woh lafafa, wahi lafafa jo ise mila tha, wahi jo is ki zindagi badalane wala tha.

"tum kaun ho?" is ne poocha — is ki aawaz mein hairat thi, aur dar bhi.

larki ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhein khali thin, jaise woh andar se mar chaki ho. is ne lafafa athaya — ise aayan ki traf brhaya, jaise woh kha rahi ho: yeh dekho, mein bhi tamhari tarah hoon.

aayan ne lafafe ko dekha — is par likha tha: "projikt zindagi — aap mantakhab ho gaye hain."

---

doctor rhan kamray mein aaya — is ne larki ko dekha, phir aayan ko dekha. is ki aankhon mein woh chiz thi jo thakawat thi, ya shayad woh qabolit jo is haqiqat ke saath aati hai ke yeh kabhi khatam nahin ho ga.

"yeh naya sharik hai," is ne kaha. "jab ek jata hai toh doosra aata hai. yeh saئikl hai."

aayan ne larki ki traf dekha — woh ab bhi khamosh thi, is ki aankhein ab bhi khali thin. woh atani chhoti thi — aur atani toti hui.

"kaun hai yeh?" aayan ne poocha — is ki aawaz mein ghussa tha, ya shayad woh bebasi jo is ne khod mhasos ki thi jab woh pehli bar yahan aaya tha.

doctor rhan ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein woh sach tha jisay woh chahapa nahin sakta tha:

"is ka bhai gazashata hafate mar gaya. is ne khod ko kamray mein band kar liya — teen din tak kuch nahin khaya, kisi se baat nahin ki. is ka rask rit — pachanawe fisad."

aayan ne larki ko dekha — is ki aankhon mein is ne khod ko dekha, apane aap ko teen hafate pehle, jab woh bhi asi kursi par baitha tha, asi lafafe ke saath, asi khali pan ke saath.

aur woh smajah gaya — yeh kabhi khatam nahin ho ga. jab tak ek shakhas theek hota hai, doosra tot jata hai. aur projikt zindagi hamesha khula rahe ga.

---

aayan is raat apane kamray mein baitha raha — is ne is nayi larki ke bare mein socha, is ke bare mein jis ne apana bhai kho diya tha, balakl is ki tarah.

woh is se kiya kahe? woh ise kaise bataye ke woh bhi is raste se guzara hai — ke woh bhi is kursi par baitha tha, is lafafe ko pakaray, asi dar ke saath?

is ne apani diary mein likha:

"aaj ek nayi larki aai — is ka bhai mar gaya. mein ne ise dekha aur apane aap ko dekha — teen hafate pehle, jab mein bhi asi tarah tota hawa tha. aur mein ne socha — shayad yahi projikt zindagi ka asl maqasad hai. hum theek nahin hote — hum sirf dosaron ko theek hote dekh kar apane aap ko snabhal lete hain."

is ne diary band ki aur apane bistar par lit gaya. kal is ka sishan tha — woh sishan jis mein woh apane bhai ki maut ka samana kare ga.

aur woh tayar tha.

pehli bar, woh waqai tayar tha.
