sara ne doosra sishan manga tha.

woh kursi par baithi toh is ka chehra wahi tha jo pehle tha — beharakt, kantrolad, balakl saaf. magar is ki aankhon mein kuch aur tha — azm, ya shayad janon.

"woh mariza," is ne kaha — is ki aawaz mein alataja thi, magar woh alataja jo kisi aur chiz se pida hui thi. "mein ise dobara dekhna chahti hoon."

doctor rhan ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein fikr thi. "sara, tum jante ho ke tum kuch nahin badal saktin. mashin sirf tumhein dakha sakti hai — tum is mein tabadili nahin la saktin."

"mein jaanti hoon," sara ne kaha. "magar mein — mein sirf dekhna chahti hoon. ek bar aur."

doctor rhan khamosh raha — lamba, bhaari khamoshi. phir is ne sar halaya. "theek hai. magar yaad rakho — tum sirf dekho gi. kuch nahin badalo gi."

sara ne sar halaya — aur mashin sharoa ho gai.

aur phir mashin bagrane lagi.

woh ise bahar le gaye — is ki aankhein band thin, is ke haath dhile thay, aur woh is tarah leti thi jaise woh so gai ho — magar koi nahin janta tha ke woh jaagay gi bhi ya nahin.

aayan khara dekhta raha — is ka dil dhak dhak kar raha tha, is ke haath kanap rahe thay, aur woh kuch nahin kar sakta tha.

doctor rhan is ke paas aaya — is ka chehra safed tha, is ke haath kanap rahe thay — pehli bar, aayan ne ise kanapte dekha tha.

"yeh — yeh mashin —" aayan ne kaha — is ki aawaz mein khauf tha.

doctor rhan ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein woh chiz thi jo woh nahin dakhana chahta tha: khauf.
