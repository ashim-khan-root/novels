---

agli subah aayan ne apane walad se kaha:

"mujhe qabrastan jaana hai."

is ke walad ne ise dekha — aur woh smajah gaye ke yeh safr ktana aham hai. unhon ne kuch nahin kaha — sirf sar halaya, aur aayan ko alwida kha kar dekhte rahe jab woh darwazay se bahar nkla.

---

qabrastan sheher ke kanare par tha — purane darakht, khamosh raste, aur hawa mein woh khamoshi jo sirf maradon ki jagah hoti hai.

aayan andar dakhal hawa — is ke qadam bhaari thay, magar woh raka nahin. woh janta tha ke ks qabr par jaana hai — woh jagah jahan woh aath saal pehle khara hawa tha, is din jab is ne apane bhai ko mitti mein dafan kiya tha.

woh qabr ke samne khara tha — patthar par asman ka nam tha, tareekh pidaئash, tareekh wafat. do tareekhein — atane kam saalon ke dramayan.

woh bith gaya — is ki tangin kanap rahi thin, magar woh bith gaya.

---

is ne qabr ke patthar ko haath lagaya — thanda, nam, jaise subah ki awas abhi tak is par thi.

aur is ne baatein karna sharoa kar dein:

"asman — mein aaya hoon. tumhein pata hai, mujhe bahut der lag gai. mein har roz sochata tha ke aaon ga, magar har bar dar jata tha. dar — yahi woh chiz thi jis ne mujhe aath saal qaid rakha."

is ki aawaz kanap rahi thi — magar woh raka nahin:

"magar ab mein drata nahin hoon — kam az kam, atana nahin jatana pehle drata tha. mein ne ek projikt mein hasa liya — jahan log apani yadon ka samana karte hain. aur mein ne wahan tumhein dobara dekha — is din gaari mein, jab tum hans rahe thay aur gana ga rahe thay."

is ki aankhon mein aansoo thay — magar woh maskra raha tha:

"tumhein pata hai, tamhari woh hansi — woh hansi jo mein bhol gaya tha — woh ab mujhe yaad hai. aur mein ise kabhi nahin bholon ga."

---

woh raka — is ne gehri saans li. hawa thandi thi, magar woh kanap nahin raha tha.

"tumhein pata hai, asman — tum ne mujhe bahut kuch skhaya. zanda ra kar bhi, aur mar kar bhi. tum ne mujhe skhaya ke zindagi ktani qimati hai — aur ke har lamha thafa hai. mein ne aath saal yeh thafa zaئa kiya — apane aap ko qasor war thahara kar, khod ko saza dete huye."

is ne qabr ke patthar par apana sar rakh diya:

"magar ab mein badal gaya hoon — aur mein chahta hoon ke tum yeh jano. mein ab theek hoon, asman. waqai theek hoon."

---

aur phir — woh roya.

woh roya jaise woh aath saalon mein kabhi nahin roya tha. woh roya, jaise is ke andar ka har zakham phot pra ho — magar woh aansoo dard ke nahin thay. woh aansoo rahai ke thay.

aur phir — woh hansa.

ek sachi, khuli hansi.

"tum ne mujhe bahut kuch skhaya, asman — zanda ra kar bhi, aur mar kar bhi. tum ne mujhe skhaya ke mohabbat woh hai jo maut ke baad bhi baqi rehti hai — aur mein tum se bahut mohabbat kurta hoon, bhai."

woh uth kar khara hawa — is ne qabr par phool rakhe jo woh saath laya tha, is ke psndida phool — safed glab.

"mein ab jaa raha hoon, asman — magar mein waps aaon ga. aur is bar — mein dar kar nahin aaon ga. mein tum se malane aaon ga — jaise bhai apane bhai se malane aata hai."

woh mara — aur woh qabrastan se bahar nkla.

is ka dil bhaari tha, magar is ka roh halka tha — aur woh pehli bar samjha ke jaane dine ka matalab bholana nahin hai. jaane dine ka matalab hai — mohabbat ko dard se aazad karna.
