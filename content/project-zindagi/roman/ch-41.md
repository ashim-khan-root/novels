---

sheher se do so klomitr daur ek chhotay se qasabe mein — maئara ek chhoti si dakan ke samne khari thi.

yeh wahi dakan thi jahan is ki behen kaam karti thi — ise pata tha kyunke is ne pachis saalon mein khamoshi se is ki har khabar rakhi thi. ise pata tha ke is ki behen ne shadi ki, is ke do bachchay hain, aur woh khush hai.

yahi is ke liay kaafi tha — yeh jaanna ke is ki behen khush hai.

magar ab woh yahan khari thi — is ke haath kanap rahe thay, is ka dil zor zor se dharak raha tha, aur woh andar jaane se dar rahi thi.

darwaza khula — aur is ki behen samne khari thi.

woh pachis saal pehle ki tarah thi — wahi aankhein, wahi muskurahat, bas thodi boorhi ho gai thi, thodi paki.

donon ne ek doosray ko dekha — ek lamha jaise brason ke brabr tha.

phir maئara ne kaha — aur is ki aawaz kanap rahi thi:

"tum wahi ho — balakl wahi jisi tum thin jab tum gai thin."

---

woh dakan ke andar baithi thin — chai ke cup in ke samne thay, magar kisi ne chai ko haath nahin lagaya.

khamoshi bhaari thi — woh khamoshi jo pachis saalon ke fasle ko ek bar khatam karna chahti thi, magar nahin kar paa rahi thi.

phir maئara ne kehna sharoa kiya:

"mein ne tumhein dhonda — pehle saal bahut dhonda. phir mein ne jaan liya ke tum khush ho, aur mein ne dhondana chhor diya. magar mein ne kabhi tumhein jaane nahin diya — tum mire andar thi, har roz, har lamha."

is ki behen khamosh thi — is ki aankhon mein aansoo thay, magar woh ro nahin rahi thi.

"mein yahan tumhein batane aai hoon — mein tumhein jaane dine aai hoon. magar is se pehle mein tumhein batana chahti hoon — mein ne tum se kabhi nafrat nahin ki."

---

is ki behen ne pehli bar is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein woh dard tha jo sirf wahi smajah sakti thi jis ne apane ghar ko chhora ho:

"tum ne majah se nafrat nahin ki — magar mein ne socha ke tum karo gi."

maئara ne ise dekha — ise smajah nahin aaya:

"kiya matalab?"

"mein ne socha ke tum majah se nafrat karo gi — is liay ke mein ne tumhein chhor diya. mein ne socha ke agar mein ne tumhein bataya ke mein khush hoon toh tumhein aur takleef ho gi — is liay ke tum wahan phanasi hui thin aur mein aazad thi."

is ke aansoo behenay lage:

"magar mein tumhein kabhi nahin bholi — tum miri behen ho, aur tum hamesha miri behen reho gi."

---

maئara apani jagah se uthi — aur woh apani behen ke paas jaa baithi. is ne is ka haath thama — aur is ne kaha:

"mein tumhein maaf karti hoon — agar tum ne kabhi socha ke mein tum se nafrat karon gi toh woh ghalat tha. tum miri behen ho — aur mein tum se mohabbat karti hoon. tum ne jo kiya woh tamhare liay shih tha — aur mein ne ise jaane diya."

is ki behen is se lapt gai — aur woh donon rawaئin, jaise pachis saalon ka fasla in aanson mein bha gaya ho.

woh donon ek doosray se lagi hui thin — aur woh khamoshi, jo pehle bhaari thi, ab praskon thi.

kyunke mohabbat — khawaa ktani bhi der se aaye — hamesha shih waqt par aati hai.
