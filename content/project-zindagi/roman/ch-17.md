---

subah hote hi aayan doctor rhan ke aafas gaya.

is ke haath mein is ki file ki tswir thi — woh jo is ne raat li thi, is baat ka saboot ke woh janta hai. is ne darwaza khola — doctor rhan meez par baitha tha, kuch prh raha tha, jaise woh is ka antazar kar raha ho.

"mein raat wahan tha," aayan ne kaha — is ki aawaz mein ghussa tha, magar woh ghussa kantrol mein tha. "mein ne apani file dekhi. 89 fisad — yeh kiya hai?"

doctor rhan ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein koi hairat nahin thi, jaise woh janta ho ke yeh din aaye ga.

"woh nambr hai — tamhari nafasayati halat ka andaza."

"mein janta hoon ke woh nambr kiya hai — mein jaanna chahta hoon ke is ka matalab kiya hai. kiya tum mujhe yeh bata rahe ho ke mein — ke mein —"

woh jumla makamal nahin kar ska.

doctor rhan ne kaha — narami se, magar sachaئi se: "tum is liay yahan ho kyunke tum kahein aur nahin jaa sakte thay. yeh aakhari moqa hai — tamhara, aur is kamray mein baithe har ek ka."

aayan khamosh raha.

"har sharik ko pehle se janacha gaya," doctor rhan ne jari rakha. "tum sab ne kahein na kahein haar man li thi. kuch ne apani zindagi mein, kuch ne apane khwabon mein, kuch ne apane aap mein. projikt zindagi — yeh aakhari moqa hai tum sab ke liay."

"magar kyun?" aayan ne poocha — is ki aawaz mein ghussa kam ho gaya tha, ab sirf sawal tha. "tum yeh sab kyun kar rahe ho? in logon ki madad kyun kar rahe ho jinhein tum jante bhi nahin?"

doctor rhan khamosh raha — ek lamha, do lamhe. phir is ne kaha:

"kyunke mein janta hoon ke is andhire mein rehna kisa hota hai — jab tum athana nahin chahte, jab tum khana nahin chahte, jab tum zanda rehna nahin chahte. aur mein janta hoon ke is andhire se nklana kisa hota hai — jab tum ek din uth kar dekhte ho ke suraj ab bhi nklata hai."

is ne gehri saans li — aur is ki aankhon mein woh chiz thi jo aayan ne pehle nahin dekhi thi: kamazori.

"mein yeh program is liay bana raha hoon take koi aur is raste par na chale jis par mein chala."

---

doctor rhan ne ise mashin ka kamra dakhaya — woh kamra jahan woh har roz jate thay, magar aaj woh ise pehli bar dekh raha tha.

"yeh mashin sirf yaadein nahin dakhati," doctor rhan ne kaha. "yeh tamhare damagh ki lharon ko rikarad karti hai — har jazabati radamal, har dar, har woh chiz jisay tum ne daba diya hai. aur phir —"

"aur phir?"

"aur phir woh tumhein skhati hai ke in radamal ko kaise badalana hai. yeh tamhare damagh ko dobara program karti hai take tum mazi ko ek naye zawaye se dekh sako — b-ghair is dar ke jo tumhein brason se roke huye hai."

aayan ne mashin ki traf dekha — woh kursi, woh helmet, woh taar. woh sab kuch jo is ne pehle bhi dekha tha, magar ab woh ise makhatalaf nazar se dekh raha tha.

"magar agar koi chahorana chahe — agar koi bradashat na kar sake — toh kiya ho ga?"

doctor rhan khamosh raha — lamba, bhaari khamoshi.

"aisa nahin ho ga," is ne aakhar mein kaha.

"kyun?"

doctor rhan ne aayan ki aankhon mein dekha — aur is ki aankhon mein woh chiz thi jo sirf sachaئi ho sakti thi:

"kyunke woh shakhas khod ko is se bhi zyada nuqsan phanacha chaka hota hai jo hum kar sakte hain."

---

aayan apane kamray mein waps aaya — doctor rhan ke alfaz is ke zhan mein gonaj rahe thay.

woh bistar par lit gaya — is ki aankhein chhat par thin, wahi daraar jisay woh ab phachanta tha, magar ab woh ise makhatalaf nazar se dekh raha tha. shayad woh daraar sirf ek daraar nahin thi — shayad woh is ke andar ki daraar thi jo bahar nazar aa rahi thi.

is ne apane haath dekhe — woh kanap rahe thay, magar is bar khauf se nahin. woh is liay kanap rahe thay kyunke woh smajah gaya tha — woh bach sakta tha.

doctor rhan bach gaya tha.

toh woh bhi bach sakta tha.

is ne aankhein band kin — aur pehli bar, is ne mhasos kiya ke shayad umeed nam ki koi chiz waqai hoti hai. shayad woh waqai theek ho sakta hai.

magar phir is ne socha — agar doctor rhan theek ho gaya, toh woh atana prasarar kyun hai? woh kyun sab kuch nahin bata raha? is ki aankhon mein woh udaasi kyun hai jo kabhi khatam nahin hoti?

shayad doctor rhan theek nahin hawa tha.

shayad woh sirf zanda rehna sikh gaya tha — magar theek hona kabhi nahin sikha.

aur yeh soch aayan ko is se bhi zyada dra rahi thi.
