---

subah hui toh pora maidan khamosh tha — jaise har chiz jaanti thi ke yeh aakhari din hai.

aayan ne apana big pik kiya — bahut chhota, jaise woh andar aaya tha. asi big mein, wahi kapre, wahi jootay. magar woh shakhas jo ab bahar jaa raha tha — woh woh nahin tha jo andar aaya tha.

is ne kamray ko ek bar dekha — chhoti meez, khirki, plang. yeh kamra is ka dard tha, is ka dar tha, magar yahi kamra is ka ghar bhi tha — jahan is ne khod ko paya tha.

woh bahar nkla toh sab pehle se mojod thay — maئara, hamara, sara, anam, zinab. in ke chaharon par muskurahat thi, magar in ki aankhon mein nami thi.

---

maئara pehle is ke paas aai — is ne apani klaئi se ek purani chori utaari aur aayan ke haath mein rakh di.

"yeh miri behen ki thi — is ne mujhe is din di thi jab woh gai thi. mein ne brason ise nahin phana, magar aaj phana — aur ab mein chahti hoon ke yeh tamhare paas rahe."

aayan ne ise dekha — chaandi ki chori, sada magar khubsurat:

"kyun?"

"is liay ke tum ne mujhe skhaya ke kuch log tamhari zindagi mein aate hain — tumhein badalane ke liay, na ke tamhare saath rhane ke liay."

---

hamara ne ise ek note bak di — is ka purana note bak, jis mein is ne apani dor ke dinon mein likha tha:

"yeh mire sab se mushkil dinon ki khani hai — jab mein ne socha ke mein kuch nahin hoon. ab mujhe is ki zarorat nahin — kyunke mein janta hoon ke mein kuch hoon. magar shayad tumhein kabhi zarorat pre — kisi aise shakhas ke liay jo yeh smajahe ke grana aakhari marhala nahin hai."

sara is ke paas aai — is ne kuch nahin diya, sirf is ki traf dekha, aur kaha:

"agar kabhi zarorat ho toh mujhe blana — chahe raat ho, chahe din, chahe mein duniya ke kisi bhi kaunay mein hoon. tum ne mujhe dekha jab mein khod ko nahin dekh paa rahi thi — aur mein kabhi nahin bholon gi ke tum ne mujhe woh dakhaya jo mein ban sakti hoon."

---

anam is ke paas aai — is ne gle lagaya, mazaboti se, jaise woh b-laal ko alwida kha rahi ho jo is ne kabhi nahin kaha tha:

"tum ise yaad rakhna — hum mein se har ek ko. kyunke hum tumhein kabhi nahin bholin ge."

zinab — woh nayi larki jis ne abhi apana safr sharoa kiya tha — khamosh khari rahi, phir boli:

"mein ne tum logon ko dekh kar sikha ke zanda rehna mumkin hai — chahe ktani bhi mushkil kyun na ho. aur mein tamhari tarah mazabot banon gi."

---

bas aa gai — neeli, purani, wahi bas jo unhein le kar aai thi.

ek ek kar ke sab swar ho gaye — maئara pehle, phir hamara, phir sara, phir anam. zinab peeche ra gai — is ka safr abhi khatam nahin hawa tha.

aayan sab se aakhar mein tha — is ne doctor rhan ki traf dekha, jo darwazay par khara tha, is ke chehray par wahi muskurahat thi jo pehle din thi — magar ab is muskurahat mein sukoon tha.

"doctor sahab —"

"haan, aayan?"

"aap ne mujhe waps aanay ka rasta dakhaya — jab mein gam ho chaka tha. mein —"

doctor rhan ne is ka haath thama — aur is ki aankhon mein woh chamak thi jo sirf is shakhas ki hoti hai jis ne apana maqasad paa liya ho:

"tum ne khod ko paya, aayan — mein ne sirf woh aaina dakhaya tha jo tamhare andar tha."

---

aayan bas mein baitha — is ne khirki se bahar dekha.

woh amarat — jis ne ise andar liya tha jab woh tota hawa tha — ab is ke peeche ra gai thi. doctor rhan khara ise dekh raha tha, is ka haath hawa mein tha — alwida kehta hawa.

aayan ne bhi haath halaya — aur is ne mhasos kiya jaise woh apane purane nafas ko alwida kha raha hai.

bas chal pri — aur aayan ne aage dekha.

ghar ki traf.

zindagi ki traf.
