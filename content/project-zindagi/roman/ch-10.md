---

agla sishan hamara ka tha.

woh kursi ki traf brha toh is ke qadam mein woh aatamad nahin tha jo is ki baaton mein tha. woh raka, mar kar sab ki traf dekha, aur maskraya — wahi muskurahat jo is ne pehle din bhi dakhaئi thi, magar ab sab jante thay ke yeh muskurahat ek dhaal hai.

"miri yadon mein toh koi drama nahin, yar," is ne kaha. "bas ek dor thi, jo mein haar gaya."

kisi ne nahin hanasaya.

hamara ne kandhay achakaئe aur kursi par bith gaya. doctor rhan ne helmet is ke sar par rakha — aur is ki aankhon mein woh chamak jo hamesha rehti thi, achank ghaئab ho gai.

"tum kuch nahin badal sakte," doctor rhan ne kaha. "sirf apana radamal."

hamara ne sar halaya — aur is ki aankhein band ho gaئin.

---

woh ek astidim tha — bara, khula, logon se bhara hawa.

sitin bhari hui thin, hazaaron log cheekh rahe thay, jahande lhara rahe thay. aur neeche trik par — hamara.

woh dor raha tha.

is ka jism mashin ki tarah kaam kar raha tha — har qadam pimaئash se, har saans kantrol mein. woh aage tha — dosaron se aage, apane rikarad se aage, apane khwabon se ek qadam aage.

is ke chehray par psina tha, magar is ke chehray par muskurahat bhi thi. woh jeet raha tha. woh janta tha ke woh jeet raha hai. hazaaron log is ka nam le rahe thay — "hamara! hamara!"

aur phir — ek aawaz.

is ki tang se aai — ek ajib si aawaz, jaise koi chiz tot gai ho.

hamara ki tang ne jawab dena chhor diya.

woh gra — atani tizi se jaise kisi ne ise neeche khinach liya ho. is ka ghatana zameen se takraya, is ka chehra raste se ragr khaya, aur woh wohein pra raha — astidim ke bich mein, hazaaron logon ke samne, is dor ke dramayan jo is ki zindagi ka sab se aham lamha tha.

khamoshi.

phir cheekhein — magar woh khushi ki nahin thin. woh khauf ki thin.

---

hospital ka kamra. safed deewarein. dawaئayawan ki bo.

doctor aaya — ek boorha aadmi, chehray par woh hamadradi jo marizon ko di jati hai. is ne hamara ki traf dekha aur kaha:

"tum dobara kabhi nahin dor sako ge."

hamara ne doctor ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein sawal tha, magar woh sawal poochh nahin ska. woh sirf is tarah dekhta raha jaise is ne abhi abhi apani zindagi khatam hote dekhi ho.

"hum ne har mumkin koshash ki," doctor ne kaha. "magar ghatana — yeh bahut bari tarah tota hai. tum chal sako ge, magar dorana — woh mumkin nahin."

hamara ne aankhein band kar lein. is ne kuch nahin kaha. woh sirf khamosh raha — is khamoshi mein jo is ki zindagi ka naya mamol banane wali thi.

---

mashin band hui toh hamara ki aankhein khuli thin magar woh kuch dekh nahin raha tha.

woh kursi par baitha raha — is ka jism wohein tha magar woh kahein aur tha, is astidim mein, is dor par, is lamhe jahan is ki zindagi badal gai thi. is ke chehray par aansoo nahin thay — sirf woh khali pan tha jo khwab marane ke baad aata hai.

woh utha — b-ghair kisi se aankh malaئe, b-ghair kuch kahe. woh darwazay ki traf brha aur bahar chala gaya.

kamray mein khamoshi thi. sab ek doosray ko dekh rahe thay — kuch pal pehle tak hamara in mein sab se zyada hanasne wala tha, aur ab woh is tarah chala gaya jaise woh kabhi tha hi nahin.

aayan utha — aur is ke peeche chala gaya.

---

hamara chhat par tha.

woh deewar se tik laga kar khara tha — is ki aankhein daur kahein thin, is ke haath jib mein thay, aur woh is tarah khara tha jaise woh arane ki koshash kar raha ho magar is ke par tote huye thay.

aayan is ke paas aaya — thodai fasle par raka. donon khamosh thay.

kuch der baad, hamara ne kaha — atani dheemi aawaz mein ke aayan ko yaqeen nahin aaya ke yeh wahi shakhas hai:

"mein roz is din ko dobara jeeta hoon."

woh raka.

"sirf fraq yeh hai ke ab mein aankhein khol kar jeeta hoon."

aayan ne kuch nahin kaha. woh sirf is ke paas khara raha — do ajanabi, ek chhat par, apane apane zakhamon ke saath.

hamara ne gehri saans li — aur phir woh maskraya. magar yeh muskurahat woh nahin thi jo woh sab ko dakhata tha. yeh chhoti thi, thaki hui, magar sachi.

"chalo, neeche chalte hain," is ne kaha. "baqayawan ko bhi hamara antazar hai."

woh donon neeche aaye — aur kamray mein waps aaye toh sab ne unhein dekha. hamara phir se maskra raha tha — magar ab sab jante thay ke is muskurahat ke peeche kiya hai.

aur woh sab pehle se zyada khamosh thay — kyunke woh jante thay ke kal in mein se kisi aur ki bari ho gi.
