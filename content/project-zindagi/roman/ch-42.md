---

chhah maa guzar gaye — aur sab kuch badal gaya tha.

hamara ek chhotay se school mein koch tha — in bachon ka jin ke paas khone ke liay kuch nahin tha, siwaye apane khwabon ke. woh unhein dorana skhata tha — magar sirf dorana nahin, woh unhein skhata tha ke grane ke baad athana kaise hota hai. aur jab is ka koi bachcha pehli bar dor jitata tha toh hamara sab se zor se hansta tha.

sara waps hospital jaa chaki thi — magar is bar woh doctor nahin thi, woh sprawaئazr thi. woh naye doctoron ko trabit deti thi — aur woh unhein skhati thi ke mariz sirf ek case nahin hai, woh ek insaan hai. aur jab kabhi koi naya doctor kisi mariz ko kho kar tot jata tha toh sara ise kehti thi: "tum kamzor nahin ho — tum sirf insaan ho."

anam ne ek art tharapi grop sharoa kiya tha — in logon ke liay jo apane zakhamon ko alfaz mein nahin baant sakte thay. woh apani pintangaz dakhati thi — woh pintangaz jo is ne projikt mein banai thin. aur jab koi is se poochta tha ke yeh kisi hain toh woh kehti thi: "yeh mire zakham hain — aur yeh mire phool hain."

maئara aur is ki behen har hafate baat karti thin — kabhi phone par, kabhi mal kar. woh fasla jo pachis saalon mein bana tha ab khatam ho chaka tha — aur woh ise dobara bana rahi thin, ek baat, ek hansi, ek aansoo ek waqt mein.

zinab ki raporat manazor ho gai thi — projikt zindagi ko klayaئaranas mal gai thi. aithaks borad ne na sirf ise jari rakhane ki ajazt di thi balke ise philane ki bhi safarash ki thi. zinab ab doctor rhan ki asastant thi — aur woh ek saath projikt ko mazid behtar bana rahe thay.

---

aayan — woh badal gaya tha.

woh ek yawanayawarasti mein nafasayat prh raha tha — matalab, woh sirf prh nahin raha tha, woh sikh raha tha ke dosaron ki madad kaise ki jaئe. is ne apani nokri chhor di thi — woh nokri jis se woh nafrat kurta tha — aur woh ab ek konaslar ke tore par trabit le raha tha.

woh har roz apane walad ke saath chai pita tha — aur woh baatein karte thay, baatein jo unhon ne brason mein nahin ki thin. is ke walad ab zyada hanaste thay — aur aayan jab bhi ise hanaste dekhta tha toh ise lagta tha ke shayad zindagi waqai badal sakti hai.

---

ek din aayan ke paas doctor rhan ka khat aaya — haath se likha hawa, purane kaghaz par:

"pyaray aayan,

mujhe khushi hai ke tum ne nafasayat ka rasta chana. tum wahan bahut achay ho ge — kyunke tum ne khod woh safr kiya hai jo tum ab dosaron ko krane mein madad karna chahte ho.

yaad rakhna — tamhara safr sirf tamhara nahin tha. tum ne woh kaam kiya jo bahut se log nahin kar pate — tum ne apane dar ka samana kiya, tum ne apane zakhamon ko dekha, aur tum ne unhein bharane diya.

ab tum dosaron ki madad kar sakte ho — aur yeh sab se bara thafa hai jo koi kisi ko de sakta hai.

tum hamesha mire dil mein reho ge — aur projikt zindagi mein hamesha tamhare liay jagah hai.

tamhara dost,
doctor rhan"

---

aayan ne khat parha — aur woh maskraya.

is ne khat ko snabhal kar rakh diya — is dabe mein jahan is ne asman ki tswir rakhi thi, aur woh diary jis mein is ne apana safr likha tha.

phir woh khirki ke paas gaya — bahar dhoop thi, aur hawa mein mosm b-haar ki khushbu thi.

is ne apane aap se kaha:

"ab mein dosaron ki madad kar sakta hoon — aur yeh mire zanda rhane ka asl maqasad hai."
