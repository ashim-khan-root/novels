---

aayan is raat so nahin ska — woh maئara ke bare mein soch raha tha, is ke haath ke bare mein jo is ke haath mein tha, is khamoshi ke bare mein jis mein donon ne sab kuch kha diya tha.

magar is ka zhan ek aur chiz par bhi tha — doctor rhan ke woh alfaz: "kyunke mein bhi kabhi tamhari tarah tha."

kiya matalab tha is ka? doctor rhan bhi kabhi aayan jisa tha? woh bhi kabhi tota tha? woh bhi kabhi is andhire mein tha jahan se nklane ka koi rasta nazar nahin aata?

aayan utha — is ke qadam ise doctor rhan ke aafas ki traf le gaye. woh janta tha ke ise wahan kuch mile ga — woh jawab jo doctor rhan ne ise dine se ankar kar diya tha.

aafas ka darwaza band tha — magar is bar ise kholana mushkil nahin tha. is ne apani asi purani kangan se taala khola — aur andar chala gaya.

is bar woh sidha meez ke paas gaya. neeche ek draz tha — band, magar is par wahi nishaan tha: woh darakht jis ki jarin gehri thin.

is ne draz khola — aur is ki saans rak gai.

---

andar fileein thin — drajanon fileein, har ek par ek nambr aur ek nam. is ne unhein dekha — kuch purane thay, kuch naye. aur har file par ek nambr tha: Subject 01 se Subject 47 tak.

pintalis sharaka — paanch saalon mein.

is ne fileein askrol kin — kuch par "makamal" likha tha, kuch par "manasokh," aur kuch par — is ka dil dharaka — "mat gaya."

aur phir is ne woh file dekhi — jo sab se neeche thi, alag rakhi hui, is par ek aur nambr tha:

"Subject 00 — Rehan Siddiqui"

doctor rhan — pehla sharik.

aayan ne file uthai — is ke haath kanap rahe thay — aur ise khola.

---

file mein doctor rhan ki pori zindagi thi — is ki shadi, is ki beti, is ki biwi ki alihadagi, aur phir is ki beti ki maut.

woh larki — is ki beti — zara. saat saal ki thi jab woh mar gai. layawakimaya. doctor rhan is waqt conference mein tha — ek aham conference, jahan woh apana risarach pipr pish kar raha tha. is ki biwi ne ise kal ki thi — magar is ne phone nahin athaya tha.

woh conference se waps aaya toh is ki beti mar chaki thi.

is ki biwi ne ise kabhi maaf nahin kiya — aur woh khod bhi kabhi maaf nahin kar ska.

file mein ek aur safha tha — asptal ka rikarad. doctor rhan chhah saal pehle asptal mein dakhal hawa tha — zhar khane ke baad. ise bacha liya gaya tha — ek sathi doctor ne ise waqt par paya tha.

woh sathi — jis ka nam file mein nahin tha — is ne doctor rhan ko projikt zindagi banane mein madad ki thi. ek mashin jo logon ko in ki yadon ka samana krane mein madad de — take koi aur is raste par na chale jis par doctor rhan chala tha.

aayan ne file band ki — is ke haath kanap rahe thay, is ka dil bhaari tha, is ki aankhon mein aansoo thay.

woh ab janta tha — woh janta tha ke doctor rhan ki aankhon mein woh udaasi kyun hai jo kabhi khatam nahin hoti. woh janta tha ke is ne yeh program kyun banaya tha. woh janta tha ke jab woh kehta tha "mein bhi kabhi tamhari tarah tha" toh is ka matalab kiya tha.

doctor rhan bhi tot chaka tha. woh bhi is andhire mein tha jahan se wapsi ka rasta nazar nahin aata. magar woh bach gaya tha — aur is ne apani zindagi dosaron ko bachane mein laga di thi.

aayan ne file waps rakhi — aur kamray se bahar nikal gaya. is ka dil halka nahin tha — magar is mein ek chiz thi jo pehle nahin thi: smajah.

woh smajah gaya tha ke doctor rhan kyun atana prasarar tha — kyunke is ka apana zakham abhi tak bhara nahin tha. woh abhi tak apani beti ki maut se nahin nikal ska tha — aur shayad kabhi nahin nikalay ga. magar woh apane zakham ke saath jina sikh gaya tha — aur woh dosaron ko bhi yahi skha raha tha.

aayan ne raat ko apani diary mein likha:

"doctor rhan bhi tota tha — balakl mire jisa. magar woh bach gaya. aur is ne mujhe skhaya ke bachane ka matalab yeh nahin ke zakham bhar jaئe — balke yeh ke tum zakham ke saath jina sikh loo. shayad yahi sab se bara sabaq hai."
