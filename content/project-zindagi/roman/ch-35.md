---

aayan ne aankhein kholin toh kamray mein roshni thi — magar woh roshni pehle jisi nahin thi. woh naram thi, gram thi, jaise suraj is ke liay khas tore par chamak raha ho.

is ne apane haath dekhe — woh kanap nahin rahe thay. is ne apana sina dekha — is ki saans thahari hui thi, praskon, gehri.

doctor rhan is ke plang ke paas baitha tha — is ki aankhein sarakh thin, is ke chehray par thakawat thi, magar is ke labon par muskurahat thi.

"tum chaar ghante be hosh rahe — tamhara damagh bradashat nahin kar ska."

aayan ne ise dekha — is ne khane ki koshash ki, magar is ki aawaz nahin nikal rahi thi.

doctor rhan ne is ka haath thama:

"tum ne kiya jo kisi ne nahin kiya — tum ne jaane diya. tum ne apane bhai ko jaane diya — aur yeh sab se mushkil kaam tha jo koi kar sakta hai."

---

aayan khirki ke paas khara tha — bahar bagh tha, darakht thay, prande thay.

woh duniya pehle jisi hi thi — magar ise lag raha tha jaise is ne yeh sab pehli bar dekha ho. suraj ki roshni pehle se zyada chamakili thi, hawa pehle se zyada khoshagawar thi, aur is ka dil pehle se zyada halka tha.

woh maskraya — ek sachi, khuli muskurahat. pehli bar aath saalon mein.

is ne apane bhai ki tswir n-kali — wahi tswir jo is ne brason pehle chahapa di thi. ab woh ise dekh sakta tha — b-ghair dar ke, b-ghair qasor ke, b-ghair dard ke.

"tum ne mujhe dekha?" is ne tswir se kaha — narami se, jaise asman is ke samne baitha ho. "mein ne tumhein jaane diya. aur mein ab theek hoon."

---

shaam ko sab kaman rom mein jama thay — aakhari bar.

doctor rhan in ke samne khara tha — is ke chehray par woh snajidagi nahin thi jo pehle thi, sirf ataminan tha.

"projikt zindagi makamal hawa."

sab khamosh thay — yeh woh jumla tha jis ka unhein antazar tha, magar ab jab unhon ne sna toh unhein yaqeen nahin aa raha tha.

"tum sab ne apani gehri yadon ka samana kiya — tum sab ne woh kiya jo zyada tr log zindagi bhar nahin kar pate. tum ne apane dar ko dekha, ise phachana — aur ise jaane diya."

is ki nazar har ek par pri:

"magar asli kaam abhi sharoa hawa hai — bahar ki duniya mein. yahan tum ne sikha ke kaise zanda rehna hai — ab tumhein wahan jaa kar dakhana hai ke kaise jina hai."

"mein kal jaa rahi hoon — apani behen se malane. pehli bar pachis saalon mein sach mein is se milein gi."

hamara ne kaha:

"mein ne faisla kiya hai — mein ek koch banon ga. in bachon ka jo sochate hain ke woh kuch nahin kar sakte. unhein dakhaؤan ga ke kiya mumkin hai."

sara boli:

"mein waps hospital jaa rahi hoon — asi hospital mein jahan woh bachchi mari thi. magar is bar — mein doctor ban kar nahin jaa rahi. mein insaan ban kar jaa rahi hoon."

anam ne kaha:

"mein b-laal ko yaad rakhon gi — aur mein zanda rhon gi. yahi woh sab se bara thafa ho ga jo mein ise de sakti hoon."

---

doctor rhan ne aayan ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein sawal tha:

"tum kiya karo ge, aayan?"

aayan ne khirki ke bahar dekha — suraj dob raha tha, magar andhera khofanak nahin tha. woh janta tha ke kal suraj phir nikalay ga — aur woh ise dikhane ke liay mojod ho ga.

"ghar jaa raha hoon," is ne kaha — aur is ki aawaz mein woh yaqeen tha jo sirf is shakhas ko hota hai jis ne apane andhire mein roshni dhond li ho. "ghar jaa raha hoon — aur pehli bar mujhe pata hai ke ghar ka matalab kiya hai."

sab khamosh thay — magar in ki khamoshi mein ek doosray ke liay mohabbat thi, woh mohabbat jo sirf wahi log smajah sakte hain janhon ne ek saath apane dar ka samana kiya ho.

doctor rhan ne sab ki traf dekha — aur woh maskraya:

"projikt zindagi khatam hawa — magar tamhari zindagi abhi sharoa hui hai."
