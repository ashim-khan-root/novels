ek hafta guzar gaya.

is hafate mein aayan ne apana kamra nahin chhora. darwaza andar se band tha, parday khinache huye thay, aur kamray mein sirf wahi roshni thi jo lip tap ki screen se aati thi. is ne khana khana chhor diya tha — nahin, woh khata tha, magar atana kam ke ise khana yaad bhi nahin rehta tha. kabhi ami aatin, darwaza khatakhatatin, aur woh kehta: "ami, mein theek hoon." aur woh chali jatin.

is ne phone band kar diya tha.

pehle din is ne socha: kal dekhon ga. doosray din is ne socha: kiya dekhna hai? teesray din is ne phone ko charging se utaar diya aur almari mein rakh diya. ab koi kal nahin aati thi, koi message nahin aata tha, koi ise dhondane ki koshash nahin kurta tha.

woh balakl akela tha.

aur is ne socha: shayad yahi theek hai. shayad yahi woh jagah hai jahan ise hona chahiye — akile, khamosh, b-ghair kisi ke.

is ne chhat ki daraar ko dekha — woh har roz bari ho rahi thi, ya shayad woh har roz ise zyada ghor se dekh raha tha. is ne socha: jab yeh daraar pori chhat ko par kar le gi, tab kiya ho ga? chhat gr jaئe gi? ya phir woh theek kar lein ge? aur is ne socha: kash woh is ke neeche so raha ho jab woh gre.

woh is khayal par hiran nahin hawa.

woh is khayal par maskraya bhi nahin.

is ne sirf socha aur phir aankhein band kar lein.

khatakhatahat hui.

aayan ne aankhein nahin kholin. woh bistar par pra tha — wahi pozishan, wahi chhat, wahi khamoshi.

khatakhatahat phir hui — zor se.

"sahab!"

malak ki aawaz thi — borhe aadmi, jo mhine ki pehli tareekh ko kraia line aata tha aur baqi dinon mein nazar nahin aata tha.

aayan ne aankhein kholin. woh utha — is ka sar chakra raha tha, is ki tangin kanap rahi thin. ise yaad nahin tha ke is ne aakhari bar kab khaya tha. is ne darwaza khola — atane mein ke malak smajahe ke woh andar hai aur chala na jaئe.

malak ne ise dekha toh is ki aankhon mein hairat thi — shayad aayan ki shakl dekh kar, shayad is ke kamray ki halat dekh kar jahan se badabo aa rahi thi.

"sahab, aap ke liay lafafa hai. kisi larki ne diya."

aayan ne lafafa liya. is ka nam is par likha tha — sirf atana, koi pata nahin, koi bhijane wala nahin.

"kaun si larki?"

"pata nahin sahab. mein ne neeche dekha toh woh jaa rahi thi. boli: 'yeh aayan khan ko de dein.' aur chali gai."

aayan ne lafafa liya aur darwaza band kar diya.

woh waps bistar par aaya. lafafa is ke haath mein tha — krim rang ka kaghaz, mota, mehnga. is ne ise plat kar dekha — koi nishaan nahin, koi logo nahin, sirf is ka nam.

is ne lafafa khola — bedali se, jaise koi purana bl kholata hai.

andar kaghaz tha — ek hi safha, asi krim rang ka, aur oopar ek nishaan tha: ek darakht jis ki jarin zameen ke neeche gehri thin aur shakhin aasman ki traf phili hui thin.

is ke neeche likha tha:

"drakhawastin band hain.
phir bhi...
aap mantakhab ho gaye hain."

aur neeche ek nambr tha.

woh is raat nahin swaya.

magar pehli bar kaafi dinon mein, woh chhat ki daraar nahin dekh raha tha. woh khat dekh raha tha — woh khat jis ne is ki zindagi ko is mor par la khara kiya tha jahan se wapsi nahin thi. woh abhi nahin janta tha ke yeh mor ise b-chai ga ya tabaa.
