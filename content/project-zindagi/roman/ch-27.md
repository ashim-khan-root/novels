---

sara ke hadase ke baad sab khamosh thay — magar hamara ne is khamoshi ko tora.

woh kursi ki traf brha — is ke chehray par wahi muskurahat thi jo dhaal thi, magar ab woh muskurahat pehle se makhatalaf thi — naram, sachi, thaki hui.

"phir se wahi dor?" is ne kaha — is ki aawaz mein mazaq tha, magar woh mazaq bhi pehle jisa nahin tha. "kuch naya kar dete."

doctor rhan ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein woh chiz thi jo smajah thi. "aaj tum apani zindagi ke bhatrin din mein jao ge."

hamara ki muskurahat thahar gai — is ne doctor rhan ki traf dekha, jaise woh yaqeen nahin kar raha ho. "bhatrin din?"

"woh din — jab tum sab se zyada khush thay."

hamara khamosh raha — is ki aankhon mein kuch badala, jaise woh kisi bahut purani chiz ko dhond raha ho jisay is ne kho diya tha. phir woh kursi par bith gaya — aur is ne sar halaya.

mashin sharoa hui — aur hamara ki aankhein band ho gaئin.

---

woh ek astidim tha — magar is bar koi dor nahin thi.

yeh shaam ka waqt tha — suraj dob raha tha, aasman narangi aur glabi tha, aur pori faza mein kisi jashan ki khushbu thi.

hamara apani tim ke saath tha — sab is ke grad thay, sab hans rahe thay, sab ise mbarakabad de rahe thay. kal qomi maqable thay — aur hamara faئanal mein phanach chaka tha.

is ka koch is ke paas aaya — ek boorha aadmi, chehray par jaharayan, magar aankhon mein woh chamak jo kabhi nahin bajahi thi. is ne hamara ke kandhay par haath rakha aur kaha:

"tum awalampak jao ge, beta."

hamara ka dil khushi se bhar gaya — is ne koch ko gle laga liya, aur is ki aankhon mein aansoo thay — khushi ke aansoo.

phir is ne apani maan ko dekha — woh bhir mein khari thin, in ka chehra aanson se gila tha, magar woh maskra rahi thin. unhon ne ise dekha aur haath halaya — aur hamara ne in ki traf dekh kar woh sab kha diya jo is ne kabhi nahin kaha tha: mein tum se pyar kurta hoon, amaan.

aur phir is ka baap — woh shakhas jis ne kabhi apane jazbaat nahin dakhaئe thay — is ke paas aaya aur is ke kandhay par haath rakha. is ne kuch nahin kaha — magar is ke haath ka wazn, is ki aankhon mein woh chamak — yeh sab kuch tha.

hamara ne is din mhasos kiya tha ke woh duniya ka sab se khush qismat insaan hai.

---

mashin band hui toh hamara ki aankhein khuli thin — magar woh ro raha tha.

woh kursi par baitha raha — is ke aansoo bha rahe thay, magar woh maskra bhi raha tha. yeh aansoo gham ke nahin thay — yeh shakar ke thay, is ehsaas ke thay ke woh kabhi khush tha, sach mein, pori tarah khush.

"mein bhol gaya tha," is ne kaha — is ki aawaz kanap rahi thi, magar is mein garmi thi. "mein bhol gaya tha ke mein kabhi khush tha."

is ne doctor rhan ki traf dekha — aur is ki aankhon mein shakar tha. "shukriya — mujhe woh din yaad dalane ke liay."

woh utha — is ke qadam pehle se halake thay, is ka chehra praskon tha. is ne kamray se bahar nklate huye sab ki traf dekha aur kaha:

"yar, tum log bhi apane achay din yaad rakho — yeh sirf bure din nahin hote jo humein banate hain."

---

aayan ne hamara ko dekha — aur is ne smajah liya ke shafa kabhi kabhi gham ki shakl mein aati hai.

hamara ro raha tha — magar woh pehle se zyada mazabot tha. woh apane zakham ko qabol kar chaka tha — aur ab woh apani khushi ko bhi qabol kar raha tha.

shayad yahi shafa thi — na bholana, na dabana, balke har chiz ko qabol karna — acha bhi, bura bhi — aur is ke saath jina sikh lena.

is raat jab aayan so raha tha toh is ne khwab mein apane bhai ko dekha — woh hans raha tha, is ke haath mein ek karad tha, aur woh kha raha tha: "bhai, mein tum se pyar kurta hoon."

aayan ne khwab mein maskra kar kaha: "mein bhi tajah se pyar kurta hoon, b-la l."

aur woh pehli bar brason mein, woh apane bhai ko yaad kar ke roya nahin — woh sirf maskraya.
