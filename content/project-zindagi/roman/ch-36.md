weight: 36

---

shaam thi jab aayan ne doctor rhan ke kamray ka darwaza khatakhataya.

"andar ao," doctor rhan ki aawaz aai — thaki hui, magar naram.

aayan ne darwaza khola toh doctor rhan meez par baitha purani tswirin dekh raha tha — is ke chehray par udaasi thi, magar woh udaasi talakh nahin thi — woh aman ke qareeb thi.

"mujhe aap se kuch poochna hai," aayan ne kaha — aur woh bith gaya. "sabajikt ziro ke bare mein."

doctor rhan ne ise dekha — is ki aankhon mein woh dard tha jo kabhi khatam nahin hota, magar is ne sar halaya.

"tum tayar ho — toh mein tumhein bata sakta hoon."

---

doctor rhan ne gehri saans li — aur is ne kehna sharoa kiya:

"sabajikt ziro — woh miri beti thi."

aayan khamosh raha — woh janta tha ke ab sirf snana hai.

"is ka nam zara tha — woh sirf atharah saal ki thi jab woh hadasa hawa. is ki maan ka antaqal is ke bachapan mein hi ho gaya tha — aur hum ek doosray ke share thay, woh mein aur mein woh."

is ki aawaz kanapi — magar woh raka nahin:

"hadase ke baad woh koma mein chali gai — teen maa. doctoron ne kaha ke woh kabhi nahin jaagay gi. magar mein ne haar nahin mani — mein ne mashin banai, yahi mashin, is liay ke mein ise waps la skon."

is ne meez par rakhi tswir uthai — ek jawan larki, maskrati hui, aankhon mein chamak.

"magar mein na-kaam raha — mashin ne is ki yaadein toh lota dein, magar woh in yadon mein kho gai. woh mazi mein rehna chahti thi — is din se pehle jab hadasa hawa — kyunke wahan is ki maan zanda thi."

is ki aankhein nam thin:

"is ne mashin mein rhane ka intikhab kiya — aur mein ise rok nahin ska. kyunke woh jagah — woh majah se zyada haqiqi thi is ke liay."

---

"toh aap ne —" aayan ne kehna chaaha, magar is ka gla bhar aaya.

"mein ne wahi kiya jo koi baap nahin kar sakta — mein ne ise jaane diya. mein ne mashin band ki, is ki yaadein mat gaئin — aur woh chali gai."

woh khamosh ho gaya — ek lamha, do lamhe. phir is ne kaha:

"magar is se pehle — is ne mujhe bataya ke woh khush hai. is ne kaha ke wahan is ki maan hai, aur woh theek hai. aur mein ne is par yaqeen kiya — kyunke agar mein yaqeen nahin kurta toh mein paagal ho jata."

aayan ne doctor rhan ka haath thama — aur is ne kaha:

"aap ne ise jaane diya — yeh sab se bari mohabbat hai jo koi baap apani beti ko de sakta hai."

---

doctor rhan ne aansoo ponachahe — aur woh maskraya:

"tum theek kehte ho — magar mujhe yeh smajahane mein brason lag gaye. mein ne yeh projikt sharoa kiya — pehle apani beti ko bachane ke liay. magar phir mein ne dekha ke yeh aur logon ki madad kar sakta hai — tum jaise logon ki."

is ne aayan ki traf dekha — aur is ki aankhon mein tshakr tha:

"tum ne woh kiya jo zara nahin kar saki — tum ne waps aanay ka intikhab kiya. tum ne apani yaad ka samana kiya, ise jaane diya — aur tum zanda rahe."

aayan khamosh tha — ise laga jaise woh doctor rhan ki beti ka qaraz nahin aُtar sakta, magar kam az kam woh yeh dakha sakta tha ke mashin sirf maut nahin la sakti — woh zindagi bhi la sakti hai, agar insaan waps aanay ka intikhab kare.

---

jab aayan kamray se nkla toh raat gehri thi — magar is ka dil halka tha.

woh braamade mein khara hawa — aur is ne staron ko dekha. is ne socha: shayad zara bhi inhin staron ko dekh rahi hai — jahan bhi woh hai. aur shayad woh maskra rahi hai.

kyunke is ke walad ne jo mashin is ke liay banai thi — woh ab kisi aur ki zindagi bacha chaki thi.

aur kabhi kabhi — yahi kaafi hota hai.
