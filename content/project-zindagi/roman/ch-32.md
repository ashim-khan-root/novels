title: "b-ab 32 — sara ka aakhari sishan"
weight: 32
type: roman

---

sab ne apane aakhari sishan makamal kar liay thay — siwaye sara ke.

woh apane kamray mein baithi thi — darwaza band, parday khinache huye, roshni band. is ne khod ko chaar dayawari mein qaid kar liya tha — jaise woh mashin se nahin, balke khod se bhag rahi thi.

aayan ne darwaza khatakhataya — pehle aahasta, phir zor se.

"sara — darwaza kholo."

khamoshi.

"mein janta hoon tum andar ho. mein janta hoon tum drati ho. magar —"

darwaza khula.

sara darwazay mein khari thi — is ki aankhein sarakh thin, is ke baal bakhare huye thay, aur woh pehli bar atani kamzor lag rahi thi jatani woh andar se thi.

"mein nahin jaa sakti," is ne kaha — is ki aawaz kanap rahi thi. "woh mujhe phir tore de gi. woh yaad — mein ise dobara nahin ji sakti."

aayan ne is ki traf dekha — aur is ne woh kaha jo woh janta tha ke sach hai:

"tum pehle hi tot chaki ho, sara."

sara ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein ghussa tha, magar woh ghussa jhoota tha.

"tum pehle hi tot chaki ho — is din jab woh bachchi mari thi. aur tum tab se apane takron ko aktha kar rahi ho, is dar se ke agar kisi ne dekh liya toh woh jaan jaein ge ke tum mazabot nahin ho. magar sara —"

is ne aahasta se kaha:

"ab sirf athana baqi hai."

---

sara kursi par baithi — is ka chehra safed tha, is ke haath kanap rahe thay, magar woh bith gai thi.

doctor rhan ne is ki traf dekha — is ki aankhon mein fikr thi, magar is ne kuch nahin kaha. is ne sirf helmet is ke sar par rakha — aur mashin sharoa ho gai.

wahi hospital. wahi rahadari. wahi kamra. wahi bachchi.

magar is bar — sara ne kuch makhatalaf kiya.

is bar jab woh bachchi ke paas gai toh is ke haath nahin rake. is ne unhein aage brhaya — kanapte huye, magar aage brhaya. is ne woh sab kuch kiya jo ise karna chahiye tha — har qadam, har aqadam, har dua.

magar bachchi phir bhi mar gai.

maut koi chiz nahin badal sakti — na mashin, na doctor, na koi. kuch chizin hoti hain, aur unhein badala nahin jaa sakta.

magar sara ne woh kiya jo woh pehle nahin kar saki thi — woh raki nahin. woh dri, magar raki nahin. woh kanapi, magar raki nahin.

---

mashin band hui toh sara ki aankhon mein aansoo nahin thay.

woh khamosh baithi rahi — is ne apane haath dekhe, wahi haath jo is din nahin hale thay. ab woh kanap rahe thay — magar woh hal rahe thay.

woh boli — is ki aawaz mein woh skhati nahin thi jo pehle thi, sirf sach tha:

"mein is din ko badal nahin saki."

woh raki — ek lamha, do lamhe — aur phir woh maskraئi:

"magar mein khod ko badal saki."

woh uth kar khari ho gai — is ki tangin kanap rahi thin, magar woh khari thi. is ne sab ki traf dekha — aur is ki aankhon mein woh chiz thi jo kabhi nahin thi: sukoon.

"mein ne socha tha ke mein kamzor hoon — kyunke mein ne is din kuch nahin kiya. magar mein kamzor nahin thi."

is ne apane haath dekhe — woh ab kanap nahin rahe thay.

"mein sirf atani mazabot nahin thi jatana mein ne socha tha. magar ab hoon."

---

aayan ne sara ko dekha — aur ise laga jaise woh apane aap ko dekh raha ho. woh bhi is din se drata tha — jab woh apane bhai ki maut ko dobara jaye ga. magar sara ne ise skha diya tha ke drane ka matalab yeh nahin ke tum kamzor ho — drane ka matalab sirf yeh hai ke tum insaan ho.

aur insaanon ki sab se bari taqat — yeh hai ke woh drate huye bhi aage brh sakte hain.

is raat aayan ne apani diary mein likha:

"sara ne mujhe skhaya ke himmat ka matalab dar na hona nahin — himmat ka matalab dar ke bawajod aage brhana hai. aur kal — mein aage brhon ga."
