---

anam woh larki thi jo hamesha roti thi.

pehle din se jab woh aai thi, is ke aansoo kabhi nahin rake thay. woh khamoshi se roti thi — b-ghair kisi aawaz ke, b-ghair kisi shakait ke. is ke aansoo is ke galon par bahutay rehte thay, aur woh unhein ponachahati bhi nahin thi.

sab ise kamzor samajhte thay.

maئara ise tsli dine ki koshash karti — magar woh aur ro prati. hamara ise hanasane ki koshash kurta — woh munh phir leti. sara ise nazar andaz karti — aur woh aur zyada roti.

aayan ne bhi ise dekha tha — aur is ne socha tha ke yeh larki tot chaki hai. shayad woh sab se kamzor hai. shayad woh program khatam nahin kar paئe gi.

magar aaj is ki bari thi.

woh kursi par baithi toh is ke aansoo pehle se zyada tez ho gaye. doctor rhan ne helmet is ke sar par rakha — aur is ki aankhon mein khauf tha, magar azm bhi tha.

"tum tayar ho," doctor rhan ne kaha — aur woh alafat is ne nahin pooche, woh ise bata raha tha.

anam ne aankhein band kar lein.

---

woh ek shadi ka ghar tha — roshanayan, phool, khoshayan.

anam dalhan thi — is ne safed labas phana hawa tha, is ke haathon mhandi lagi hui thi, is ke chehray par woh muskurahat thi jo har dalhan ke chehray par hoti hai — khushi, umeed, ek nayi zindagi ka aaghaz.

woh aaئine ke samne khari thi — apane aap ko dekh rahi thi, maskra rahi thi, soch rahi thi ke is ki zindagi ab badalane wali hai. is ne apani maan ko dekha jo darwazay par khari thin — in ki aankhon mein khushi thi, aur shayad thodi si udaasi bhi.

"beta, Allah tumhein khush rakhe," in ki maan ne kaha.

anam ne unhein gle lagaya — aur woh donon ro dein. magar woh aansoo khushi ke thay.

phir is ka shohar aaya.

woh kamray mein dakhal hawa — maskra raha tha, is ke haath mein phool thay. is ne anam ko dekha aur kaha: "tum bahut khubsurat lag rahi ho."

anam sharama gai — is ne aankhein jahaka lein, is ka dil khushi se dharak raha tha. is ne socha: yeh woh shakhas hai jis ke saath mein apani baqi zindagi gazaron gi.

magar woh ghalat thi.

---

pehli raat sab kuch naramal tha — magar doosray din kuch badala hawa tha.

is ke shohar ki aawaz mein woh narami nahin thi jo kal thi. woh chala raha tha — kisi chiz par, ise yaad nahin ks chiz par. aur phir is ka haath utha — aur woh anam ke chehray se takraya.

woh hiran ra gai.

woh khari rahi — is ka gal jal raha tha, is ki aankhon mein yaqeen nahin aa raha tha. is ne socha: shayad yeh ghalati thi. shayad woh ghase mein tha. shayad kal sab theek ho jaئe ga.

magar kal nahin aaya.

kal ki jagah ek aur din aaya — aur phir ek aur, aur ek aur. har din wahi hota tha — wahi aawaz, wahi haath, wahi dard — magar anam ne chhora nahin. woh raki — apani maan ki khatr, apane ghar ki khatr, is dar ki khatr ke log kiya kahein ge.

is ne darwazay band kar liay — apane ghar ke, apane dil ke, apani baaton ke. is ne phone athaya aur neeche rakh diya — ks ko bataati? kaun sne ga? aur agar kisi ne sna bhi toh kiya badalay ga?

woh ek din uthi — aur is ne apana big pik kiya. is ne apane shohar ko dekha — jo so raha tha, be-khabar — aur woh bahar nikal gai. is ne peeche mar kar nahin dekha.

woh apani maan ke ghar aai — aur darwaza khola toh is ki maan ne ise dekha aur kuch nahin poocha. woh sirf rawaئin — aur anam bhi roi.

aur woh tab se ro rahi thi.

---

mashin band hui toh anam ne aankhein kholin.

magar woh ro nahin rahi thi.

pehli bar, jab se woh is program mein aai thi, is ki aankhein khashak thin. is ne helmet utaara aur uth kar khari ho gai — is ki aankhon mein woh chiz thi jo pehle nahin thi: taqat.

is ne sab ki traf dekha — ek ek kar ke. aur phir woh boli:

"tum sab mujhe kamzor samajhte thay."

kamray mein khamoshi thi.

"mein janta hoon," is ne jari rakha. "tum ne socha ke mein sab se kamzor hoon. mein roti hoon, mein baatein nahin karti, mein chahap jati hoon. magar —"

is ne aankhein utha kar sab ko dekha.

"magar mein wahan se nkli. zanda."

woh raki — is ki aawaz mein kapakapi thi magar woh mazabot thi.

"tum mein se ktane yeh kha sakte ho?"

kisi ne jawab nahin diya.

anam ne aahasta se kaha: "mein nahin jaanti ke yeh program humein theek kare ga ya nahin. magar ek baat mein jaanti hoon — mein ne apani zindagi waps le li hai. aur tum mein se har ek — tum bhi le sakte ho."

woh apani kursi par bith gai — is ki aankhein khashak thin, is ka chehra praskon tha, aur woh pehli bar is tarah baithi thi jaise woh waqai mojod ho — na ro rahi ho, na chahap rahi ho — sirf mojod ho.

aayan ne ise dekha — aur is ne socha ke woh is bare mein ktana ghalat tha. is ne socha tha ke taqat woh hai jo sara mein hai — kantrol, khamoshi, behasi. magar ab ise pata chala — taqat yeh hai ke tum sab se bura dikhane ke baad bhi uth kar khare ho jao.

anam ne yeh kiya tha.

aur woh sab ise dekh rahe thay — khamosh, matasar, badalay huye.

is raat, jab aayan apane kamray mein waps aaya toh is ne apani diary mein likha:

"anam ne mujhe skhaya ke kamazori ka matalab taqat nahin hai. aur taqat ka matalab yeh nahin ke tum rawaؤ nahin — balke yeh ke tum ro kar bhi uth khare ho."
