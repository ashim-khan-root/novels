---
title: "b-ab 43 — naya safr"
weight: 43
type: roman
---


---

subah thi jab aayan apane daftar mein dakhal hawa — ek chhota sa kamra, ek meez, do krasayan, aur deewar par lagi ek pintang.

yeh is ka pehla din tha — b-tore konaslar.

woh apani kursi par baitha — is ke haath kanap rahe thay, magar is ne gehri saans li aur khod ko snabhala. woh janta tha ke yeh aasaan nahin ho ga — magar woh janta tha ke yahi woh rasta tha jis par ise chalna tha.

is ka pehla klaئant solah saal ka ek larka tha — is ka nam zid tha.

zid khamosh tha, is ki aankhon mein ghussa tha, aur woh kursi par is tarah baitha tha jaise woh kisi bhi waqt bhagane ke liay tayar ho.

aayan ne ise dekha — aur ise apana purana khod yaad aa gaya. wahi khamoshi, wahi ghussa, wahi be aatamadi.

is ne kuch nahin kaha — woh sirf baitha raha, zid ke saath, khamoshi se.

---

marate gaye — paanch minute, das minute, pandrah minute.

zid ne apani ghari dekhi — phir darwazay ki traf, phir aayan ki traf. is ki aankhon mein sawal tha — "tum kuch kyun nahin poochte?"

magar aayan ne kuch nahin poocha. woh sirf baitha raha — jaise woh kha raha ho: "mein tamhare saath hoon, chahe tum kuch kaho ya na kaho."

aakhar mein — jab sishan khatam hone wala tha — zid ne kaha:

"tum pehle shakhas ho jo majah se kuch pooche b-ghair mire saath baitha."

aayan ne ise dekha — aur woh maskraya:

"kyunke mein janta hoon — kabhi kabhi sab se bari madad sirf kisi ka saath hoti hai."

---

aayan ghar waps aaya toh is ke walad ne darwazay par is ka astaqabal kiya — in ke haath mein chai ka cup tha, aur in ke chehray par muskurahat thi:

"kisa raha pehla din?"

aayan ne chai ka cup liya — aur woh apane walad ke paas bith gaya:

"mushkil tha — magar mein ne wahi kiya jo mujhe projikt mein skhaya gaya tha. mein ne ise snane ki koshash nahin ki — mein ne is ke saath rhane ki koshash ki."

is ke walad ne is ki traf dekha — aur in ki aankhon mein fakhar tha:

"tum ne bahut kuch sikh liya hai, beta."

---

raat ko aayan ne apani diary mein likha:

"aaj mein ne apana pehla qadam rakha — is raste par jo mujhe projikt zindagi ne dakhaya tha. yeh aasaan nahin tha — magar mein ne woh kiya jo mein ne sikha tha: mein ne khamosh rehna sikha, mein ne snana sikha, aur mein ne wahan rehna sikha — jahan koi aur nahin rehna chahta.

zid ne mujhe aaj wahi skhaya jo mein ne kabhi kisi kitab mein nahin parha — ke kabhi kabhi sab se bara thafa jo tum kisi ko de sakte ho woh tamhara waqt hai, tamhari khamoshi, tamhari mojodagi.

mein tayar hoon — is safr ke liay.

kyunke agar mein apani zindagi badal sakta hoon — toh shayad mein kisi aur ki bhi badal sakta hoon."

is ne diary band ki — aur woh so gaya.

praskon, khush, aur pehli bar — makamal.
