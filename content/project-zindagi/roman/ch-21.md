agle din jab aayan sishan rom mein dakhal hawa toh doctor rhan kursi ke paas khara tha — magar is bar mashin ke ilawa kuch aur bhi tha.

"aaj koi yaad nahin," doctor rhan ne kaha. "aaj sirf aaina hai."

phir is ki apani aawaz — woh aawaz jo is ke zhan mein har roz gonajati thi, jisay woh kabhi khamosh nahin kar ska tha: "tum kuch nahin ho. tum hamesha na-kaam ho ge. tamhari waja se sab kuch tota. agar tum na hote toh sab theek hota. tum bojah ho. tum kisi ke kaam nahin."

"mein kuch nahin hoon," is ne saragoshi ki — aur woh saragoshi is ke zhan mein gonaj kar ek cheekh ban gai.

"mein kuch nahin hoon!"

shayad woh woh aawaz thi jisay is ne is din sna tha jab maئara ne kaha tha: "tamhari hansi bahut achi hai." ya shayad woh anam ki aawaz thi jab is ne kaha: "mein wahan se nkli, zanda." ya shayad woh hamara ki thi jab is ne kaha: "tum bhi bach sakte ho."

is ne aaئine mein dekha — is bagre huye chehray ko, is beqimat wajod ko — aur woh chikha:

"bas karo!"

"mein woh nahin hoon jo tum kehte ho! mein woh nahin hoon jo baap ne kaha! mein woh nahin hoon jo mein ne khod socha!"

aaina tot gaya — is ke aks ke saath, is ki bari yadon ke saath, is ke andar ki is aawaz ke saath jo ise brason se kha rahi thi ke woh kuch nahin hai.

shishe ke takre hawa mein bakhar gaye — aur phir sab khamosh ho gaya.

---

aayan ki aankhein khuli thin — woh kanap raha tha, is ka chehra psine se sharabor tha, is ka dil is ke sine se bahar nklane ko tha.

magar woh maskra raha tha.

doctor rhan is ke paas khara tha — is ne aayan ki traf dekha aur is ki aankhon mein woh chiz thi jo kabhi nahin thi: fakhar.

"tum ne pehla qadam utha liya. tum ne is aawaz ko chilanaj kiya."

aayan ne gehri saans li — is ka jism abhi bhi kanap raha tha, magar is ke andar kuch badal gaya tha. woh aawaz jo brason se is ke zhan mein thi — woh ab atani zor se nahin bol rahi thi. is ne ise khamosh nahin kiya tha, magar is ne ise jawab de diya tha.

aur woh janta tha: yeh jang abhi khatam nahin hui. yeh toh sirf sharoa hui thi.

magar pehli bar, ise laga ke woh is jang ko jeet sakta hai.
